## 1. Cấu trúc chung

| Phần | Thân mật | Trang trọng |
|---|---|---|
| **Lời chào** | *Hi Lan, / Dear Lan,* | *Dear Mr Tran, / Dear Sir or Madam,* |
| **Câu mở đầu** | *Thanks for your email! / How are you?* | *I am writing to enquire about / complain about / apply for...* |
| **Thân bài** | Đoạn ngắn, mỗi đoạn một ý của đề | Đoạn rõ ràng, mỗi đoạn một ý |
| **Câu kết** | *Can't wait to see you! / Write soon!* | *I look forward to hearing from you.* |
| **Ký tên** | *Love, / Best wishes, / Cheers,* | *Yours sincerely,* (biết tên) / *Yours faithfully,* (không biết tên) / *Kind regards,* |

## 2. Quy tắc vàng: trả lời **đủ mọi ý** của đề

Đề thường có 3 gạch đầu dòng — **mỗi gạch đầu dòng = ít nhất một đoạn/vài câu**. Thiếu một ý = mất điểm "hoàn thành nhiệm vụ" dù ngôn ngữ tốt.

**Kỹ thuật:** đánh số các ý của đề, viết xong đánh dấu ✓ từng ý.

## 3. Các loại email thường gặp

| Loại | Cụm từ hữu ích |
|---|---|
| **Hỏi thông tin** (trang trọng) | *I am writing to enquire about... / Could you please tell me...? / I would also like to know whether...* |
| **Phàn nàn** | *I am writing to complain about... / Unfortunately, ... / I would be grateful if you could... / I expect a full refund.* |
| **Xin lỗi** | *I'm so sorry for... / I apologise for any inconvenience caused.* |
| **Mời** (thân mật) | *Would you like to...? / How about...? / Let me know if you can come.* |
| **Cảm ơn** | *Thank you so much for... / I really appreciate...* |
| **Xin việc** | *I am writing to apply for the position of... / I have experience in... / Please find attached my CV.* |
| **Đề xuất / khuyên** | *Why don't you...? / I'd recommend... / If I were you, I'd...* |

## 4. Email mẫu

### Thân mật — trả lời bạn hỏi về chuyến thăm

> **Đề:** *Your English friend Tom is visiting Vietnam next month and asks for advice. Write an email: suggest places to visit, tell him what to bring, and offer to meet.*

> *Hi Tom,*
>
> *Great to hear you're coming to Vietnam! I'm sure you'll love it.*
>
> *If you're interested in history, you should definitely visit Hue and Hoi An. Hue has an amazing old citadel, and Hoi An is famous for its lantern-lit streets. For beaches, I'd recommend Da Nang — it's only 30 minutes from Hoi An.*
>
> *Next month is the rainy season in central Vietnam, so don't forget to bring a raincoat and some comfortable shoes. Sunscreen is a good idea too, because it can still be hot between showers.*
>
> *I'll be in Da Nang during your trip, so let's meet up! I could show you my favourite seafood restaurant. Just let me know your dates.*
>
> *See you soon,*
> *Minh*

> ℹ️ Mùa mưa ở miền Trung thường vào khoảng tháng 9–12; kiểm tra thời tiết thực tế trước khi đi.

### Trang trọng — hỏi thông tin khóa học

> *Dear Sir or Madam,*
>
> *I am writing to enquire about the IELTS preparation course advertised on your website.*
>
> *Firstly, could you please tell me how long the course lasts and how many hours of classes there are per week? I work full-time, so I would prefer evening or weekend classes.*
>
> *I would also like to know whether there is a placement test before the course starts, and what the maximum class size is.*
>
> *Finally, could you let me know the total fee and whether payment can be made in instalments?*
>
> *I look forward to hearing from you.*
>
> *Yours faithfully,*
> *Nguyen Thi Hoa*

## 5. Lỗi thường gặp

| Lỗi | Sửa |
|---|---|
| *Dear Sir or Madam ... Yours sincerely* | *... Yours faithfully* |
| *Dear Mr Nam* (dùng tên) | *Dear Mr Nguyen* (họ) |
| Trộn văn phong (*Hi Sir, ... Cheers!*) | Giữ một văn phong |
| Thiếu một ý của đề | Đánh số và kiểm tra |
| Rút gọn, viết tắt trong thư trang trọng (*I'm, ASAP, pls*) | Viết đầy đủ |
| Mở đầu dài dòng hỏi thăm trong thư trang trọng | Vào thẳng mục đích |

## 6. Bài luyện

**1. (Thân mật, 100–120 từ)** *Your friend has invited you to her birthday party, but you can't go. Write an email: apologise and explain why, suggest another time to meet, and say what present you've bought her.*

**2. (Trang trọng, 120–150 từ)** *You bought a pair of headphones online, but they stopped working after one week. Write to the company: describe the problem, say what you have already done, and say what you want them to do.*

**3. (Trang trọng, 120–150 từ)** *You saw an advertisement for a part-time job as a tour guide assistant. Write an email: say why you are interested, describe your relevant skills and experience, and ask about working hours.*
