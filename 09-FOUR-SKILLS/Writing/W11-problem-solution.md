# W11 · Problem–Solution Essays — Bài luận vấn đề – giải pháp

| Mục | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| **Cấp độ** | B1 → B2 |
| **Mục tiêu** | Phân tích nguyên nhân/vấn đề và đề xuất giải pháp cụ thể, khả thi |
| **Ngữ pháp liên quan** | [B2-G019 Purpose, result, reason](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G019-purpose-result-reason.md), [B2-G015 Subjunctive](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G015-subjunctive.md) |
| **Đáp án** | [Writing Chapters — Answers](../../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/SKILLS/WRITING-CHAPTERS-ANSWERS.md#w11) |

## 1. Các biến thể đề

| Đề | Hai phần cần trả lời |
|---|---|
| *What problems does X cause? What solutions can you suggest?* | Vấn đề + giải pháp |
| *What are the causes of X? How can it be solved?* | Nguyên nhân + giải pháp |
| *Why is X happening? What can be done?* | Nguyên nhân + giải pháp |

## 2. Cấu trúc

| Đoạn | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| Mở bài | Paraphrase + nêu sẽ bàn nguyên nhân/vấn đề và giải pháp |
| TB1 | 2 nguyên nhân/vấn đề + giải thích + ví dụ/hậu quả |
| TB2 | 2 giải pháp — **mỗi giải pháp nên tương ứng một vấn đề** ở TB1 |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt + nhấn mạnh tầm quan trọng của hành động |

> Giải pháp tốt là giải pháp **cụ thể** (ai làm? làm gì? vì sao hiệu quả?): ❌ *The government should solve this problem.* → ✅ *The government could introduce a deposit scheme for plastic bottles, so that people are paid to return them.*

## 3. Ngôn ngữ

| Chức năng | Cụm |
|---|---|
| Nguyên nhân | *One of the main causes is... / This problem stems from... / X is largely due to...* |
| Vấn đề / hậu quả | *This leads to... / As a result, ... / One serious consequence is...* |
| Giải pháp | *One effective solution would be to... / The government could... / It is essential that...* |
| Giải thích hiệu quả | *This would... / By doing this, ... / so that...* |
| Đề xuất trang trọng | *It is recommended that the government (should) introduce...* |

## 4. Bài mẫu (~260 từ)

**Đề:** *In many cities, the amount of plastic waste is increasing. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?*

> *Plastic waste has become one of the most visible environmental problems in modern cities. This essay will examine the main causes of this trend and suggest some practical solutions.*
>
> *The first cause is the convenience of single-use plastic. Many products, from drinks to takeaway meals, are sold in plastic packaging because it is cheap and light. Busy city residents rarely think about what happens to it after use. A second cause is the lack of an effective recycling system. In many places, waste is not separated at home, so plastic that could be recycled ends up in landfill or rivers.*
>
> *Several measures could help tackle this problem. Firstly, governments could introduce a small charge for plastic bags and a deposit scheme for bottles. Such policies give consumers a financial reason to reduce plastic use or return bottles for recycling. Secondly, local authorities should provide separate bins for recyclable waste and run campaigns to teach residents how to use them. If recycling were made simple and convenient, far more plastic would be reused instead of polluting the environment.*
>
> *In conclusion, plastic waste is increasing mainly because of the convenience of single-use products and poor recycling systems. Financial incentives and better waste collection could significantly reduce the problem, provided that governments, businesses and individuals all play their part.*

## 5. Bài luyện

**1.** Lập dàn ý: *"Many young people are spending too much time on social media. What problems does this cause, and what can be done?"*
**2.** Viết bài luận 250–280 từ: *"Obesity among children is increasing in many countries. What are the causes, and what solutions can you suggest?"*
