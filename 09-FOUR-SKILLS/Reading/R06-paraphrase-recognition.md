## 1. Nguyên tắc

Câu hỏi đọc hiểu B1–B2 **hiếm khi lặp lại nguyên văn** câu trong bài. Bạn phải nhận ra **cùng một ý** được viết bằng **từ/cấu trúc khác**.

| Bài đọc | Câu hỏi |
|---|---|
| *The museum is closed on Mondays.* | *Visitors **cannot** go to the museum **at the start of the week**.* |
| *Prices rose by 50%.* | *Prices went up **by half**.* |
| *Few people attended.* | ***Not many** people came.* |

## 2. Kỹ thuật paraphrase thường gặp

| Kỹ thuật | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Từ đồng nghĩa | *assist = help; purchase = buy; reside = live* |
| Đổi từ loại | *They **decided** to... → Their **decision** to...* |
| Chủ động ↔ bị động | *Scientists discovered X → X was discovered* |
| Khẳng định ↔ phủ định | *It's rare → It doesn't happen often* |
| Khái quát hóa | *cars, buses, motorbikes → vehicles* |
| Đổi cấu trúc câu | *Although it was expensive, ... → Despite the high price, ...* |

## 3. True / False / Not Given

| Đáp án | Khi nào |
|---|---|
| **True** | Bài **khẳng định** cùng ý (dù diễn đạt khác) |
| **False** | Bài **khẳng định điều ngược lại** |
| **Not Given** | Bài **không có đủ thông tin** để khẳng định hay phủ định |

### Phân biệt False và Not Given — quan trọng nhất

> **Bài:** *The hotel has 50 rooms, most of which have a sea view.*
> - *All rooms have a sea view.* → **False** (bài nói *most*, mâu thuẫn với *all*)
> - *The hotel has a swimming pool.* → **Not Given** (bài không nhắc)
> - *More than half of the rooms have a sea view.* → **True** (*most* = hơn một nửa)

> **Bẫy:** Đừng dùng kiến thức bên ngoài. Dù bạn **biết** điều gì đó đúng ngoài đời, nếu bài không nói → **Not Given**.

### Từ "chốt" cần chú ý

*all / some / most / none · always / often / never · only · first / last · more / less · may / will · not*

## 4. Bài luyện

### Task 1 — Ghép câu cùng nghĩa

| Bài đọc | Paraphrase |
|---|---|
| **1.** *The project was postponed due to a lack of funding.* | a. Hardly anyone came to the event. |
| **2.** *Attendance was extremely low.* | b. Because there wasn't enough money, the project was delayed. |
| **3.** *She is fluent in three languages.* | c. The company employs fewer staff than before. |
| **4.** *The company has reduced its workforce.* | d. She can speak three languages very well. |

### Task 2 — True / False / Not Given

> **Text:** *The city's first metro line opened in late 2024 after more than a decade of construction. It runs for about 20 kilometres and has 14 stations, most of which are elevated. On the opening weekend, rides were free, and the trains were extremely crowded. The city government plans to build several more lines, although no exact dates have been confirmed.*

> ℹ️ Đoạn văn là văn bản luyện tập; các số liệu dùng cho bài tập, không thay thế nguồn chính thức.

**5.** The metro line took less than ten years to build.
**6.** All the stations are underground.
**7.** Passengers did not have to pay during the first weekend.
**8.** The metro is the fastest way to travel in the city.
**9.** The government has announced the opening date of the second line.
**10.** Many people used the metro on its opening weekend.
