# R02 · Skimming — Đọc lướt lấy ý chính

|---|---|
| **Cấp độ** | A2 → B2 |
| **Mục tiêu** | Nắm ý chính của một văn bản trong thời gian ngắn |
| **Đáp án** | [Reading Chapters — Answers](../../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/SKILLS/READING-CHAPTERS-ANSWERS.md#r02) |

## 1. Skimming là gì?

Đọc lướt nhanh để trả lời: **văn bản nói về gì? mục đích? cấu trúc?** — không cần hiểu từng chi tiết. Tốc độ gấp 2–3 lần đọc thường.

## 2. Đọc những phần nào?

| Đọc | Vì sao |
|---|---|
| **Tiêu đề, tiêu đề phụ** | Cho biết chủ đề |
| **Hình ảnh, chú thích, chữ in đậm** | Gợi ý nội dung |
| **Đoạn đầu** (thường toàn bộ) | Giới thiệu chủ đề, luận điểm |
| **Câu đầu mỗi đoạn** | Thường là câu chủ đề ([R04](R04-finding-main-ideas.md)) |
| **Đoạn cuối** (hoặc câu cuối) | Kết luận |

Bỏ qua: số liệu, ví dụ, tên riêng, chi tiết phụ.

## 3. Quy trình

1. Đặt **giới hạn thời gian** (ví dụ 1 phút cho 300 từ).
2. Đọc tiêu đề → đoạn đầu → câu đầu các đoạn → đoạn cuối.
3. Tự hỏi: *Ai? Cái gì? Mục đích?*
4. Viết **một câu tóm tắt** (≤ 15 từ).

## 4. Bài luyện (giới hạn 90 giây cho mỗi bài)

### Text A

> **Why More Young People Are Choosing to Rent**
>
> In many Asian cities, owning a home has traditionally been seen as a sign of success. However, a growing number of young professionals are now choosing to rent instead of buying.
>
> The most obvious reason is cost. Property prices in major cities have risen much faster than salaries, making it difficult for young people to save enough for a deposit.
>
> Flexibility is another factor. Many young workers change jobs frequently or want the freedom to move to a different city. Renting allows them to do this without the responsibility of selling a property.
>
> Lifestyle also plays a role. Some young people would rather spend their money on travel and experiences than on a mortgage.
>
> Of course, renting has disadvantages, such as rising rents and less security. Nevertheless, for many young people today, renting seems to be the more practical choice.

**1.** What is the text mainly about?
A. The history of property prices B. Reasons why young people rent rather than buy C. How to buy a house
**2.** How many reasons for renting are given?
**3.** Viết một câu tóm tắt (≤ 15 từ).

### Text B

> **Volunteer Tourism: Helping or Harming?**
>
> "Voluntourism" — combining travel with volunteer work — has become popular, especially among students. Participants pay to spend a few weeks building schools or teaching children abroad.
>
> Supporters say these trips allow young people to learn about other cultures while doing something useful. However, critics argue that short-term volunteers without proper skills may do little real good. For example, children may become attached to volunteers who leave after only two weeks.
>
> Experts suggest that anyone considering voluntourism should research organisations carefully and choose longer programmes that work closely with local communities.

**4.** What is the writer's main purpose?
A. To present both sides of voluntourism and give advice B. To advertise voluntourism trips C. To criticise students
**5.** Which paragraph gives advice?
