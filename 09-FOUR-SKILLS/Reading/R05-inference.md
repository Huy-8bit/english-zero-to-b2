# R05 · Inference — Suy luận khi đọc

| Mục | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| **Cấp độ** | B1 → B2 |
| **Mục tiêu** | Hiểu điều không được nói thẳng: hàm ý, thái độ, mục đích, kết luận hợp lý |
| **Đáp án** | [Reading Chapters — Answers](../../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/SKILLS/READING-CHAPTERS-ANSWERS.md#r05) |

## 1. Suy luận là gì?

**Suy luận** (inference) = rút ra kết luận **dựa trên bằng chứng trong bài**, dù tác giả không nói thẳng.

> *Mai looked at the clock again and sighed. The bus was supposed to arrive twenty minutes ago.*
> → Suy luận: *Mai is impatient / annoyed; the bus is late.* (Bài không viết "Mai is annoyed".)

## 2. Suy luận hợp lý vs suy đoán quá xa

| ✅ Suy luận hợp lý | ❌ Suy đoán quá xa |
|---|---|
| Có **bằng chứng trực tiếp** trong bài | Dựa vào kiến thức/ý kiến cá nhân |
| *Mai is waiting for a bus that is late.* | *Mai will be fired for being late to work.* |

> Quy tắc vàng: **Luôn chỉ ra được câu/cụm từ trong bài** làm bằng chứng cho suy luận.

## 3. Các dạng câu hỏi suy luận

| Câu hỏi | Tìm gì |
|---|---|
| *What can be inferred from paragraph 2?* | Kết luận logic từ thông tin đoạn 2 |
| *The writer implies that...* | Hàm ý — điều được gợi ý |
| *What is the writer's attitude towards...?* | Từ ngữ mang cảm xúc: *unfortunately, surprisingly, sadly, impressive* |
| *Why does the writer mention...?* | Chức năng của chi tiết: ví dụ? đối lập? bằng chứng? |
| *Who is the text probably written for?* | Văn phong, từ vựng, nội dung |

## 4. Dấu hiệu thái độ tác giả

| Thái độ | Từ ngữ gợi ý |
|---|---|
| Tích cực | *remarkable, impressive, fortunately, encouragingly, valuable* |
| Tiêu cực | *unfortunately, worryingly, disappointing, sadly, problematic* |
| Hoài nghi | *supposedly, so-called, claims (that), it remains to be seen* |
| Cân bằng | *on the one hand... on the other hand, while, admittedly* |

## 5. Bài luyện

### Text — The Last Bookshop on Trang Tien Street

> When Mr Hai opened his small bookshop in 1985, there were more than a dozen others on the same street. Today, his is the only one left. "People used to come here every weekend," he says, looking at the empty chairs near the window. "Now most of them buy online."
>
> Yet Mr Hai has no plans to close. Last year he started a reading club for local children, and on Saturday mornings the shop is full of noise again. He has also begun selling second-hand books, which, he says, "students can actually afford."
>
> His daughter, who works in marketing, created a social media page for the shop. "I didn't think anyone would be interested," Mr Hai admits. Within six months, the page had attracted thousands of followers — many of them young people who had never visited a bookshop before.
>
> "Some people say bookshops are a thing of the past," he says with a smile. "I'd like them to come here on a Saturday morning and say that again."

> ℹ️ Nhân vật và cửa hàng là hư cấu, dùng để luyện đọc.

**1.** What can be inferred about the bookshop business on the street since 1985?
A. It has grown B. It has declined significantly C. It has stayed the same
**2.** What does Mr Hai imply by saying second-hand books are something "students can actually afford"?
**3.** How did Mr Hai probably feel when his daughter suggested the social media page?
A. Excited B. Angry C. Doubtful
**4.** Why does the writer mention that many followers "had never visited a bookshop before"?
**5.** What is Mr Hai's attitude towards people who say bookshops are "a thing of the past"?
**6.** Trích bằng chứng cho câu trả lời ở câu 5.
