## Part 1 — Short conversations (transcripts L08-P1-1 → 4)

Mỗi đoạn một câu hỏi.

**1.** Why is the man calling the restaurant?
A. To make a reservation B. To ask for directions C. To change a reservation D. To complain about food

**2.** What will the woman probably do next?
A. Go to another shop B. Buy the red dress C. Ask for a refund D. Try on a bigger size

**3.** What is the problem with the train?
A. It is cancelled B. It goes to a different platform C. It is full D. It is delayed by 40 minutes

**4.** How does the man feel about his new job?
A. Angry B. Bored C. Disappointed D. Nervous but excited

## Part 2 — Conversation (transcript L08-P2: two colleagues planning a team-building trip)

**5.** Where do they decide to go?
A. To a city museum B. To a farm C. To the mountains D. To the beach

**6.** Why do they reject the first suggestion?
A. It's too far B. The weather will be bad C. It's too expensive D. Some colleagues can't swim

**7.** How will they travel?
A. By train B. By their own cars C. By hired bus D. By plane

**8.** What does the woman agree to do?
A. Collect money B. Contact the bus company C. Plan the activities D. Book the accommodation

## Part 3 — Talk (transcript L08-P3: a lecture on the history of the bicycle in cities)

> ℹ️ Bài giảng là văn bản luyện tập, nêu các ý khái quát phổ biến; không dùng làm tài liệu lịch sử chính xác.

**9.** What is the main topic of the lecture?
A. Why bicycles became popular again in cities B. Bicycle racing C. The danger of cycling D. How bicycles are made

**10.** According to the lecturer, why did cycling decline in many cities in the mid-20th century?
A. Laws banned bicycles B. Bicycles became expensive C. People became less healthy D. Cars became more affordable and roads were built for them

**11.** What example of a city policy does the lecturer give?
A. Separate, protected cycle lanes B. Banning all cars C. Free bicycles for children D. Taxing bicycles

**12.** What is one benefit of bike-sharing schemes mentioned?
A. They are faster than cars B. They are free C. They reduce taxes D. People don't need to own or store a bike

**13.** What challenge does the lecturer mention for cities in hot climates?
A. Bicycles break easily B. Bicycles are too expensive C. There is no space D. People arrive at work sweaty

**14.** What is the lecturer's conclusion?
A. Cycling can grow if cities provide safe infrastructure B. Cycling is only suitable for small towns C. Bicycles will replace cars completely D. Electric bikes are dangerous

**15.** Viết một câu tóm tắt bài giảng (≤ 25 từ).
