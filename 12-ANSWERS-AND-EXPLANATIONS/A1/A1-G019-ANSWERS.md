# A1-G019 · Answer Key — Future with Will

> Bài học: [A1-G019](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G019-future-will.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. will** | Đề nghị/quyết định tức thời. |
| L1.2 | **B. will get** | Dự đoán; *will* + nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.3 | **B. Will** | Lời nhờ: *Will you...?* |
| L1.4 | **A. won't** | Lời hứa phủ định. |
| L1.5 | **B. Shall** | Đề nghị giúp với *I*. |
| L1.6 | **B. get** | Sau *when* → hiện tại đơn. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~85 từ):

> *I think life in 2040 will be very different. Most cars will probably be electric, and some of them will drive themselves. I don't think people will work in offices every day — they'll work from home more often. Robots will definitely do a lot of housework. Phones probably won't exist; we'll use smart glasses instead. As for me, I'll be 50 years old. I hope I'll live in a quiet house near the sea.*

✅ Tiêu chí: *will/won't* + V; *I don't think... will*; vị trí *probably/definitely* đúng (*will probably*, *probably won't*).

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
1. *I'll lend you my umbrella.*
2. *I'll have tea, please.*
3. *I'll help you. Let me have a look.*
4. *I'll go to the shop and buy some.*
5. *I'll take you out for coffee.*
6. *I'll get it!*

## Knowledge Check

1. Quyết định tức thời (*I'll have the fish*), dự đoán theo ý kiến (*I think it will rain*), lời hứa (*I won't tell anyone*), đề nghị giúp (*I'll carry your bag*), sự thật/thông báo tương lai (*I'll be 30 next year*). Ngoài ra: yêu cầu *Will you...?* và *won't* = không chịu (*The car won't start*).
2. *will not*; đọc /wəʊnt/ (nguyên âm như trong *go*), khác *want* /wɒnt/.
3. *I* và *we* trong câu hỏi: *Shall I...?* để đề nghị giúp, *Shall we...?* để gợi ý cùng làm.
4. Hiện tại đơn: *I'll call you when I arrive.* (không *when I will arrive*).
5. *I don't think he'll come.*
