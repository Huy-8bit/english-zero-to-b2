# A1-G007 · Answer Key — Object Pronouns

> Bài học: [A1-G007](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G007-object-pronouns.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. them** | *keys* số nhiều, vị trí tân ngữ. |
| L1.2 | **B. her** | Sau động từ *visit*. |
| L1.3 | **C. us** | Sau giới từ *for*. |
| L1.4 | **B. him** | Sau động từ *know*. |
| L1.5 | **B. I** | Vị trí chủ ngữ (*I am a teacher* — bỏ "and my brother"). Câu đúng và lịch sự nhất: *My brother and I are teachers.* |
| L1.6 | **B** | Vật là đại từ → *give it to me*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | I often call **them**. |
| L2.2 | She loves **it**. |
| L2.3 | We meet **him** on Mondays. |
| L2.4 | They invited **us**. |
| L2.5 | Can you help **her**? |
| L2.6 | She sent a message **to me**. |
| L2.7 | I bought **her** a scarf. |
| L2.8 | Can you show the photos **to us**? |
| L2.9 | He cooked **them** dinner. |
| L2.10 | Tell the news **to him**. (*Tell him the news* tự nhiên hơn; mẫu *tell sth to sb* ít dùng hơn nhưng đúng) |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | My boss always helps **me**. |
| L3.2 | **Tuan and I** are best friends. |
| L3.3 | This present is for **her**. |
| L3.4 | Please give **it to me**. |
| L3.5 | My parents love **us** very much. |
| L3.6 | She told **us** a funny story. (*tell* + người + vật; không dùng *told to us a story*) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I miss **you**. |
| L4.2 | He sent **me** a letter. / He sent a letter **to me**. |
| L4.3 | Can you come with **us**? |
| L4.4 | Don't worry about **them**. |
| L4.5 | My mother cooks breakfast **for us** every day. / My mother cooks **us** breakfast every day. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu:

> *Hi Minh! Our teacher, Ms Ha, is leaving next week. Let's give **her** a surprise. I can buy **her** some flowers. Can you write **her** a card? Ask the other students and tell **them** about the plan. Let's meet at 4 p.m. — Ms Ha can't see **us** in the library. I have a photo of **her** with our class. I can print **it** and give **it to her** with the card. She always helps **us**, so we love **her** a lot!*

✅ Tiêu chí: dùng đại từ tân ngữ ≥ 6 lần, ≥ 4 loại (*her, them, us, it*); ≥ 2 câu hai tân ngữ (*buy her some flowers, write her a card, give it to her*).

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *My best friend is Quan. I meet **him** every weekend, and he always listens to **me**. My grandparents live in the countryside. I visit **them** every Tet, and they always give **us** lucky money. My manager is Ms Lan. I respect **her** a lot because she teaches **me** many useful things.*

✅ Tiêu chí: mỗi người có 2–3 câu; đại từ tân ngữ đứng sau động từ/giới từ; không dùng *I/he/they* ở vị trí tân ngữ.
