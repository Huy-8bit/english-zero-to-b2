# A1-G001 · Answer Key — Present Simple: Affirmative

> Bài học: [A1-G001](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G001-present-simple-affirmative.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. drinks** | *My grandmother* = she; thói quen (*every afternoon*). C sai vì *is* + V nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.2 | **A. live** | *We* → nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.3 | **B. closes** | *The shop* = it; *close* tận cùng *e* → chỉ thêm *s*. Giờ đóng cửa là lịch trình. |
| L1.4 | **B. studies** | Phụ âm *d* + *y* → *ies*. |
| L1.5 | **B** | Sự thật chung về loài mèo. A, C là việc đang diễn ra. |
| L1.6 | **C. /ɪz/** | Âm cuối /ʃ/ → /ɪz/. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|
| L2.1 | **watches** | *-ch* → *es* |
| L2.2 | **go** | *parents* số nhiều |
| L2.3 | **cries** | phụ âm + *y* |
| L2.4 | **has** | bất quy tắc |
| L2.5 | **starts** | lịch trình |
| L2.6 | **fixes** | *-x* → *es* |
| L2.7 | **loves** | *Everybody* là ngôi 3 số ít (dù nghĩa là "mọi người") |
| L2.8 | **enjoy** | *I* |
| L2.9 | **rains** | *It* — thời tiết thường niên (sự thật) |
| L2.10 | **teaches** | *-ch* → *es* |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | My father **works** in a car factory. | Ngôi 3 số ít. |
| L3.2 | She **speaks** three languages. | Bỏ *is*. |
| L3.3 | He **studies** at night. | *-ies*. |
| L3.4 | The children **play** in the park after school. | *children* là số nhiều. |
| L3.5 | My aunt **has** a beautiful garden. | *have → has*. |
| L3.6 | I **get** up at six o'clock. | *I* không thêm *-s*. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | My younger brother **goes** to school by bike. |
| L4.2 | This shop **opens** at 7 a.m. |
| L4.3 | My (older) sister **works** for a travel company. |
| L4.4 | The sun **sets** in the west. |

**L4.5.** *My sister **gets** up at 6:30. She **has** a shower and she **makes** coffee. She **goes** to work by bus. She **finishes** work at 5 and she **watches** TV in the evening.*

✅ Kiểm tra 6 động từ: *gets, has, makes, goes, finishes, watches*.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~95 từ):

> *My father is a taxi driver in Ho Chi Minh City. He gets up very early, at half past four. He drinks a cup of strong coffee and reads the news on his phone. He leaves home at five and drives until two in the afternoon. He knows every street in the city. He usually has lunch at a small restaurant near the airport. In the evening, he plays chess with our neighbour and watches football on TV. He loves his job because he meets interesting people every day.*

✅ Tiêu chí tự chấm:
- [ ] ≥ 10 động từ khác nhau ở ngôi 3 số ít, đúng chính tả (*watches, has, goes*...).
- [ ] Có cụm thời gian (*every day, in the evening, at four*).
- [ ] 80–100 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *I usually get up at six. I have breakfast with my family and then I go to work by motorbike. I start work at eight. I work in an office, so I answer emails and go to meetings. I have lunch at twelve with my colleagues. I finish work at five thirty. In the evening, I cook dinner, I study English for an hour, and I go to bed at eleven.*

✅ Tự kiểm tra: dùng *I* + V nguyên mẫu (không thêm *-s*); có các mốc giờ; nói liên tục khoảng 1 phút.
