# A1-G012 · Answer Key — Can / Can't

> Bài học: [A1-G012](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G012-can-cant.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. can** | Modal không chia, không *to*. |
| L1.2 | **B. Can** | Modal tự đảo. |
| L1.3 | **B. can't** | Câu hỏi *Can* → trả lời *can't*. |
| L1.4 | **A. hear** | Sau *can* → V nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.5 | **B** | Người hành động là "tôi" → *Can I...?* |
| L1.6 | **B** | Trong câu khẳng định, *can* đọc yếu /kən/. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | A fish **can't walk**. |
| L2.2 | Birds **can fly**. |
| L2.3 | My grandfather **can use** a computer. |
| L2.4 | I **can't come** to the party because I'm working. |
| L2.5 | You **can pay** by credit card here. |
| L2.6 | **Can you repeat that, please?** |
| L2.7 | **Can I borrow your pen?** |
| L2.8 | **Where can I change money?** |
| L2.9 | **Can you swim?** |
| L2.10 | **Can you send the email, please?** (hoặc *Can you send me the email?* — tùy ý) |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | She can **speak** three languages. |
| L3.2 | **Can you** help me? |
| L3.3 | I can **cook** Italian food. |
| L3.4 | My son **can't** swim. |
| L3.5 | He **can** play chess. |
| L3.6 | Yes, they **can**. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I **can speak** English, but I **can't speak** French. |
| L4.2 | **Can I sit** here? |
| L4.3 | **Can you speak** louder, please? |
| L4.4 | You **can buy** tickets online. |
| L4.5 | We **can see** the mountains from here. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~85 từ):

> *I have some useful skills. I can speak English quite well and I can understand a little Japanese. I can cook simple Vietnamese dishes like spring rolls and fried rice. I can use Excel and PowerPoint very well. However, I can't play any musical instruments, and I can't swim — which is a bit embarrassing because I live near the sea! This year, I want to learn to swim and play the guitar.*

✅ Tiêu chí: *can/can't* + V nguyên mẫu; không *to*; có cả khả năng và không khả năng. (*want to learn* — *want* + *to V*, học kỹ ở A2-G015.)

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
- *Can I have the Wi-Fi password, please? — Sure, it's on your key card.*
- *What time can I have breakfast? — From 6:30 to 10.*
- *Can you call a taxi for me? — Of course.*
- *Can I leave my luggage here after check-out? — Yes, you can.*
- *Can I change my room? It's very noisy. — Let me check.*
- *Can I check out late tomorrow? — You can check out at 1 p.m.*
