# A1-G016 · Answer Key — Past Simple of Be

> Bài học: [A1-G016](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G016-past-simple-be.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. was** | *I* + *last night*. |
| L1.2 | **B. were** | *My parents* số nhiều + 2019. |
| L1.3 | **C. Were** | *you* → *were*; *be* không dùng *did*. |
| L1.4 | **B. weren't** | *students* số nhiều. |
| L1.5 | **A. in 1998** | Năm → *in*. |
| L1.6 | **B. wasn't** | Câu hỏi *Was* → *wasn't*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **was** |
| L2.2 | **weren't** ... **was** |
| L2.3 | **Were** |
| L2.4 | **was** |
| L2.5 | **wasn't** (không đói nên không ăn trưa) |
| L2.6 | **were** ... **was** |
| L2.7 | I **was** tired last night. |
| L2.8 | There **were** many people at the beach yesterday. |
| L2.9 | **Was** she at work this morning? |
| L2.10 | They **weren't** happy with the hotel last week. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | I **was born** in Hai Phong in 2000. |
| L3.2 | **Were you** busy yesterday? |
| L3.3 | We **were** very hungry after the game. |
| L3.4 | **Last night** I was at home. |
| L3.5 | There **were** many cars on the road. |
| L3.6 | She was here **two hours ago**. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I **was** sick (ill) yesterday. |
| L4.2 | Where **were** you born? |
| L4.3 | How **was** your holiday? |
| L4.4 | There **weren't** any restaurants here last year. / There were no restaurants here last year. |
| L4.5 | When I **was** a child, I **was** very shy. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~80 từ):

> *My last holiday was in Phu Quoc in June. The weather was hot and sunny, and the sea was very clear. Our hotel was near the beach. It wasn't big, but it was clean and comfortable. There was a small swimming pool, and there were a lot of palm trees. The seafood was fresh and cheap. The people were very friendly. We were happy and relaxed every day. It was the best holiday of my life!*

✅ Tiêu chí: chỉ dùng *was/were/there was/were* đúng chủ ngữ; có ít nhất 1 câu phủ định.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *I was born in a small village in Thai Binh. Our house was small, and there was a big garden with a lot of fruit trees. My best friend was Hung — he was my neighbour. My favourite subject was art. I was a quiet child, but I was very curious. There weren't any computers in my school, so we were always outside, playing games.*
