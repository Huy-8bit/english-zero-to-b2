# A1-G002 · Answer Key — Present Simple: Negatives & Questions

> Bài học: [A1-G002](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G002-present-simple-negatives-questions.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. doesn't** | *My boss* = he/she. |
| L1.2 | **B. Do** | *your children* số nhiều. |
| L1.3 | **C. does** | *the post office* = it. |
| L1.4 | **B. doesn't** | Câu hỏi *Does* → *doesn't*. |
| L1.5 | **B** | *What does + S + mean?* — C lặp *-s*. |
| L1.6 | **B** | *live* là động từ thường → *Do*. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | My mother **doesn't** work on Mondays. | Ngôi 3 → *doesn't*. |
| L3.2 | Does your brother **like** football? | Sau *Does* → nguyên mẫu. |
| L3.3 | Where **do** your parents live? | Thiếu trợ động từ. |
| L3.4 | What **does** "fluent" **mean**? | |
| L3.5 | I **don't** drink alcohol. | |
| L3.6 | **Do** you go to the gym every day? | Động từ thường → *Do*. |
| L3.7 | Yes, I **do**. | Trả lời ngắn bằng trợ động từ. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu câu hỏi:

Yes/No: *Do you live near the office? · Do you have any children? · Does your partner work here too? · Do you cook at home? · Do you like sports?*
Wh-: *Where do you come from? · How do you get to work? · What time do you start work? · What do you do at weekends? · Why do you study English?*

Đoạn tóm tắt mẫu (~70 từ):

> *Hai lives in Binh Thanh, so he doesn't live near the office. He comes to work by motorbike. He starts work at eight. He has one son. His wife doesn't work here — she's a teacher. He doesn't cook at home, but he loves sports, especially swimming. At weekends, he goes to the beach. He studies English because he wants to travel.*

✅ Tiêu chí tự chấm:
- [ ] Câu hỏi đúng QUASI; *do/does* đúng chủ ngữ.
- [ ] Đoạn tóm tắt dùng ngôi 3 số ít (*lives, comes, has*) và ≥ 3 câu phủ định *doesn't + V*.

**L5.2.** Ví dụ:
- *I get up at 5 every day.* / *I don't eat rice.* / *I play the guitar.*
- Câu hỏi có thể hỏi: *Do you really get up at 5 on Sundays? — Yes, I do.* / *Do you eat noodles? — Yes, I do.* / *Do you have a guitar? — Yes, I do.*

✅ Tiêu chí: 3 câu khẳng định/phủ định đúng; câu hỏi Yes/No dùng *Do* + *you* + V; trả lời ngắn *Yes, I do / No, I don't*.
