# A1-G021 · Answer Key — Basic Conjunctions

> Bài học: [A1-G021](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G021-basic-conjunctions.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. but** | Tương phản: muốn đi chơi ↔ trời mưa. |
| L1.2 | **A. or** | Lựa chọn. |
| L1.3 | **B. so** | Lỡ xe buýt (nguyên nhân) → muộn (kết quả). |
| L1.4 | **C. because** | Vui (kết quả) ← đỗ (lý do). |
| L1.5 | **B. or** | Câu phủ định. |
| L1.6 | **B** | A dùng cặp đôi; C dùng *because of* + mệnh đề. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | I was thirsty, **so** I drank a glass of water. / I drank a glass of water **because** I was thirsty. |
| L2.2 | The restaurant was expensive, **but** the food wasn't very good. |
| L2.3 | We can go by bus **or** (by) train. |
| L2.4 | She stayed at home **because** she had a headache. / She had a headache, **so** she stayed at home. |
| L2.5 | My father is a farmer, **and** my mother is a nurse. |
| L2.6 | The shop was closed, **so** we went to another one. |

> L2.2: *but* ở đây diễn tả "đắt nhưng (lại) không ngon" — kết quả trái kỳ vọng (đắt thì lẽ ra phải ngon).

**L2.7–L2.10** (mẫu):
- *I'm learning English because I want to travel / get a better job.*
- *It was my birthday, so my friends took me out for dinner.*
- *I like my city, but the traffic is terrible.*
- *At the weekend, I usually go swimming or visit my parents.*

✅ Tiêu chí: sau *because* là mệnh đề lý do; sau *so* là kết quả; sau *but* là ý tương phản; *or* nối hai lựa chọn cùng dạng.

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | Because I didn't have money, I didn't buy it. / I didn't have money, so I didn't buy it. |
| L3.2 | I don't like cats **or** dogs. |
| L3.3 | We cancelled the trip **because** it was raining. (hoặc *because of the rain*) |
| L3.4 | I was sleepy, **so** I drank three cups of coffee. |
| L3.5 | She is beautiful **and** (she is) kind. |
| L3.6 | I bought apples, oranges, bananas and grapes. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I wanted to buy that phone, **but** it was too expensive. |
| L4.2 | It was cold, **so** I wore a jacket. |
| L4.3 | I like living in Da Lat **because** the air is fresh. |
| L4.4 | Do you want to go to the cinema **or** stay at home? |
| L4.5 | He doesn't smoke **or** drink alcohol. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu:

> *I live in a small town. It's quiet, but it's quite boring. There aren't many jobs, so young people move to big cities. I want to stay because I love my family. Maybe I will open a café or teach English.*

✅ Tiêu chí: các mối quan hệ được thể hiện đúng (*but* — tương phản, *so* — kết quả, *because* — lý do, *or* — lựa chọn); không lặp *maybe*.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
1. *I learn English because I want to study abroad, and I also love American films.*
2. *I prefer the countryside because it's quiet, but I live in the city because of my job.*
3. *Last weekend, I was tired, so I stayed at home and watched TV.*
4. *I like my job because my colleagues are nice, but it's sometimes stressful.*
5. *Next summer, I'm going to go to Thailand or Malaysia, because flights there are cheap.*
