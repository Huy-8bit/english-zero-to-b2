# A1-G003 · Answer Key — Adverbs of Frequency

> Bài học: [A1-G003](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G003-adverbs-of-frequency.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **always → often → sometimes → rarely → never** | Theo thang 100% → 0%. |
| L1.2 | **B** | Trạng từ đứng sau *be*. |
| L1.3 | **A** | *never* + động từ khẳng định. |
| L1.4 | **B** | Trước động từ thường. |
| L1.5 | **B. How often** | Hỏi tần suất. |
| L1.6 | **A** | aux + adv + V nguyên mẫu. B, C lặp *-s* sau *doesn't*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | My father **always drinks** coffee in the morning. |
| L2.2 | I **am usually** hungry after work. (hoặc *Usually, I am hungry after work.*) |
| L2.3 | They **rarely go** to the beach in winter. |
| L2.4 | She **is never** late for meetings. |
| L2.5 | Do you **often eat** out? |
| L2.6 | We **don't usually watch** TV. |
| L2.7 | **once a week** |
| L2.8 | **twice a year** |
| L2.9 | **four times a day** |
| L2.10 | **every Sunday** / **on Sundays** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | I **always go** to bed at 11. |
| L3.2 | My brother **never helps** with the housework. (bỏ *doesn't*; *helps* có *-s* vì ngôi 3) |
| L3.3 | She **is usually** tired on Mondays. |
| L3.4 | We visit our grandparents **twice a month**. |
| L3.5 | How often **do you** clean your room? |
| L3.6 | I **always forget** her birthday. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | My mother **always gets up** early. |
| L4.2 | I **rarely eat** fast food. (hoặc *hardly ever*) |
| L4.3 | He **is never** on time. |
| L4.4 | **Do you ever take** the bus? |
| L4.5 | I go to the cinema **about once a month**. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~85 từ):

> *I have some good habits and some bad ones. I always drink a lot of water and I usually walk to work. I go to the gym twice a week. I never smoke. However, I often stay up late because I watch videos on my phone. I hardly ever eat breakfast, and I sometimes drink three cups of coffee a day. I am rarely in bed before midnight. I want to change these bad habits this year.*

✅ Tiêu chí: ≥ 6 trạng từ/cụm tần suất khác nhau; vị trí đúng với *be* (*I am rarely...*) và động từ thường; không phủ định kép.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
1. *I go jogging three times a week.*
2. *Yes, I often cook at the weekend.*
3. *I go to the cinema about once a month.*
4. *I'm hardly ever late, but sometimes the traffic is terrible.*
5. *I call my parents every evening.*
6. *No, never!*
