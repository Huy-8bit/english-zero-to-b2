# A1-G010 · Answer Key — Prepositions of Time

> Bài học: [A1-G010](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G010-prepositions-of-time.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **C. at** | Giờ. |
| L1.2 | **B. on** | Ngày cụ thể. |
| L1.3 | **A. in** | Tháng. |
| L1.4 | **C** | *next week* — không giới từ. |
| L1.5 | **B. in** | *in the morning*. |
| L1.6 | **B. In** | "20 phút nữa" (tương lai từ bây giờ). |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L2.1 | **in** ... **on** ... **at** | năm → *in*; ngày cụ thể → *on*; *at night* |
| L2.2 | **at** | Dịp lễ. |
| L2.3 | **on** | Thứ, lặp lại. |
| L2.4 | **–** | *this evening*. |
| L2.5 | **at** | *at night*. |
| L2.6 | **on** ... **at** | Thứ → *on*; giờ → *at*. |
| L2.7 | **in** | Thập kỷ. |
| L2.8 | **–** | *yesterday afternoon*. |
| L2.9 | **in** | Mùa. |
| L2.10 | **on** | Ngày đặc biệt. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | I go to the gym **on** Tuesdays and Thursdays. (hoặc *on Tuesday and Thursday*) |
| L3.2 | See you **next** Monday! |
| L3.3 | My flight leaves **in** the morning. |
| L3.4 | She was born **in** 1995. |
| L3.5 | The film starts **in** 10 minutes. Hurry up! |
| L3.6 | We have English classes **in** the afternoon. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I work **from** Monday **to** Friday. |
| L4.2 | The shop is open **until** 10 p.m. |
| L4.3 | We're going to Da Lat **next month**. |
| L4.4 | I usually drink coffee **in** the morning and read books **at** night. |
| L4.5 | The meeting is **on** Thursday afternoon, **at** 3 o'clock. |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~85 từ):

> *Hi Jenny,*
>
> *Thanks for your message. I'd love to meet! I'm quite busy next week. On Monday and Tuesday, I work from 8 a.m. until 7 p.m. On Wednesday evening, I have a yoga class at 6:30. However, I'm free on Thursday afternoon after 3 p.m. I'm also free at the weekend — maybe on Saturday morning? I usually have breakfast at a café near my house at 9. Let me know!*
>
> *Cheers,*
> *Nhi*

✅ Tiêu chí: ≥ 8 cụm thời gian đúng (*next week* — không giới từ; *on Monday, from...until, on Wednesday evening, at 6:30, on Thursday afternoon, after 3 p.m., at the weekend, on Saturday morning, at 9*).

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *My birthday is on the 12th of June, in summer. I usually have a small party at home. At Tet, I go back to my hometown and stay with my family for a week. In April, my company has a holiday, so we often travel. My favourite season is autumn — in October, the weather in Hanoi is perfect. On New Year's Eve, I watch the fireworks at midnight with my friends.*
