# A1-G015 · Answer Key — Much / Many / A Lot Of

> Bài học: [A1-G015](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G015-much-many-a-lot-of.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. many** | *brothers* đếm được. |
| L1.2 | **B. much** | *coffee* không đếm được, phủ định. |
| L1.3 | **B. a lot of** | Khẳng định + có danh từ. |
| L1.4 | **B. How much** | Hỏi giá. |
| L1.5 | **A. a lot** | Không có danh từ theo sau. |
| L1.6 | **B. too much** | *traffic* không đếm được. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L2.1 | **much** | Phủ định + không đếm được |
| L2.2 | **a lot of** | Khẳng định (*many* cũng đúng nhưng trang trọng hơn) |
| L2.3 | **many** | |
| L2.4 | **much** | Phủ định + không đếm được |
| L2.5 | **a lot of** | Khẳng định |
| L2.6 | **many** (hoặc *a lot of*) | Câu hỏi + đếm được |
| L2.7 | **How much water do you drink a day?** | |
| L2.8 | **How many people are there in your team?** | |
| L2.9 | **How much is this laptop?** / *How much does this laptop cost?* | |
| L2.10 | **How many eggs do you need for this cake?** | |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | How **many** people live in your house? |
| L3.2 | I have **a lot of** good friends. |
| L3.3 | There are a lot **of** cars on the road. |
| L3.4 | Thank you **very much**! / Thanks **a lot**! |
| L3.5 | How **much** is a ticket? |
| L3.6 | He doesn't have **much** money. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I don't have **much** experience. |
| L4.2 | My city has **a lot of** parks. / There are **a lot of** parks in my city. |
| L4.3 | **How many** cups of coffee do you drink a day? |
| L4.4 | There's **too much** sugar in this dish. |
| L4.5 | **How much** is this bag? |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~85 từ):

> *I live in Ho Chi Minh City. There are a lot of good things here: there are lots of restaurants, a lot of jobs and a lot of interesting people. But there are also some problems. There are too many motorbikes, and there's too much noise at night. There aren't many parks, so children don't have much space to play. In the rainy season, there's a lot of flooding. But I still love my city!*

✅ Tiêu chí: ≥ 6 lượng từ đúng loại danh từ; *too many/much* dùng với ý tiêu cực.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
- *How much is this T-shirt? — It's 250,000 dong.*
- *How much are these sunglasses? — They're 400,000.*
- *How many colours do you have? — We have five.*
- *That's too much. Can you give me a discount? — OK, 350,000 for the sunglasses.*
