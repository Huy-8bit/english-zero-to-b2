# A1-G020 · Answer Key — Be Going To

> Bài học: [A1-G020](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/grammar/A1-G020-be-going-to.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. are going to** | Đã báo trước → kế hoạch. C thiếu *be*. |
| L1.2 | **B. is going to** | Bằng chứng nhìn thấy. |
| L1.3 | **A. 'll** | Đề nghị/quyết định tức thời. |
| L1.4 | **B. are** | Gia đình *be*. |
| L1.5 | **B. start** | *going to* + V nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.6 | **B** | *gonna* chỉ dùng trong văn nói thân mật. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L2.1 | **I'm going to clean** my room this afternoon. | |
| L2.2 | **They aren't going to buy** a new house. | |
| L2.3 | **Are you going to watch** the football match? | |
| L2.4 | **'re going to be late** | Bằng chứng: còn 5 phút mà vẫn ở nhà |
| L2.5 | **'s going to be** | Bằng chứng: trời trong xanh |
| L2.6 | **'ll buy** | Quyết định ngay sau khi nhận ra đã quên |
| L2.7 | **is going to have** | Bằng chứng (đang mang thai) |
| L2.8 | **'m going to play** | Kế hoạch có sẵn (đã mặc đồ tập) |
| L2.9 | **will do** | Dự đoán theo ý kiến (*I think*) |
| L2.10 | **'ll help** | Đề nghị tức thời |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | I**'m going to** visit Hue next month. |
| L3.2 | He is going to **buy** a new phone. |
| L3.3 | **Are** you going to come to my party? |
| L3.4 | "I**'ll** answer it." |
| L3.5 | We are going **to travel** to Sapa. |
| L3.6 | Look out! The glass **is going to** fall! |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | What **are you going to do** tonight? |
| L4.2 | We **aren't going to go** on holiday this year. |
| L4.3 | Be careful! You**'re going to spill** the coffee! |
| L4.4 | My (older) brother **is going to do** a master's degree in Australia next year. |
| L4.5 | "We're out of milk." — "I**'ll go** and buy some." |

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~95 từ):

> *Next year is going to be a busy year for me. First, I'm going to take an IELTS course because I want to study abroad. I'm also going to join a gym and exercise three times a week. In the summer, my family is going to travel to Thailand — we've already booked the hotel. I'm going to save some money every month, and I'm going to read one book a month. I'm not sure about my job. Maybe I'll look for a new one, or I'll probably ask for a promotion.*

✅ Tiêu chí: ≥ 5 kế hoạch với *be going to*; ≥ 2 câu không chắc chắn với *maybe/probably + will*. (*we've already booked* là hiện tại hoàn thành — học ở A2.)

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *Next weekend, I'm going to visit my cousin in Bac Ninh. On Saturday morning, we're going to go to a pottery village. In the afternoon, I'm going to help her cook a family dinner. I think it'll be very hot, so I'm going to bring a hat. I think my little nephew will love the toy I bought for him. On Sunday, I'm not sure — maybe I'll stay at home and rest.*
