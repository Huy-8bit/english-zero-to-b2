## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~95 từ):

> *I know Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City well. Hanoi is older and much more traditional, with a lot of temples and lakes. Ho Chi Minh City is bigger, busier and more modern. In winter, Hanoi is colder, but Ho Chi Minh City is hotter all year round. Life in Hanoi is a bit cheaper, and I think the street food is better there. However, Ho Chi Minh City is more dynamic and the job opportunities are better. The traffic in both cities is terrible, but in Saigon it's even worse!*

✅ Tiêu chí: ≥ 8 so sánh hơn; có *much/a bit*; có *better/worse*.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *Five years ago, I was a student. Now I'm older and busier. I'm more confident when I speak to people. My English is much better, and I'm more independent because I live alone. But I'm a bit less healthy — I exercise less and I'm more tired. I think my life is more interesting now.*

> *less* (kém hơn) là cấu trúc ngược của *more* — học kỹ ở A2-G027.
