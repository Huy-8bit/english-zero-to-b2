## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Câu hỏi mẫu:

Yes/No: *Did you go abroad? · Did you travel by plane? · Was the hotel nice? · Did you try the local food? · Did you buy any souvenirs?*
Wh-: *Where did you go? · Who did you go with? · How long did you stay? · What did you do there? · What did you like best?*

Đoạn kể lại mẫu (~75 từ):

> *Minh went to Bangkok with his sister last April. They flew there and stayed for five days. Their hotel was small, but it was near the river. They visited temples and markets and tried a lot of street food. Minh loved the mango sticky rice. They didn't go to the beach, and they didn't buy many souvenirs because they didn't have much money. But Minh said it was a great trip.*

✅ Tiêu chí: câu hỏi đúng *Did/Was*; đoạn văn dùng V2 ngôi 3 và ≥ 3 câu *didn't + V*.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:

> *I went to Da Nang. I went with my best friend. We got there by plane. We stayed for four days, in a small hotel near My Khe beach. We swam every morning, visited the Marble Mountains and went to Hoi An one evening. Yes, the food was excellent — I loved mi Quang. And yes, I had a fantastic time!*

## Knowledge Check

1. Nguyên mẫu, vì *did* đã mang dấu hiệu quá khứ; đánh dấu hai lần (~~didn't went~~, ~~Did she went?~~) là sai.
2. *Were you...?* khi sau chủ ngữ là tính từ/danh từ/nơi chốn (*Were you tired?*); *Did you...?* khi có động từ hành động (*Did you sleep well?*).
3. *What did you do at the weekend?* (Anh-Mỹ: *on the weekend*).
4. *She didn't go to bed early.*
5. Không. *Did/didn't* dùng cho mọi chủ ngữ ở quá khứ.
