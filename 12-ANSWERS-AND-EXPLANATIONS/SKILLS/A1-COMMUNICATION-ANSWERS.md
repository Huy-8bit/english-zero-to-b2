# A1 Communication — Câu trả lời mẫu và tiêu chí tự chấm

> Bài tập: [A1-COMMUNICATION](../../02-A1-BEGINNER/A1-COMMUNICATION.md). Các nhiệm vụ đều là câu mở — bài của bạn có thể khác hoàn toàn mà vẫn đúng. Hãy đối chiếu với **tiêu chí tự chấm**.

## Tiêu chí chung cho mọi nhiệm vụ

- [ ] Dùng mẫu câu phù hợp tình huống (lịch sự với người lạ: *Can I...? / Could you...? / I'd like...*).
- [ ] Câu hỏi đúng cấu trúc (QUASI; *do/does/did* với động từ thường).
- [ ] Có câu đáp lại tự nhiên (*Thank you. / You're welcome. / That sounds great.*).
- [ ] Không có lỗi làm người nghe hiểu sai (giá tiền, giờ, số phòng, hướng đi).

---

## Nhiệm vụ 1 — Meeting people

> **You:** Hi, I'm Linh. I think you're new here?
> **Mark:** Yes, I started this week. I'm Mark.
> **You:** Nice to meet you, Mark. Where are you from?
> **Mark:** I'm from Canada, from Toronto.
> **You:** Cool! What do you do here?
> **Mark:** I'm a designer in the marketing team. And you?
> **You:** I work in accounting. How do you like Ho Chi Minh City?
> **Mark:** I love it, but the traffic is crazy!

✅ ≥ 4 câu hỏi khác nhau; có *Nice to meet you*; câu trả lời dùng đúng *be* / hiện tại đơn.

## Nhiệm vụ 2 — Restaurant with a special request

> **Waiter:** Are you ready to order?
> **You:** Yes, but I have a question. I'm allergic to peanuts. Is there any peanut in the pad thai?
> **Waiter:** Yes, there is, but we can make it without peanuts.
> **You:** Great, thank you. I'd like the pad thai without peanuts, please.
> **Waiter:** No problem. Would you like anything to drink?
> **You:** Can I have an iced tea, please?
> **Waiter:** Sure. Anything else?
> **You:** No, that's all, thanks.
> *(later)*
> **You:** Excuse me, can I have the bill, please?
> **Waiter:** Here you are. That's 210,000 dong.
> **You:** Can I pay by card?
> **Waiter:** Of course.

✅ Nêu yêu cầu rõ (*I'm allergic to... / I don't eat...*); dùng *any* trong câu hỏi; gọi món bằng *I'd like / Can I have*.

## Nhiệm vụ 3 — Buying shoes at a market

> **Seller:** Hello! Shoes? Very cheap!
> **You:** How much are these?
> **Seller:** 400,000 dong.
> **You:** Do you have them in black?
> **Seller:** Yes, here. What size?
> **You:** Thirty-nine. Can I try them on?
> **Seller:** Sure.
> **You:** They're comfortable, but 400,000 is a bit expensive. Can you give me a discount?
> **Seller:** OK, 350,000.
> **You:** How about 300,000?
> **Seller:** 320,000. Last price!
> **You:** OK, I'll take them.

✅ *How much are these* (giày — số nhiều); *them* (đại từ tân ngữ số nhiều); *I'll take them*.

## Nhiệm vụ 4 — Directions (mẫu một lộ trình)

> *To get to the market from my house, turn left when you leave the house. Go straight on for about 200 metres. At the crossroads, turn right into Le Loi Street. The market is on your left, opposite a big pharmacy. It's about five minutes on foot.*

✅ Dùng câu mệnh lệnh (*turn, go, walk*); giới từ vị trí (*on your left, opposite, next to*); có thông tin khoảng cách.

## Nhiệm vụ 5 — Hotel problem

> **Receptionist:** Reception. How can I help you?
> **You:** Hello, this is room 512. I'm sorry, but there's no hot water in the bathroom.
> **Receptionist:** Oh, I'm very sorry. I'll send someone to check it now.
> **You:** Thank you. Also, the air conditioner isn't working very well. It's very hot.
> **Receptionist:** I'm sorry about that. Would you like to change rooms?
> **You:** Yes, please, if that's possible.
> **Receptionist:** Of course. We have a room on the sixth floor. I'll bring you the new key in ten minutes.
> **You:** Great, thank you very much.

✅ Lịch sự (*I'm sorry, but... / if that's possible*); dùng *there's no / isn't working*.

## Nhiệm vụ 6 — Calling a clinic

> **Receptionist:** Good morning, Family Clinic.
> **You:** Hello. I'd like to make an appointment with a doctor, please.
> **Receptionist:** Sure. What's the problem?
> **You:** I have a bad stomach ache.
> **Receptionist:** OK. Can you come tomorrow at 9:30?
> **You:** Sorry, I'm working in the morning. Do you have anything in the afternoon?
> **Receptionist:** Yes, at 4:15.
> **You:** Perfect. And what time do you close?
> **Receptionist:** At 7 p.m. Can I have your name, please?
> **You:** It's Tran Thi Hoa. H-O-A.

✅ *I'd like to make an appointment*; hiện tại tiếp diễn chỉ kế hoạch (*I'm working*); đánh vần tên.

## Nhiệm vụ 7 — Messages

(a) *Hi Nam! Are you free on Friday evening? Would you like to try the new Korean restaurant on Tran Phu Street? 😊*
(b) *Thanks for inviting me! I'd love to come, but I'm visiting my grandparents this weekend. Have fun, and let's go together next time!*
(c) *Hi Lan, sorry, I'm going to finish work late on Saturday. Can we meet at 7:30 instead of 6? Or shall we meet on Sunday?*

✅ Lời mời dùng *Would you like to...?*; từ chối có lý do và lời cảm ơn; gợi ý đổi giờ dùng *Can we...? / Shall we...?*

## Nhiệm vụ 8 — At the doctor's

> **Doctor:** Good morning. What's the matter?
> **You:** I have a terrible headache and a fever.
> **Doctor:** How long have you had it?
> **You:** Since yesterday evening.
> **Doctor:** Do you have a cough or a sore throat?
> **You:** No, I don't. But I feel very tired.
> **Doctor:** OK. It's probably the flu. Take this medicine three times a day, drink lots of water and rest at home for two days.
> **You:** Thank you, doctor.

✅ Triệu chứng dùng *have a/an + bệnh* và *feel + tính từ*; trả lời Yes/No ngắn đúng.

## Nhiệm vụ 9 — Small talk

Câu hỏi mẫu: *How was your weekend? · Did you watch the match last night? · How's your new job going? · What are you doing for the holiday? · Have you tried the new coffee shop downstairs?*
Phản hồi mẫu: *Really? · That sounds amazing! · Oh no! · Lucky you! · Me too!*

✅ Câu hỏi mở (Wh-) giúp kéo dài hội thoại; phản hồi ngắn thể hiện sự quan tâm.
