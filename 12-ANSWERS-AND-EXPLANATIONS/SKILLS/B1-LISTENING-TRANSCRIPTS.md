# B1 Listening Transcripts — Units 1–3

Dùng cho [B1 Skills Practice](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/B1-SKILLS-PRACTICE.md). Chưa có audio. Nhờ người đọc hai lần hoặc dùng TTS; chỉ đưa phần tiếng Anh trong khung trích dẫn vào TTS, không đưa tiêu đề và ghi chú. Người học chỉ xem sau khi làm bài. Tốc độ tự nhiên (khoảng 130–150 từ/phút); với hội thoại nhiều người, chọn giọng khác nhau cho mỗi vai nếu công cụ TTS cho phép.

## Unit 1 — A podcast story

> Welcome back to *Wrong Turns*, the podcast about trips that didn't go as planned. Today's story comes from Minh, a listener in Ho Chi Minh City.
>
> So, last spring I was flying from Ho Chi Minh City to Paris for a friend's wedding. I'd booked a cheap ticket with a connection in Doha, and I only had ninety minutes between flights. I remember thinking, "That's fine, I'll just walk fast."
>
> Well, while we were waiting to board in Ho Chi Minh City, they announced that there was a technical problem with the plane. In the end we took off three hours late. By the time we landed in Doha, my flight to Paris had already left.
>
> I was furious at first. But the airline was actually very helpful. They rebooked me on the next flight, which was the following afternoon, and they gave me a hotel room and some meal vouchers. 
>
> So suddenly I had a free evening in a city I'd never planned to visit. I took a taxi to the old market, and while I was looking at some spices, a woman asked me in French if I knew where the exit was. It turned out she was a student called Claire, and she was going to the same wedding! We'd never met, but we had the same friend. We spent the whole evening exploring together, and we're still good friends now.
>
> The wedding was wonderful, of course. But honestly, my favourite part of the trip was that unexpected night. If my first flight hadn't been delayed, I would never have met Claire. My only regret? If only I'd had more time there. I wish I'd stayed a few more days.

Đọc giờ và số (*ninety minutes, three hours*) rõ, tự nhiên. Không nhấn mạnh bất thường vào các cụm chứa đáp án.

## Unit 2 — A staff meeting

> **Manager:** OK, everyone, thanks for coming. I've got some important news. As many of you know, several of you suggested that we try a four-day week. Well, the directors have agreed to a trial.
>
> **Staff:** *(murmurs)*
>
> **Manager:** The trial will start on the first of March and it'll run for three months. During that time, you'll work Monday to Thursday, nine hours a day — so from eight thirty until six thirty, with an hour for lunch. Your salary won't change.
>
> There's one condition. At the end of each month, everyone will be asked to fill in a short online form about their workload and any problems. The results will be checked by HR, so please be honest.
>
> **Tom:** Can I ask something? Will we have to answer emails on Fridays?
>
> **Manager:** Good question, Tom. No, you won't. But one person from each team will be on call in case a client has an emergency. We'll set up a rota so that nobody has to do it more than once a month.
>
> **Staff member:** And what happens if it doesn't work?
>
> **Manager:** Well, we'll look at the results carefully. If productivity falls, we'll probably go back to the five-day week. But I'm optimistic. I think we'll be surprised by how much we can do in four days.

*On call* = sẵn sàng khi được gọi; *a rota* = lịch phân công luân phiên. Tình huống là giả định.

## Unit 3 — A radio discussion

> **Host:** Good evening and welcome to *City Talk*. Tonight's question: should motorbikes be banned from the city centre? With me are Dr Lan, an urban planner, and Mr Hung, who represents a group of small business owners. Dr Lan, let's start with you.
>
> **Dr Lan:** Thank you. I think a ban in the very centre is a good idea. The air quality there is getting worse and worse, and the streets are so crowded that walking is actually dangerous. Many cities around the world have created car-free zones, and people often find that the streets become more pleasant.
>
> **Host:** Mr Hung, do you agree?
>
> **Mr Hung:** I see your point, but I'm not sure it's realistic right now. My main concern is for street vendors and delivery workers. They depend on motorbikes to earn a living. If they can't enter the centre, many of them will lose customers.
>
> **Dr Lan:** That's a fair point, and I agree that we must protect people's jobs. That's why I'm not suggesting a sudden ban.
>
> **Host:** So what are you suggesting?
>
> **Dr Lan:** A step-by-step approach. We could start with car-free and motorbike-free weekends on just a few streets. If it works, we can extend it gradually.
>
> **Mr Hung:** Well, I could accept a trial like that. But we both agree on one thing: none of this will work unless public transport improves first. People need a real alternative.
>
> **Dr Lan:** Absolutely. Better buses and more metro lines have to come first.
>
> **Host:** Thank you both. After the break, we'll hear from our listeners.

Lưu ý: ý "nhiều thành phố có khu cấm xe" là phát biểu chung của nhân vật, không phải số liệu thống kê.
