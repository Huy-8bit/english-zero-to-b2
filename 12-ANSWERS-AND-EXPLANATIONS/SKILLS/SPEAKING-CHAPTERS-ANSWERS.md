Bài nói không có một đáp án đúng duy nhất — mẫu dưới đây chỉ để tham khảo cách tổ chức ý và ngôn ngữ. **Đừng học thuộc mẫu**; hãy dùng thông tin thật của bạn. I'm learning English because I'd like to work for an international company in the future. I'm also going to travel to Japan next year, so I want to improve my speaking.*

**Task 2 — mẫu (6):**
> *A: Hi, I bought this shirt yesterday, but it's too small. Could I change it for a bigger size?*
> *B: Of course. Do you have the receipt?*
> *A: Yes, here it is.*
> *B: What size would you like?*
> *A: Large, please. Could I try it on first?*
> *B: Sure, the changing room is over there.*

**Tiêu chí tự chấm S02:** có mở đầu/kết thúc lịch sự; có ít nhất 1 lần hỏi lại hoặc xác nhận; dùng *Could I/Can I*.

## S03

**Task 2 — mẫu (3):** *I'd like to talk about my high school English teacher, Ms Thu. She's in her forties, quite short, with shoulder-length wavy hair and glasses. She always wears an áo dài on Mondays. She's very patient and encouraging — for example, she never laughed at our mistakes. She also had a great sense of humour, so her lessons were never boring. She influenced me because she made me believe I could speak English confidently. That's why I'm still studying it today.*

**Task 2 — mẫu (4):** *A place I love to visit is Hoan Kiem Lake in the centre of Hanoi. It's surrounded by trees and old buildings, and in the middle there's a small tower on an island. Early in the morning, you can see people jogging and doing exercises. At weekends, the streets nearby become pedestrian zones, so it's very lively. I like it because it's peaceful in the morning but exciting in the evening.*

## S04

**Mẫu (1):** *Personally, I'd prefer to live in a city, at least while I'm young. The main reason is that there are many more job opportunities. For example, most international companies have offices in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City. Having said that, city life can be stressful because of traffic and pollution. So overall, I think the city is better for young people, but the countryside might be better for families.*

**Mẫu (5) — B2 với nhượng bộ:** *In my view, university education shouldn't be completely free, but it should be much more affordable. The main reason is that free education would cost the government enormous amounts of money, which might be better spent on primary schools. However, I strongly believe that talented students from poor families should receive full scholarships. Admittedly, it's hard to decide who deserves support, but a fair system is possible. So, all things considered, I'd support low fees plus generous scholarships.*

**Tiêu chí:** có đủ O-R-E-O; ≥ 1 cụm nhượng bộ ở câu 5; không dùng *According to me / I am agree*.

## S05

**Task 1:** **b – c – d – a – e**

**Mẫu (2):** *Last summer, my friends and I went camping in Ba Vi. On the first night, while we were cooking dinner, it suddenly started to rain heavily. We realised that we had forgotten to check the weather forecast, and our tent wasn't waterproof. Everything got wet — our clothes, our sleeping bags, even the food. In the end, a family staying nearby invited us into their big tent, and we spent the night playing cards with them. Looking back, it was a disaster, but also one of my best memories. If it hadn't rained, we wouldn't have made new friends.*

## S06

**Task 2 — paraphrase mẫu:**
- *fridge* → *It's a big machine in the kitchen that keeps food cold.*

- *dentist* → *It's a doctor who looks after your teeth.*

**Task 1 & 3:** không có đáp án cố định. Theo dõi số lần dừng > 3 giây; mục tiêu giảm qua ba lần 4-3-2.

## S07

**Part 1 — mẫu (1b):** *What I like most is that it's very convenient. There's a market, a park and a supermarket within walking distance, so I rarely need to use my motorbike.*

**Part 2 — mẫu (3):** *In this situation, I think the best option is walking or cycling to work. First of all, it's free and easy to include in a daily routine, so my friend would be more likely to continue in the long term. Another advantage is that it also reduces stress and pollution. As for joining a gym, it can be effective, but it's quite expensive, and many people stop going after a few weeks. A strict diet isn't ideal either, because it can be unhealthy and difficult to maintain. So, all things considered, walking or cycling to work is the most sustainable choice.*

**Part 3 — dàn ý mẫu (4):**
- **Mở:** I agree that city life has more advantages, especially for young people.
- **Job opportunities:** most companies are in cities; higher salaries; example: my cousin found a job in Ho Chi Minh City within a month.
- **Public services:** better hospitals, universities; example: specialist hospitals are mainly in big cities.
- **Entertainment:** cinemas, concerts, restaurants; never boring.
- **Ý riêng:** cities offer more chances to meet people from different backgrounds, which broadens your horizons.
- **Kết:** although pollution and traffic are serious problems, the benefits outweigh them.
- **Follow-up (a):** traffic, pollution, high cost of living. **(b):** encouraging people with jobs and better infrastructure in rural areas is better than forcing them.
