## Listening 2 — Meeting a new classmate

> **Kim:** Hi! I'm Kim. Are you the new student?
> **Daniel:** Hi, Kim. Yes, I am. I'm Daniel.
> **Kim:** Nice to meet you, Daniel. Where are you from?
> **Daniel:** I'm from Ireland. From Dublin.
> **Kim:** Oh, cool. How old are you?
> **Daniel:** I'm thirty-three. And you?
> **Kim:** I'm twenty-eight. What do you do in Vietnam?
> **Daniel:** I'm a cook. I work in a restaurant near my home. I love Vietnamese food!
> **Kim:** That's great. Do you have any brothers or sisters?
> **Daniel:** Yes, I have two brothers. What about you?
> **Kim:** I don't have any brothers, but I have two sisters.
> **Daniel:** Nice. What do you like doing at the weekend?
> **Kim:** I like swimming. And you?
> **Daniel:** I don't like swimming. I love running. I run every morning.
