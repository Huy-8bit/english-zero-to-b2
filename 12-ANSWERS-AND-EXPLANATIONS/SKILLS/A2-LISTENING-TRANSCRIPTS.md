# A2 Listening Transcripts — Units 1–3

Dùng cho [A2 Skills Practice](../../03-A2-ELEMENTARY/A2-SKILLS-PRACTICE.md). Chưa có audio. Nhờ người đọc hai lần hoặc dùng TTS; chỉ đưa đoạn tiếng Anh bên dưới vào TTS, không đưa tiêu đề và hướng dẫn. Người học chỉ xem sau khi làm bài. Tốc độ rõ, tự nhiên vừa phải; có thể giảm ở vòng luyện sau khi đã ghi kết quả lần đầu.

## Unit 1 — Message from the organiser

> Hello, everyone. This is Anna from the English club. Our next meeting is on Thursday at seven in the evening. We used to meet on Tuesday, but Thursday is better for most people now. Please come to room thirteen, not room thirty. The room is on the first floor, next to the stairs. You have to bring a notebook and a pen, but you don't have to buy a book. We provide all the reading materials. During the speaking activities, you should listen to your partner and give them time to answer. If you don't understand a question, you can say, “Could you repeat that, please?” You mustn't record other students without their permission. After the meeting, we are having tea in the café downstairs. You can join us there. See you on Thursday!

Đọc “thirteen” và “thirty” rõ nhưng không nhấn kiểu báo trước đáp án. Câu *If you don't understand...* là câu điều kiện dùng để hiểu chỉ dẫn; G017 sẽ phân tích cấu trúc sau. Không yêu cầu người học tạo câu điều kiện trong nhiệm vụ này.

## Unit 2 — Message to volunteers

> Hello, this is Ben from the community centre. Thank you for helping with Sunday's book swap. We have changed the location. The event will be in room four, not in the garden, because rain is expected. Visitors can come in at half past ten. Volunteers should arrive at nine forty-five so we have time to prepare. The tables are already in the room, but we need to put labels on them. Each visitor can bring up to five books. We do not accept books with missing pages. If you need help sorting the books, please find Rosa. She is the volunteer who wears a green badge. The event ends at one, and we will spend half an hour cleaning the room afterwards. Please call me if you cannot come.

Đọc giờ rõ, không thêm bình luận tiếng Việt. *Rain is expected* = dự báo sẽ mưa; trong bài chỉ cần hiểu lý do đổi phòng. Tình huống này khác buổi thứ Bảy ở bài đọc Unit 2.

## Unit 3 — Lost property

> Visitor: Excuse me. Could you tell me where the lost-property office is? I left a bag here this morning.  
> Assistant: It is on the ground floor. Can you describe your bag?  
> Visitor: It is a small black bag with a yellow ribbon on the strap. I think I left it under a chair near the café.  
> Assistant: We have a bag like that. It was brought here about an hour ago. There is a notebook and a blue water bottle inside.  
> Visitor: That sounds like mine. Do you know if I can collect it now?  
> Assistant: The office is closed for lunch. Please come back after two and bring your ID. It closes at five today.  
> Visitor: Can I come by myself, or do I need my friend with me?  
> Assistant: You can come by yourself. The staff will ask you to describe the contents before they return it.

*Lost-property office* = quầy đồ thất lạc; *ribbon* = dải ruy băng; *ID* = giấy tờ định danh; *contents* = đồ bên trong. Quy trình là dữ kiện của tình huống giả định, không phải chính sách chung của mọi địa điểm.
