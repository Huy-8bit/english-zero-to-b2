# B2 Skills Practice — Đáp án, mẫu và rubric

[Đề bài](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/B2-SKILLS-PRACTICE.md) · [Transcript](B2-LISTENING-TRANSCRIPTS.md). Câu trả lời nghe/đọc có thể viết tiếng Việt hoặc tiếng Anh; chấm theo nội dung. Bài mẫu chỉ là một phương án.

## Rubric chung cho Speaking và Writing (/10)

| Tiêu chí | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hoàn thành nhiệm vụ** | Lạc đề / thiếu nhiều ý | Đủ ý nhưng phát triển chưa sâu | Đủ ý, mỗi ý có lý do và ví dụ, độ dài đúng |
| **Mạch lạc & liên kết** | Rời rạc | Có cấu trúc, từ nối lặp | Bố cục rõ, liên kết đa dạng (từ nối, quy chiếu) |
| **Ngữ pháp** | Lỗi gây khó hiểu thường xuyên | Có cấu trúc B2 nhưng còn lỗi | Đa dạng cấu trúc B2 yêu cầu, lỗi nhỏ |
| **Từ vựng** | Hạn chế, lặp | Đủ dùng, đôi khi sai collocation | Chính xác, có collocation, văn phong phù hợp |
| **Độ chính xác / phát âm** (Writing: chính tả, dấu câu; Speaking: phát âm, ngữ điệu, độ trôi chảy) | Thường xuyên gây hiểu nhầm | Đôi khi gây khó hiểu | Rõ ràng, tự nhiên |

| U1-R8 | (a) *"**Not only do they need** new skills, but they also need..."*; (b) *"**What seems clear is that** the ability to keep learning..."* (cũng chấp nhận *"What actually happened... was rather different"*). |

### Speaking mẫu

**U1-S1.** *I'd like to talk about accountants. At the moment, accountants spend a lot of time entering data and checking calculations. In the next twenty years, these tasks are likely to be done almost entirely by software. If I had chosen accounting as a career, I would probably be worried now. However, I think the job will become more about advising clients — explaining what the numbers mean and helping businesses make decisions. Overall, I think these changes are positive, provided that accountants are given time to retrain.*

**U1-S2.** Mẫu ý (1): *I'd argue that governments should share the cost, because retraining benefits society as a whole... Admittedly, it's expensive. However...* (chấm theo rubric chung).

### Writing mẫu (U1-W1, ~270 từ)

> *The rapid development of artificial intelligence and robotics has raised an important question: will automation create more jobs than it destroys? Opinions on this issue are sharply divided.*
>
> *On the one hand, optimists point to history. When new technologies appeared in the past, such as computers or the internet, they eliminated some jobs but created entirely new industries. Automation is expected to do the same, generating demand for engineers, data analysts and technicians. Furthermore, by taking over repetitive tasks, machines may allow people to focus on more creative and rewarding work.*
>
> *On the other hand, pessimists argue that this time is different. Modern AI is said to be capable of performing not only physical tasks but also cognitive ones, such as writing reports or analysing legal documents. If this is true, the speed of change could exceed people's ability to adapt, leading to long-term unemployment, particularly among workers with fewer qualifications.*
>
> *In my opinion, both views contain some truth, but the outcome is not predetermined. Admittedly, some jobs will disappear, and the transition is likely to be painful for many workers. However, I believe that, were governments to invest seriously in lifelong learning, most people could move into new roles. What matters most is not the technology itself but how societies prepare for it.*
>
> *In conclusion, automation will probably destroy some jobs and create others. Whether the overall effect is positive will depend largely on education and training policies introduced over the next decade.*

✅ Bị động tường thuật (*is expected to, is said to be*), điều kiện đảo ngữ (*were governments to invest*), nhượng bộ – phản bác, rào đón (*likely, probably, largely*), câu chẻ.

### Speaking mẫu

**U2-S1.** *One of the most serious problems in Ho Chi Minh City is flooding. Caused by heavy rain and high tides, floods can cover streets in minutes. The problem largely results from the fact that many canals have been filled in for construction, which has led to less space for water. One solution would be to build more underground drains; another would be to create green spaces that absorb water. In my view, the second option is more effective in the long run, because it also reduces heat.*

**U2-S2.** Mẫu trả lời Huong: *Thank you, Huong — that's a very fair point, and I admit I didn't address it fully. However, a car-free zone doesn't have to exclude everyone. Many cities allow access for people with disabilities, taxis or small electric shuttles. I'd suggest that any scheme include services like these from the start.*

### Writing mẫu (U2-W1, ~240 từ)

> ***Greener streets, cooler summers: three ideas for our city***
>
> *Have you ever walked down a street in July and felt as if the pavement were cooking you? You're not alone. As temperatures rise, it is time our city took green ideas seriously. Here are three that could genuinely work.*
>
> ***1. Rooftop gardens.** Thousands of flat roofs **sitting** empty above our heads could be turned into gardens. **Planted** with grass and vegetables, they would cool the buildings below and give residents a place to relax.*
>
> ***2. Sponge parks.** Parks **designed** to flood safely during storms could reduce the damage caused by heavy rain. **What makes them so attractive is that** they are useful all year round: playgrounds in the dry season, temporary lakes in the rainy season.*
>
> ***3. Trees on every school street.** Not only would trees give shade to students walking to school, but they would also improve air quality around classrooms. Schools could involve students in planting and caring for them, turning the project into a learning experience.*
>
> *None of these ideas is impossible. Some cities in the region have already introduced similar schemes, and the cost is often lower than people expect.*
>
> *So, what can you do? Start small. Suggest a rooftop garden to your building manager, join a tree-planting day, or simply share this article. A greener city won't appear overnight — but it won't appear at all unless we ask for it.*

✅ Rút gọn (*sitting, planted, designed*), câu chẻ (*What makes them so attractive is that*), đảo ngữ (*Not only would trees...*), văn phong bài báo sinh viên.

| U3-L1 | Tin giả là vấn đề nghiêm trọng và đang ngày càng tệ hơn. |

| U3-L6 | *"I take Ms Ha's point that education is important. But..."* |
| U3-L7 | Trước khi chia sẻ gì đó, hãy dừng lại 10 giây và tự hỏi ba câu hỏi đó. |

| U3-R1 | Mạng xã hội là nguồn tin được dùng nhiều nhất nhưng cũng là nguồn ít được tin tưởng nhất. |
| U3-R2 | Người trẻ tin thông tin do bạn bè/gia đình chia sẻ hơn, kể cả khi không biết nguồn gốc ban đầu — dễ lan truyền thông tin sai. |
| U3-R3 | Những người đã được đào tạo về năng lực truyền thông ở trường. |
| U3-R4 | Thiếu thời gian; không biết cách kiểm tra. |
| U3-R5 | (1) Đưa năng lực truyền thông vào chương trình trung học; (2) nền tảng giúp dễ xác định nguồn gốc bài đăng; (3) các cơ quan báo chí làm bài kiểm chứng ngắn, dễ tiếp cận cho người dùng di động. Khuyến nghị đầu dùng **thức giả định**: *recommends that media literacy **be** integrated*. |
| U3-R6 | Dựa vào hành vi tự báo cáo (có thể không phản ánh hành vi thật); mẫu không bao gồm nông thôn. |
| U3-R7 | **Not Given** — báo cáo chỉ nói mẫu không có nông thôn và mô hình *có thể* khác, không cho biết người nông thôn tin mạng xã hội ít hay nhiều hơn. |
| U3-R8 | Ví dụ: *"Respondents **tended to** trust..."*, *"which **may not** reflect..."*, *"where patterns **could** differ"* — giảm mức độ khẳng định, phù hợp với giới hạn của dữ liệu. |

### Speaking mẫu

**U3-S1 (trích):**
> *A: I firmly believe platforms should be legally responsible. They make huge profits from engagement, and false stories generate engagement. For example, a fake health claim can reach millions of people in a day.*
> *B: I see your point, but I'm not convinced. If platforms were held legally responsible, they would probably delete anything controversial, which could threaten free speech.*
> *A: That's a fair concern. However, laws could target only clearly false and harmful content...*

**U3-S2.** Mẫu: *Last year I saw a post claiming that a famous singer had died. It had thousands of shares. I almost shared it too, but something felt wrong, so I checked a few news websites — none of them mentioned it. It turned out to be a hoax. What I learned is that the more emotional a story is, the more carefully I should check it.*

### Writing mẫu (U3-W1, ~240 từ)

> ***Report: Introducing media literacy at Nguyen Trai Secondary School***
>
> ***Introduction***
> *The aim of this report is to summarise recent findings on young people's news habits and to recommend the introduction of media-literacy lessons at our school.*
>
> ***Findings***
> *According to a recent survey of 1,200 young people in three cities (illustrative data), social media is the main source of news for the majority of respondents. However, it is also the least trusted source. Worryingly, fewer than half of respondents usually verify information before sharing it, often owing to a lack of time or knowledge. Significantly, those who had received media-literacy training were considerably more likely to check stories.*
>
> ***Recommendations***
> *It is therefore recommended that media literacy **be** integrated into the Year 10 curriculum, ideally within existing literature or civic education classes. I would also suggest that teachers **receive** short training courses before the programme begins. In addition, the school could invite local journalists to run workshops.*
>
> ***Conclusion***
> *Although the survey has some limitations, the evidence suggests that training **can** make a real difference to students' behaviour online. Introducing these lessons would **arguably** be a cost-effective way of preparing students for a world in which information is abundant but not always reliable.*

✅ Văn phong báo cáo, giả định (*recommended that... be, suggest that teachers receive*), rào đón (*suggests, can, arguably*), danh từ hóa (*introduction, verification → verify*), tiêu đề mục.
