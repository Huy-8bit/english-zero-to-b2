## Listening 1 — A voicemail message

> Hi Linh, it's Jenny. Sorry, I can't meet you on Thursday because I have to work late. Are you free on Friday instead? We can meet at half past six, at the café opposite the cinema — you know, the one with the green door. The film starts at seven fifteen. Oh, and can you bring my umbrella? I left it at your house last week. Thanks! Call me back. Bye!

## Listening 2 — Daily routines

> **Speaker 1 — Hung:** I'm a baker, so I start work very early. I get up at half past four every morning, even on Sundays. I have a quick breakfast — just bread and an egg — and then I walk to the bakery. It's only five minutes away.
>
> **Speaker 2 — Sophie:** I usually take the train to the office, but this week I'm working at home because my daughter is ill. To be honest, I like it! I don't need to get up early. The first thing I do every day is check my emails — I never read the news in the morning, it's too stressful.
>
> **Speaker 3 — Mr Tanaka:** I'm retired now, so my mornings are slow. I get up at seven and I always have a big breakfast — rice, soup and fish. Then I cycle around the park for an hour. I don't drive any more, so my bike is very important to me.

## Listening 3 — A weekend story

> Last weekend was amazing. On Saturday morning, my husband and I drove to Tam Dao. It's in the mountains, about two hours from Hanoi. We wanted to go by motorbike, but it was too far. The weather was really cold and windy, so I was happy we had our jackets! We stayed in a small guesthouse. In the afternoon, we walked around the town and took a lot of photos. In the evening, we cooked dinner in the guesthouse kitchen — grilled chicken and vegetables. We didn't go to the waterfall because it was too foggy. So we're going to go back next weekend to see it!
