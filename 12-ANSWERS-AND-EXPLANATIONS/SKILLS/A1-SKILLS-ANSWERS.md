### Listening 2 — Daily routines

| Người | Đáp án | Căn cứ |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hung | **E** | "I get up at half past four every morning" — trước 5 giờ. |
| 2. Sophie | **C** | "this week I'm working at home" |
| 3. Mr Tanaka | **B** | "Then I cycle around the park for an hour." |

**Hai phương án gây nhiễu:**
- **A** (*never eats breakfast*): Hung ăn sáng nhanh, Mr Tanaka ăn sáng nhiều — không ai "không bao giờ ăn sáng".
- **D** (*always reads the news first*): Sophie nói việc đầu tiên là **check emails** và "I **never** read the news in the morning". Đây là bẫy dùng từ khóa *news* và *first thing*.

> 💡 Bài học về kỹ năng: đừng chọn đáp án chỉ vì nghe thấy **từ khóa** (*news*); hãy nghe cả câu để biết người nói khẳng định hay phủ định. Xem [L02 — Listening for Keywords](../../09-FOUR-SKILLS/Listening/L02-listening-for-keywords.md).

### Reading 3 — Find the grammar

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ trong email |
|---|---|
| Present continuous | *I'm sitting... / I'm looking at the sea* |
| Past simple (irregular) | *went, swam, saw, had, was* |
| Comparative | *more comfortable than the bus* |
| Superlative | *the best seafood I've ever eaten* |
| *be going to* | *we're going to visit / we're going to try* |
| *will* | *I'll send you some photos later* |

(*We're flying back home on Saturday* là hiện tại tiếp diễn chỉ kế hoạch — học ở [A2-G008](../../03-A2-ELEMENTARY/grammar/A2-G008-present-continuous-future.md).)

## Part 3 — Speaking (câu trả lời mẫu)

1. *On weekday evenings, I usually cook dinner and watch an episode of a TV series. Sometimes I go to the gym.*
2. *These days I'm taking an online English course, so I'm studying for an hour every evening.*
3. *Last weekend, I visited my cousin in Bien Hoa. We went to a new shopping mall and had hotpot for dinner.*
4. *Next weekend, I'm going to clean my flat on Saturday, and on Sunday I'm going to have lunch with my parents.*
5. *The most interesting place in my town is the old market. It's busier than the supermarket, but the food is cheaper and fresher.*
6. *Yes, I can. I can make fried rice and spring rolls, but I can't make cakes.*

**Speaking 3 — mẫu "My last holiday" (~1,5 phút):**

> *My last holiday was in Hoi An in April. I went with three friends from university. We took a night bus from Hanoi — it was long but cheap. We stayed in a small homestay near the river. During the day, we rode bicycles around the old town and to An Bang beach. In the evening, we walked by the river and bought lanterns. The best moment was when we released flower candles on the river — it was so beautiful. The worst moment was the bus journey home, because I couldn't sleep at all! Next year, we're going to visit Ha Giang.*

## Part 4 — Writing

### Writing 1 — Fix the paragraph (8 lỗi)

| # | Lỗi | Sửa | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *I am work* | **I work** | Không ghép *be* + V |
| 2 | *I gets up* | **I get up** | *I* không thêm *-s* |
| 3 | *this week I take the bus* | **this week I'm taking the bus** | Tạm thời → tiếp diễn |
| 4 | *in the fifth floor* | **on the fifth floor** | Tầng → *on* |
| 5 | *she help me* | **she helps me** | Ngôi 3 → *-s* |
| 6 | *In the weekend* | **At the weekend** (Anh) / **On the weekend** (Mỹ) | Giới từ thời gian |
| 7 | *I like go shopping* | **I like going shopping** | *like* + V-ing |
| 8 | *more young than* | **younger than** | Tính từ ngắn → *-er* |

**Đoạn văn đã sửa:**

> *My name is Phuong and I work in a bank in Hai Phong. Every morning I get up at 6 o'clock. I usually go to work by motorbike, but this week I'm taking the bus because my motorbike is broken. My office is on the fifth floor. My boss is very kind and she helps me a lot. At the weekend, I like going shopping with my sister. She is younger than me.*

### Writing 2 — Message (mẫu ~50 từ)

> *Hi Minh, I'm really sorry, but I can't come to dinner tonight. My boss asked me to finish an important report, so I'm going to work late. Can we meet on Thursday or Friday instead? I'll book a table at that new Thai restaurant. Sorry again! — Lan*

✅ Có: lý do, lời xin lỗi (*I'm really sorry / Sorry again*), gợi ý thời gian khác; 40–60 từ.

### Writing 3 — Holiday email (mẫu ~100 từ)

> *Hi Jess,*
>
> *How are you? I just came back from a wonderful holiday in Da Lat with my parents. We stayed in a small hotel near Xuan Huong Lake. The weather was cooler and fresher than in Saigon, so we walked a lot. We visited flower gardens, drank hot soy milk at the night market and ate the best strawberry cake I've ever tried! My favourite part was a bike ride around the lake early in the morning. Next month, I'm going to visit Hue with my cousins. I'll send you some photos soon!*
>
> *Best wishes,*
> *Nhu*

✅ ≥ 5 động từ quá khứ (*came, stayed, was, walked, visited, drank, ate*); 1 so sánh (*cooler and fresher than*); 1 *be going to*; mở/kết email đúng.
