# B2-G012 · Answer Key — Future Perfect Continuous & Future in the Past

> Bài học: [B2-G012](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G012-future-perfect-continuous.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. will have been living** | *by + for* → thời lượng. |
| L1.2 | **A. will have finished** | Số lượng hoàn thành → *will have done*. |
| L1.3 | **A. was going to** | Dự định không thành. |
| L1.4 | **B. about to** | *be about to + V*. |
| L1.5 | **B. would** | Tường thuật: *will → would*. |
| L1.6 | **B. to have taken place** | Kế hoạch chính thức không thành. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | ... she will **have been** teaching for 15 years. |
| L3.2 | By the time we **get** there, ... |
| L3.3 | I was going to **visit** ... |
| L3.4 | By 2027, I will **have owned** this car for ten years. (*own* — động từ trạng thái) |
| L3.5 | We were about **to leave** ... |
| L3.6 | He said that he **would** come, but he didn't. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **By this December, I will have been working at this company for five years.** |
| L4.2 | **I was going to tell you the truth, but I was scared / afraid.** |
| L4.3 | **We were about to go out when it started to pour (with rain).** |
| L4.4 | **The concert was to have taken place on Saturday, but it was postponed because of the storm.** |

**L4.5.** Câu 1 (*will have written*) nhấn mạnh **kết quả**: đến tháng 6 luận văn sẽ **hoàn thành**. Câu 2 (*will have been writing ... for a year*) nhấn mạnh **thời lượng/quá trình**: đến tháng 6 tôi đã viết được **một năm** — không cho biết đã xong hay chưa.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~140 từ):

> *This year didn't go exactly as planned. In January, I **was going to** join a gym, but I never found the time. In the summer, my family **was going to** travel to Thailand, but my father got ill, so we stayed home. I knew he **would** recover quickly, and fortunately he did.*
>
> *In September, I **was about to** sign up for an expensive English course when a friend recommended a free online programme instead. It turned out to be excellent.*
>
> *Next year, I'm going to be more realistic. By the end of 2027, I **will have been studying** English seriously for three years, and I hope to take the B2 exam. By then, I **will have been working** at my company for five years, so it might be time for a new challenge.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 *was going to* · *was about to* · *would* (quá khứ) · ≥ 2 FPC · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *By the end of this year, I will have been learning English for eight years. By the time I'm 30, I will have been working as an engineer for about seven years. And by 2030, my wife and I will have been living in our new flat for three years.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *S + will have been + V-ing*.
2. *will have done* — kết quả/số lượng hoàn thành; *will have been doing* — thời lượng/quá trình.
3. Dự định trong quá khứ, thường không thực hiện được.
4. *was to + V* — kế hoạch/số phận (thường đã xảy ra); *was to have + V3* — kế hoạch chính thức không xảy ra.
5. Động từ trạng thái không dùng ở dạng tiếp diễn; dùng *will have known/owned* thay thế.
