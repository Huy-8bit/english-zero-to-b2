# B2-G023 · Answer Key — Ellipsis & Substitution

> Bài học: [B2-G023](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G023-ellipsis-substitution.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. not** | *hope not*. |
| L1.2 | **B. one** | *shirt* số ít. |
| L1.3 | **C. so** | *afraid so*. |
| L1.4 | **B. to** | Tỉnh lược sau *to*. |
| L1.5 | **A. so** | *do so* — văn trang trọng (biển báo). |
| L1.6 | **B. does** | *neither + trợ động từ + S*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | Do you have any sweeter **ones**? |
| L2.2 | "I think **so**." |
| L2.3 | "I hope **not**." |
| L2.4 | ... but my sister **has**. |
| L2.5 | "Not really, but I have **to**." |
| L2.6 | ..., and my uncle **did the same**. |
| L2.7 | If **they do so**, they must keep the volume low. |
| L2.8 | **If so**, let's meet for coffee. |
| L2.9 | "Nam **did**." |
| L2.10 | Would you like **one** too? |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | "I **hope not**." |
| L3.2 | "I think **so**." |
| L3.3 | My old **one** is too slow. |
| L3.4 | I prefer hot water to **cold**. |
| L3.5 | "I'd like **to**, but I can't." |
| L3.6 | "Yes, I **did**." |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **I think so.** (hoặc *I expect so / I suppose so*) |
| L4.2 | **No, but I'd like to / I'd love to.** |
| L4.3 | **I'm afraid not.** |
| L4.4 | **The white one, please.** |

**L4.5.** Với *think, believe, expect*, tiếng Anh cho phép **phủ định chuyển** lên động từ chính (*I don't think so*). *hope* không theo quy tắc này — phủ định phải đặt ở phần thay thế: *I hope **not***.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~160 từ):

> *A: Are we still having the barbecue on Saturday?*
> *B: I **think so**, unless it rains. **If so**, we'll move it to my flat.*
> *A: Will Linh come?*
> *B: She said she'd **like to**, but she might have to work.*
> *A: I **hope not**. She makes the best sauce.*
> *B: I know! Should we buy a new grill? The old **one** is broken.*
> *A: I don't **think so**. My cousin has two good **ones** — I'll borrow one.*
> *B: Great. Can you bring some chairs too?*
> *A: I can't, but Tuan **can**. He has a car.*
> *B: Perfect. Has everyone replied yet?*
> *A: Most people **have**, but Nam **hasn't**. Anyway, I'll buy the drinks — last year Nam bought them all, so this year I'll **do the same**.*
> *B: Do you want me to send the invitations?*
> *A: Yes, if you have time **to**.*
> *B: Sure. And if anyone wants to bring a friend, they can **do so**.*
> *A: Will it be expensive?*
> *B: **I'm afraid so**, but it'll be worth it!*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 *one/ones* · ≥ 3 *so/not* · *do so/do the same* · ≥ 3 tỉnh lược sau trợ động từ, ≥ 2 sau *to* · *If so/If not*.

**L5.2.** Mẫu câu trả lời: *I do. / No, but I'd like to. / I hope not. / I think so. / I'm afraid so. / I have. / I can't, but my brother can. / I will. / I'd love to. / I don't think so.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Thay thế: dùng từ khác thay cho từ đã nói (*one, so, do so*); tỉnh lược: bỏ hẳn phần có thể hiểu từ ngữ cảnh.
2. *I hope not*; *I don't think so*.
3. Không — chỉ thay danh từ đếm được.
4. Trang trọng.
5. Giữ *to*, bỏ động từ phía sau.
