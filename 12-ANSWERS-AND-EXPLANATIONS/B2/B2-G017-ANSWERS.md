# B2-G017 · Answer Key — Advanced Linking Devices

> Bài học: [B2-G017](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G017-advanced-linking-devices.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. such as** | Ngay sau danh từ *fruits*, + danh sách. |
| L1.2 | **B. In addition to** | + V-ing. |
| L1.3 | **A. namely** | Liệt kê đầy đủ (đúng 2 thành phố). |
| L1.4 | **A. The former / the latter** | Trà (trước) lành mạnh hơn; cà phê (sau) nhiều caffeine hơn. |
| L1.5 | **A. on the whole** | Khái quát sau khi xét các mặt. |
| L1.6 | **A. This situation** | Danh từ tóm tắt. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **For example,** many people prefer online shopping. |
| L3.2 | In addition to **being** cheap, ... |
| L3.3 | **Besides,** the restaurant was too expensive. |
| L3.4 | I have three brothers. **The youngest / The last one I mentioned** is a pilot. (*latter* chỉ cho 2) |
| L3.5 | The food was delicious. **Furthermore / What's more,** it was also cheap. |
| L3.6 | **Climate change, in particular,** is the biggest challenge. (hoặc *There is one major challenge, namely climate change.*) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Living in the city centre is very expensive, not to mention noisy.** |
| L4.2 | **Sales have fallen by 40%. In other words, the company is in serious trouble.** |
| L4.3 | **I have two options: doing a master's degree or getting a job. The former takes more time, while the latter provides an immediate income.** |
| L4.4 | **Many students work full-time. This trend affects their academic performance.** |

**L4.5.** Tiêu chí liên kết đánh giá **độ đa dạng, chính xác và tự nhiên** của phương tiện liên kết. Lặp *Moreover* cho thấy vốn từ hạn chế và thường dùng sai chức năng (không phải câu nào cũng là "bổ sung"), khiến bài viết máy móc. Nên dùng nhiều loại: từ nối khác nhau, quy chiếu (*this + N*), hoặc nối bằng cấu trúc câu.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~190 từ):

> *Tourism has transformed many communities in Vietnam, **particularly** coastal towns and mountain villages. **To begin with**, it creates jobs. Local people can work in hotels, restaurants and tour companies, **not to mention** selling handicrafts. **In addition to** providing income, tourism encourages governments to improve infrastructure **such as** roads and airports.*
>
> ***Nevertheless**, these benefits come at a cost. In popular destinations, land prices have risen sharply; **in other words**, many young locals can no longer afford to buy homes in their own towns. **This problem** is especially serious in places like Hoi An and Sa Pa. **Furthermore**, traditional customs are sometimes turned into performances for visitors. **Such changes** may weaken local culture over time.*
>
> *Communities therefore face two options, **namely** limiting visitor numbers or spreading tourism to less popular areas. **The former** protects existing sites, while **the latter** shares the economic benefits more widely. **On the whole**, tourism can be positive, provided that local people have a voice in how it develops.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 8 phương tiện, ≥ 5 chức năng · ≥ 2 quy chiếu · không lặp · đúng cấu trúc ngữ pháp sau từ nối · 160–200 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu ý: *To begin with, electric cars produce no exhaust fumes. What's more, they're cheaper to run. Older drivers, in particular, like how quiet they are. However, charging stations are still rare outside big cities. In other words, long trips can be difficult. By and large, though, I think they're the future. Overall, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *such as* đứng ngay sau danh từ, + danh sách N; *for example* linh hoạt (đầu câu, giữa câu), + câu hoàn chỉnh.
2. *namely* = cụ thể là, liệt kê đầy đủ; *such as* = như là, chỉ một vài ví dụ.
3. Khi nhắc lại **hai** người/vật đã nêu trước đó.
4. *situation, problem, issue, trend, approach* (cũng chấp nhận *development, change, measure, factor, view*).
5. Danh từ hoặc V-ing.
