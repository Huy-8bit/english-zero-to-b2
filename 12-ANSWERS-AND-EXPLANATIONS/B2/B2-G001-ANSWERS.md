# B2-G001 · Answer Key — Mixed Conditionals

> Bài học: [B2-G001](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G001-mixed-conditionals.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A** | Điều kiện quá khứ (*had saved*) → kết quả hiện tại (*would own ... now*). |
| L1.2 | **2** | Giả định hiện tại → lời khuyên hiện tại. |
| L1.3 | **B** | Đặc điểm hiện tại (*weren't so busy*) → sự kiện quá khứ (*would have come*). |
| L1.4 | **3** | Cả hai vế đều quá khứ. |
| L1.5 | **B. would be** | *right now* → kết quả hiện tại. A dùng cho quá khứ; C là loại 1. |
| L1.6 | **B. weren't** | Tính cách lâu dài (lười) → type B. A cũng có thể chấp nhận nếu người nói chỉ muốn nói về sự lười **trong thời gian làm dự án** (thành loại 3) — nhưng *weren't* thể hiện đặc điểm chung, phù hợp nhất với ngữ cảnh. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **had accepted** / **would live** (hoặc *would be living*) |
| L2.2 | **were** / **wouldn't have missed** |
| L2.3 | **wouldn't be** / **had brought** |
| L2.4 | **weren't** / **would have adopted** |
| L2.5 | **had gone** / **wouldn't feel** |
| L2.6 | **If I had studied law, I would be a lawyer now.** |
| L2.7 | **If she spoke English well, she would have got the job.** |
| L2.8 | **If they hadn't missed the flight, they would be in Bangkok now.** |
| L2.9 | **If I were good at singing, I would have joined the choir.** |
| L2.10 | **If he had taken the medicine, he wouldn't feel ill now / he would feel better now.** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | ... I **would be** rich now. | *now* → kết quả hiện tại. |
| L3.2 | If I **had listened** to you, ... | Không *would* trong mệnh đề *if*. |
| L3.3 | If he **were** a better driver, ... | Giả định không có thật. |
| L3.4 | If she **hadn't lost** her keys yesterday, ... | Điều kiện quá khứ cụ thể. |
| L3.5 | ... we **would be** millionaires today. | *today* → hiện tại. |
| L3.6 | If I **weren't** so tired, I **would have gone** to the party last night. (hoặc *If I hadn't been so tired last night, I would have gone* — loại 3) | *last night* → kết quả quá khứ. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **If I had studied English seriously at university, I would have a better job now.** |
| L4.2 | **If he weren't so shy, he would have told her how he felt last year.** (*confessed his feelings to her* cũng đúng) |
| L4.3 | **If we hadn't got lost, we would be having dinner at the restaurant now.** |
| L4.4 | Mẫu: *If I had moved to Ho Chi Minh City after graduating, I would earn more now.* (A) / *If I were more patient, I would have finished that online course.* (B) |

**L4.5.** Vì kết quả (*sống ở Singapore*) là tình trạng **hiện tại** (*now*), không phải sự việc trong quá khứ. Chỉ điều kiện (*nhận việc*) nằm ở quá khứ, nên mệnh đề *if* dùng *had V3* còn mệnh đề chính dùng *would + V*.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~165 từ):

> *The biggest turning point in my life was failing my university entrance exam in 2015. At the time I was devastated. I had wanted to study architecture, but I missed the required score by just one point. If I had passed, I would be an architect now, and I would probably live in Ho Chi Minh City.*
>
> *Instead, I spent a year working in a hotel in Hoi An. That year changed everything. I discovered that I loved working with tourists. If I hadn't failed the exam, I wouldn't know anything about the hospitality industry, and I certainly wouldn't be managing my own guesthouse today.*
>
> *To be honest, if I were a more confident person, I would have tried the exam again the following year. But I didn't, and I'm glad. If I had become an architect, I might have been successful — but I doubt I would be as happy as I am now.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 type A · ≥ 1 type B · ≥ 1 loại 3 · trình tự câu chuyện rõ · 150–180 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu ý: *If Steve Jobs hadn't returned to Apple in 1997, we might not have smartphones like the ones we use today... If he hadn't dropped out of college, he might not have taken that calligraphy class, so computers wouldn't have such beautiful fonts now...* (Chấp nhận nhân vật bất kỳ; chấm cấu trúc, không chấm độ chính xác lịch sử ngoài các sự kiện phổ biến.)

## Knowledge Check

1. Type A: *If + had V3, would + V*; Type B: *If + V2/were, would have + V3*.
2. Khi điều kiện ở quá khứ nhưng kết quả ở hiện tại.
3. *now, today, at the moment, still*, hoặc ngữ cảnh nói về tình trạng hiện tại.
4. Mệnh đề *if* diễn tả điều kiện; *would* chỉ dùng ở mệnh đề kết quả (trừ một số trường hợp đặc biệt như lời yêu cầu lịch sự *If you would follow me...*, không thuộc phạm vi bài này).
5. *If I weren't so impatient, I wouldn't have argued with my boss yesterday.*
