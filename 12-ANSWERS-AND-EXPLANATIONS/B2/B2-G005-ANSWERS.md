# B2-G005 · Answer Key — Passive Reporting Structures

> Bài học: [B2-G005](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G005-passive-reporting.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. to live** | Thói quen/trạng thái hiện tại. |
| L1.2 | **B. to have sunk** | Sự việc năm 1880 xảy ra trước thời điểm "nghĩ". |
| L1.3 | **A. It is** | It-passive + that. |
| L1.4 | **B. to be leaving** | *at the moment* → đang diễn ra. |
| L1.5 | **B. to have been built** | Bị động + xảy ra trước. |
| L1.6 | **A. are thought** | *over 100 people* — số nhiều. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **It is said that the old man is over 100 years old.** / **The old man is said to be over 100 years old.** |
| L2.2 | **It is believed that the company is planning a merger.** / **The company is believed to be planning a merger.** |
| L2.3 | **It is thought (by experts) that the fire started in the kitchen.** / **The fire is thought to have started in the kitchen.** |
| L2.4 | **It was reported that the singer had cancelled her tour.** / **The singer was reported to have cancelled her tour.** |
| L2.5 | **It is expected that the new airport will open in 2028.** / **The new airport is expected to open in 2028.** |
| L2.6 | She **is known to speak six languages**. |
| L2.7 | He **is considered to have been the best player of his generation**. (*was* — quá khứ — xảy ra trước *consider* — hiện tại → *to have been*) |
| L2.8 | The painting **is thought to have been stolen by an insider**. |
| L2.9 | There **are said to be wolves in these mountains**. |
| L2.10 | The minister **is reported to be resigning**. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **It is said that** he is very generous. / He is said **to be** very generous. |
| L3.2 | ... to have **been** built by the French. |
| L3.3 | The fire is thought **to have started** at midnight last night. |
| L3.4 | **He is said to be** a talented musician. |
| L3.5 | There is said to **be** a ghost in the castle. |
| L3.6 | The police are believed **to be searching** the area now. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **The One Pillar Pagoda is believed / said to have been built in 1049.** |
| L4.2 | **It is estimated that there are about 8 million motorbikes in Ho Chi Minh City.** |
| L4.3 | **This scientist is known to have won many awards.** |
| L4.4 | **The two sides are believed / thought / reported to be negotiating a new agreement.** |

**L4.5.** Con tàu chìm năm 1880 — **trước** thời điểm "người ta nghĩ" (hiện tại). Khi sự việc xảy ra trước động từ tường thuật, phải dùng nguyên mẫu hoàn thành *to have + V3*. *to sink* sẽ chỉ sự việc cùng lúc/tương lai, không hợp với mốc 1880.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~135 từ):

> ***Ancient coins found in Quang Nam***
>
> *A collection of more than 200 coins has been discovered by farmers near Hoi An. The coins **are believed to have been buried** in the 17th century, when the town was an important trading port. **It is thought that** they belonged to a wealthy merchant family.*
>
> *Experts **are reported to be examining** the coins at a local museum. Some of them **are said to come** from Japan and China, which shows how international the port was. **It is expected that** the findings will be published next year.*
>
> *The farmers **are understood to have contacted** the authorities immediately after the discovery. The site **is now considered to be** of great historical importance.*

> ℹ️ Bản tin giả định để luyện tập.

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 It-passive · ≥ 4 Subject-passive · ≥ 3 dạng nguyên mẫu khác nhau · văn phong bản tin · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *In my hometown there's a lake called Ho Tien. It is said that a fairy once came down to bathe there. The lake is believed to have been formed more than a thousand years ago. There is said to be a golden turtle living at the bottom...*

## Knowledge Check

1. *It + be + V3 + that + mệnh đề*; *S + be + V3 + to-infinitive*.
2. Khi sự việc xảy ra trước thời điểm của động từ tường thuật.
3. Khi sự việc đang diễn ra tại thời điểm tường thuật.
4. *There is/are + V3 + to be...* (hoặc *to have been* nếu trước đó).
5. Giúp đưa chủ thể chính lên đầu câu và giữ khoảng cách với thông tin chưa được xác minh (không khẳng định là sự thật, không cần nêu nguồn cụ thể).
