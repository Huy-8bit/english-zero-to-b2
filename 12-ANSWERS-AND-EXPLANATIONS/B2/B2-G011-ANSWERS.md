# B2-G011 · Answer Key — Advanced Modal Verbs

> Bài học: [B2-G011](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G011-advanced-modals.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. needn't have bought** | Đã mua, nhưng thừa. |
| L1.2 | **B. didn't need to** | Không cần học, và đã không học (đi xem phim). |
| L1.3 | **B. were able to** | Thành công trong một tình huống cụ thể. |
| L1.4 | **A. were supposed to** | Lẽ ra phải (theo thỏa thuận). |
| L1.5 | **A. would** | Thói quen (hành động) trong quá khứ. B sai nghĩa (*be used to* = quen với). |
| L1.6 | **A. bound to** | Chắc chắn sẽ. |

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | You **don't need to / needn't** come tomorrow. | Việc chưa xảy ra. |
| L3.2 | ... all the passengers **were able to / managed to** swim to the shore. | Thành công cụ thể. |
| L3.3 | When I was young, I **used to have** a dog called Lucky. (hoặc *I had*) | Trạng thái sở hữu. |
| L3.4 | We **are supposed to** hand in ... | |
| L3.5 | She's bound to **pass** the exam. | |
| L3.6 | We might as well **wait** for the next one. | |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **You needn't have bought flowers — I already have lots.** |
| L4.2 | **Although he was injured, he managed to finish the race.** (*Despite being injured* cũng đúng) |
| L4.3 | **I was supposed to send the report yesterday, but I forgot.** |
| L4.4 | **When I was little, my mother would sing to me before I went to sleep.** |

**L4.5.** *I needn't have waited* — tôi **đã chờ**, nhưng hóa ra việc chờ là không cần thiết (thừa). *I didn't need to wait* — việc chờ không cần thiết; thường hiểu là tôi **đã không chờ** (hoặc câu không nói rõ).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ):

> *Last month my friends and I planned a camping trip to Ba Vi. When we were students, we **would** go camping almost every month, so we thought it would be easy.*
>
> *We **were supposed to** leave at 6 a.m., but Minh overslept. I had packed three kinds of medicine, but I **needn't have bothered** — nobody got sick. We **didn't need to** bring a tent either, because the campsite had cabins.*
>
> *The real problem was the weather. It started to rain heavily, and the road became very muddy. Fortunately, we **managed to** reach the campsite before dark. By then everyone was so tired that we decided we **might as well** skip the campfire and go to sleep.*
>
> *Next time, we're **bound to** forget something else — but that's part of the fun.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đủ cấu trúc yêu cầu · đúng nghĩa từng modal · ngữ pháp sau modal đúng · câu chuyện mạch lạc · 130–160 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *When I was a kid, I used to live in a small town. Every afternoon, my friends and I would ride our bikes to the river. We would catch fish and we'd often come home covered in mud. I used to be very shy, but outdoors I felt free. Nowadays, children are likely to spend more time on screens, and they're bound to have different memories from mine.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *needn't have done* — đã làm nhưng không cần; *didn't need to do* — không cần làm (thường là không làm).
2. Khi nói về một lần cụ thể thành công → dùng *was able to / managed to* (trừ động từ tri giác như *see, hear*).
3. *be bound to* = chắc chắn sẽ; *be supposed to* = lẽ ra phải (theo quy định/kỳ vọng); *be due to* = dự kiến theo lịch.
4. *would* chỉ cho hành động lặp lại, không cho trạng thái; *used to* cho cả hai.
5. Khi không có lựa chọn nào tốt hơn ("thôi thì cứ...").
