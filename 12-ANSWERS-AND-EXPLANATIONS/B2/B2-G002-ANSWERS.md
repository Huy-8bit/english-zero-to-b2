# B2-G002 · Answer Key — Advanced Conditional Structures

> Bài học: [B2-G002](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G002-advanced-conditionals.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. Should** | Đảo loại 1 trong văn trang trọng. |
| L1.2 | **B. Had** | Đảo loại 3; A thiếu *had*; C dùng cho loại 2. |
| L1.3 | **B. But for** | + danh từ *your advice*. |
| L1.4 | **B. otherwise** | "nếu không thì". *unless* cần mệnh đề sau; *without* cần danh từ. |
| L1.5 | **A. Had it** | *Had it not been for*. B rút gọn sai; C thiếu *had*: *If it had not been for*. |
| L1.6 | **A. Supposing** | Giả định + V2. *Should* không đi với *were offered* ở dạng này. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **Should you need any help, just ask.** |
| L2.2 | **Were I the manager, I would change the rules.** |
| L2.3 | **Had they arrived earlier, they would have seen the show.** |
| L2.4 | **Had she not been so tired, she would have gone out.** |
| L2.5 | **Were the company to decide to close the factory, 500 people would lose their jobs.** (chấp nhận *Were the company to close...*) |
| L2.6 | **But for the firefighters, the whole building would have burned down.** |
| L2.7 | **Without my sister, I would feel lonely.** |
| L2.8 | **I didn't know you were in town; otherwise I would have called you.** |
| L2.9 | **Had it not been for the scholarship, I couldn't have studied abroad.** |
| L2.10 | **Supposing you lost your passport abroad, what would you do?** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **Had she not** helped me, ... |
| L3.2 | Should you **need** assistance, ... |
| L3.3 | But for **his arrival on time / his punctuality**, ... (hoặc *If he hadn't arrived on time, ...*) |
| L3.4 | **Were I to know** the answer, ... / **If I knew** the answer, ... |
| L3.5 | If it **hadn't been** for his help last year, ... |
| L3.6 | Study hard, otherwise you**'ll** fail the exam. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Should you have any questions, please contact our customer service department.** |
| L4.2 | **Had I known (that) the price was so high, I wouldn't have booked the room.** |
| L4.3 | **But for my colleagues' support, the project would have failed.** |
| L4.4 | **I had to work overtime; otherwise I would have gone to the cinema with you.** |

**L4.5.** Trong đảo ngữ điều kiện, *not* không được rút gọn và gắn vào trợ động từ đứng đầu câu (vì sẽ biến câu thành câu hỏi phủ định *Hadn't I known...?*). Phải giữ *not* sau chủ ngữ: *Had I not known...*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~135 từ):

> *Dear Ms Pham,*
>
> *I am writing to inform you that, due to a shortage of materials, the delivery of your order (No. 4521) will be delayed by approximately one week. The new delivery date is 18 November.*
>
> *Had we received the materials from our supplier on time, we would have delivered your order as planned. We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.*
>
> *Should this delay cause serious problems for your business, please let us know immediately. Were you to cancel the order, we would, of course, provide a full refund.*
>
> *Without the support of loyal customers like you, our company would not be where it is today. We will include a 5% discount on your next order; otherwise, we will contact you to discuss other compensation.*
>
> *Yours sincerely,*
> *Le Van Nam*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 đảo ngữ đúng · *otherwise* đúng thì · *but for/without/if it weren't for* đúng · văn phong trang trọng · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *Supposing I could change one decision, I'd choose to study abroad after high school. If I had gone to Australia, I would speak English much more fluently now... What if I had never come back? ... But honestly, if it hadn't been for my decision to stay, I wouldn't have met my best friends.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *Should* (loại 1), *Were* (loại 2), *Had* (loại 3).
2. *Had + S + not + V3*: *Had I not known...*
3. Danh từ / cụm danh từ.
4. *If it weren't for* — hiện tại; *If it hadn't been for* — quá khứ.
5. *would have + V3*.
