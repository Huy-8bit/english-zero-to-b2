# B2-G019 · Answer Key — Purpose, Result & Reason

> Bài học: [B2-G019](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G019-purpose-result-reason.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. owing to** | + danh từ *fog*. |
| L1.2 | **B. working** | *with a view to* + V-ing. |
| L1.3 | **B. Now that** | + mệnh đề, lý do mới. |
| L1.4 | **B. result in** | Thiếu ngủ → dẫn đến kém tập trung. |
| L1.5 | **A. thereby** | + V-ing. |
| L1.6 | **B. upsetting** | *for fear of* + V-ing. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **The match was cancelled owing to (the) heavy rain.** |
| L2.2 | **Given that he has no experience, he shouldn't expect a high salary.** |
| L2.3 | **She took notes so as not to forget anything.** |
| L2.4 | **He didn't ask questions for fear of looking stupid.** |
| L2.5 | **They are building a new road with a view to reducing traffic in the centre.** |
| L2.6 | **The factory closed, resulting in 300 people losing their jobs.** (hoặc *resulting in the loss of 300 jobs*) |
| L2.7 | **Many health problems stem from stress.** |
| L2.8 | **Prices went up. Consequently, people bought less.** |
| L2.9 | **Now that I live near the beach, I swim every morning.** |
| L2.10 | **The bridge is closed, which means that we'll have to take the ferry.** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **Owing to the snow**, ... / **As it was snowing**, ... |
| L3.2 | ... with a view to **buying** a car. |
| L3.3 | Pollution **results in** many deaths each year. (hoặc *Many deaths result from pollution*) |
| L3.4 | The government raised taxes, thereby **causing people to spend** less. |
| L3.5 | ... for fear of **missing** the bus. |
| L3.6 | Now that I **have finished / am finishing** university, ... (hoặc *Once I finish university, ...* nếu chưa xong) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Owing to the pandemic, many small businesses had to close.** |
| L4.2 | **She is taking extra Korean classes with a view to working for a Korean company.** |
| L4.3 | **Excessive use of social media can lead to anxiety.** |
| L4.4 | **The library is closed on Sundays, which means that students have to study at home.** |

**L4.5.** *result in* = **dẫn đến** (nguyên nhân đứng trước): *Poor diet results in illness.* *result from* = **là kết quả của** (kết quả đứng trước): *Illness results from poor diet.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~200 từ):

> *In recent years, a growing number of young Vietnamese people have chosen to live alone. This trend **stems from** several social and economic changes.*
>
> *Firstly, **owing to** higher incomes, many young professionals can now afford to rent their own flats. Secondly, **given that** more people move to big cities for work, they often live far from their families. **Now that** attitudes towards independence are changing, living alone is no longer seen as unusual. Some young people also move out **with a view to** developing their own routines and identities.*
>
> *This trend **has led to** several effects. On the positive side, young adults become more independent and learn practical skills such as cooking and budgeting. On the other hand, some feel lonely, **which means that** they may spend more time online. Rental demand has also increased, **resulting in** higher rents in central districts. **Consequently**, some people end up spending a large part of their salary on housing.*
>
> *Overall, living alone **brings about** both opportunities and challenges, and its impact depends largely on individual circumstances.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 lý do · ≥ 3 kết quả · ≥ 2 động từ nhân quả · mục đích nâng cao · 180–220 từ, bố cục rõ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *House prices have risen sharply, mainly owing to land speculation and rapid urbanisation. This has led to a situation where young couples can't afford to buy homes, which means that they have to rent for longer. Consequently, many delay having children...*

## Knowledge Check

1. Danh từ hoặc V-ing.
2. *owing to* + N/V-ing; *given that* + mệnh đề.
3. *result in* = dẫn đến; *result from* = bắt nguồn từ.
4. V-ing.
5. *cause, lead to, result in, bring about* (cũng chấp nhận *give rise to, contribute to*).
