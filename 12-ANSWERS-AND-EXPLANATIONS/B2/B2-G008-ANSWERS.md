## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | Walking along the beach, **we watched / I saw a beautiful sunset**. (hoặc *While we were walking..., the sunset was beautiful.*) |
| L3.2 | **Written** by a famous author, ... |
| L3.3 | **Having eaten** dinner, we watched a film. |
| L3.4 | Not **understanding** the question, ... |
| L3.5 | **Having finished** the exam, the students were allowed to leave. |
| L3.6 | **As it was** a holiday, all the shops were closed. (hoặc *It being a holiday,* — trang trọng) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Having lived in Hanoi for ten years, he knows the city well.** |
| L4.2 | **Surrounded by rice fields, the village looks like a painting.** |
| L4.3 | **Not wanting to disturb anyone, she left quietly.** |
| L4.4 | **When asked about her future plans, she just smiled.** |

**L4.5.** Phân từ *Walking into the room* có chủ ngữ ngầm là chủ ngữ mệnh đề chính — *the lights* — nghĩa là "đèn đi vào phòng", vô lý. Sửa: *Walking into the room, I noticed that the lights went out.* / *As I walked into the room, the lights went out.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~170 từ):

> ***The Letter***
>
> ***Having lived** in Saigon all her life, Mai had never visited her grandmother's village in the north. One summer, **encouraged** by her mother, she finally bought a train ticket.*
>
> ***Arriving** in the village late at night, she was greeted by an elderly neighbour **holding** a torch. **Exhausted** after the long journey, Mai fell asleep immediately.*
>
> *The next morning, **while exploring** the old house, she found a wooden box **hidden** under the stairs. **Opening** it carefully, she discovered dozens of letters, all addressed to her grandmother. **Having read** the first one, Mai realised they were love letters from a soldier — not her grandfather.*
>
> ***Not knowing** what to do, she called her mother. **After hearing** the story, her mother was silent for a long time. **Having been kept** secret for sixty years, the letters were finally about to reveal the truth.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 V-ing · ≥ 2 V3 · ≥ 2 *Having V3* · ≥ 2 liên từ + phân từ · không dangling participle.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *To make spring rolls, first soak the rice noodles. Having drained them, mix them with minced pork, carrot and wood ear mushrooms. Seasoned with fish sauce and pepper, the filling should taste slightly salty. Before rolling, dip each rice paper in water. Fried in hot oil, the rolls become golden and crispy.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Hai mệnh đề có cùng chủ ngữ.
2. V-ing: chủ động, cùng lúc/ngay sau; V3: bị động; Having V3: hành động hoàn tất trước.
3. *Not + V-ing* / *Not having + V3*.
4. Phân từ có chủ ngữ ngầm không khớp chủ ngữ mệnh đề chính, gây sai/buồn cười nghĩa.
5. *after, before, while, when, since* (và *if, unless, although, once* với V3).
