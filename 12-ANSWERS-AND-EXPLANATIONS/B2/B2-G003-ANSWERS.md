# B2-G003 · Answer Key — Unless, Provided That, In Case

> Bài học: [B2-G003](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G003-unless-provided-in-case.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. in case** | Mang tiền mặt trước, đề phòng máy thẻ hỏng. |
| L1.2 | **A. unless** | = *if you don't have*. |
| L1.3 | **C. provided** | Điều kiện bắt buộc. |
| L1.4 | **B. Even if** | Kết quả không đổi dù mưa. |
| L1.5 | **B. In case of** | + danh từ *fire*. |
| L1.6 | **B** | Phủ định kép *unless ... don't*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **Unless you water the plants, they will die.** |
| L2.2 | **Unless he apologises, I won't speak to him.** |
| L2.3 | **We'll have the party in the garden unless it rains.** |
| L2.4 | **in case** (mang sách trước, đề phòng chán) |
| L2.5 | **if** (chỉ đến đón khi được gọi) |
| L2.6 | **in case** |
| L2.7 | **You can borrow my laptop provided (that) you are careful with it.** |
| L2.8 | **I don't care where we go as long as I'm with you.** |
| L2.9 | **I wrote down her address in case I forgot it.** |
| L2.10 | **The prisoner was released on condition that he reported to the police every week.** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **Unless you finish** your work, ... |
| L3.2 | ... in case it **rains**. |
| L3.3 | **In case of** emergency, call 115. |
| L3.4 | ... provided you **keep** quiet. |
| L3.5 | I'll be really upset **if you don't come** to my wedding. (cảm xúc về điều phủ định → *if ... not*) |
| L3.6 | Only if you practise every day **will you** improve. (hoặc *You will improve only if you practise every day.*) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **You won't be allowed into the exam room unless you bring your admission card.** |
| L4.2 | **I'll take a power bank in case my phone runs out of battery.** |
| L4.3 | **You can work from home provided (that) / as long as you finish your work on time.** |
| L4.4 | **Even if I had time, I wouldn't want to go to that party.** (hoặc *Even if I have time, I don't want to...*) |

**L4.5.** *in case*: tôi mang bản đồ **ngay từ đầu** để đề phòng bị lạc — dù có lạc hay không. *if*: tôi chỉ mang/lấy bản đồ **khi** đã bị lạc — điều kiện xảy ra trước, hành động sau (nghe khá vô lý trong ngữ cảnh này, vì khi đã lạc thì không thể quay lại lấy bản đồ).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~120 từ):

> **Green Leaf Book Club — House Rules**
>
> 1. *Members may borrow up to three books **provided that** they return them within two weeks.*
> 2. *Late books will not be accepted **unless** the member has informed the organiser in advance.*
> 3. *Guests are welcome **as long as** they are introduced by a member.*
> 4. *Please bring your own cup **in case** there are not enough clean ones.*
> 5. ***In case of** cancellation, a message will be sent to the group chat.*
> 6. *Meetings will take place every Friday **even if** only a few members can attend.*
> 7. *Members will not be removed from the club **unless** they miss five meetings in a row.*
> 8. *New members can vote **only if** they have attended at least two meetings.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đủ số liên từ yêu cầu · đúng nghĩa từng liên từ · đúng thì · văn phong quy định rõ ràng · 100–130 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu:
> *Tenant: I'll sign the contract provided that you lower the rent by 500,000 dong.*
> *Landlord: I can't do that unless you agree to a one-year contract.*
> *Tenant: OK, as long as you repair the air conditioner before I move in.*
> *Landlord: Fine. And you can keep a cat on condition that you pay for any damage.*
> *Tenant: Great. Could you give me the plumber's number in case something breaks?*

## Knowledge Check

1. *if ... not*; không nên dùng khi nói về cảm xúc/kết quả của điều phủ định (*I'll be sad if you don't come*) và trong câu hỏi *What if ... not*.
2. *in case* — hành động đề phòng làm trước; *if* — hành động xảy ra sau khi điều kiện đúng.
3. *in case* + mệnh đề; *in case of* + danh từ.
4. *as long as* (thân mật) → *provided (that)* (trung tính) → *on condition that* (trang trọng).
5. Hiện tại đơn (không *will*).
