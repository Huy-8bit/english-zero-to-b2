## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | The documents must **be** signed by the manager. |
| L3.2 | This mistake should have **been** noticed earlier. |
| L3.3 | **The situation was explained to us** by the director. |
| L3.4 | The children are being looked **after** by their grandparents. |
| L3.5 | I remember **being taken** to the zoo when I was five. (*remember + V-ing* = nhớ việc đã xảy ra) |
| L3.6 | The thief was seen **to enter** the shop. (hoặc *seen entering*) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **All luggage must be checked before boarding.** |
| L4.2 | **The accident could have been avoided.** |
| L4.3 | **Nobody likes being criticised in front of others.** (hoặc *to be criticised*) |
| L4.4 | **I was given a watch for my graduation.** |

**L4.5.** *give* là động từ hai tân ngữ trực tiếp (*give **me** a watch*), nên tân ngữ người có thể làm chủ ngữ bị động. *explain* không có cấu trúc *explain **somebody** something*; nó chỉ có *explain something **to** somebody*, nên chỉ vật (*the rules*) mới làm chủ ngữ bị động: *The rules were explained to me.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~135 từ):

> **Swimming Pool Safety Rules**
>
> *All swimmers **must be registered** at reception before entering the pool area. Children under 10 **must be accompanied** by an adult at all times. Glass bottles **must not be brought** into the changing rooms, and running **should be avoided** near the edge.*
>
> *Swimmers who wish **to be taught** by a lifeguard can book a lesson at the front desk. Anyone who feels unwell **will be looked after** by our first-aid team.*
>
> *Last year, several accidents happened that **could have been prevented** if these rules had been followed. Please remember that nobody enjoys **being told off**, but rules exist for everyone's safety. Any equipment that is damaged **should be reported** immediately so that it can **be repaired**.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 4 modal bị động hiện tại · ≥ 1 modal bị động quá khứ · có *to be V3* và *being V3* · cụm động từ bị động đúng · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *Last summer our flight to Phu Quoc got cancelled at the last minute. We weren't told anything for three hours — we should have been informed much earlier. Then our luggage got lost, and when it was finally found, one suitcase had been damaged. Honestly, most of the problems could have been avoided if the staff had been better trained.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *I was given a book.* / *A book was given to me.*
2. Hiện tại: *modal + be + V3*; quá khứ: *modal + have been + V3*.
3. *to be V3* sau động từ đi với *to V* (*want, expect, hope, need*); *being V3* sau động từ đi với V-ing (*enjoy, hate, mind, avoid*) và sau giới từ.
4. Văn nói, thân mật; sự việc không mong muốn/bất ngờ, hoặc thay đổi trạng thái.
5. *He was seen to leave.*
