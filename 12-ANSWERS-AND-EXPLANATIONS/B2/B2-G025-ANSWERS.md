# B2-G025 · Answer Key — Hedging & Academic Language

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B** | Có *regular consumption, may, contribute to*. |
| L1.2 | **A** | *Most experts* thay vì *everybody*. |
| L1.3 | **B** | *suggest, may be* — phù hợp mức độ bằng chứng. |
| L1.4 | **A. tend** | *tend to + V*. |
| L1.5 | **A. likely** | *It is likely that...* |
| L1.6 | **B** | Chồng 4 phương tiện rào đón. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **Some video games may make some children more aggressive.** (hoặc *Violent video games may increase aggression in some children.*) |
| L2.2 | **People in cities tend to be more stressed.** |
| L2.3 | **This law is likely to reduce pollution.** / **It is likely that this law will reduce pollution.** |
| L2.4 | **The town's growth is largely due to tourism.** |
| L2.5 | **It could be argued that working from home is more effective than working in an office (for some employees).** |
| L2.6 | **The majority of people use smartphones nowadays.** |
| L2.7 | **The new teaching method appears to have improved students' results.** |
| L2.8 | **Online shopping often saves time.** |
| L2.9 | **There is evidence that climate change is causing more extreme weather.** |
| L2.10 | **The project is unlikely to succeed.** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | Teenagers tend **to spend** too much time on social media. |
| L3.2 | It is likely **that prices will increase** next year. / Prices are likely **to increase** next year. |
| L3.3 | The study **suggests** that coffee **may be beneficial for many people**. |
| L3.4 | This **may** cause problems. / This **could possibly** cause problems. |
| L3.5 | **Many** students **dislike** exams. |
| L3.6 | It seems that the plan will **probably** be successful. / The plan **seems likely to** succeed. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Air pollution may be one of the causes of respiratory diseases.** |
| L4.2 | **It seems / appears that young people are reading fewer printed books.** |
| L4.3 | **House prices are likely to continue rising over the next few years.** |
| L4.4 | **It could be argued that online education helps to widen access to learning.** |

**L4.5.** Khi nói về **sự thật hoặc kết quả nghiên cứu**, người viết phải trung thực với mức độ chắc chắn của bằng chứng — khẳng định quá mức dễ bị phản bác và làm giảm độ tin cậy. Còn **quan điểm cá nhân** là lập trường của chính người viết; nêu rõ ràng (*I firmly believe...*) giúp bài luận có lập luận mạch lạc. Rào đón quá nhiều ở phần quan điểm khiến bài viết thiếu thuyết phục.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~170 từ):

> *Since 2020, working from home has become common in many industries. Its effect on productivity, however, **appears to** vary considerably. **Some** surveys **suggest** that employees who work remotely **tend to** complete more tasks, **possibly** because they spend less time commuting and are interrupted less often.*
>
> *On the other hand, **it could be argued that** productivity is difficult to measure in creative or team-based roles. Remote workers **may** find it harder to share ideas informally, which **could** slow down innovation. **In many cases**, the outcome **is likely to** depend on the type of job, the quality of home working conditions and the employee's level of self-discipline.*
>
> *What is **clear** is that communication technology has made remote work possible on a scale that was unimaginable twenty years ago. Overall, the evidence **seems to** indicate that a hybrid model **would probably** offer the best balance between flexibility and collaboration.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 6 phương tiện rào đón · ≥ 4 nhóm · booster có căn cứ · không khái quát tuyệt đối · văn phong học thuật mạch lạc.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *It seems that people in Vietnam are getting married later than in the past. This may be because more young people, especially women, are focusing on their careers. In many cases, the high cost of housing also appears to play a role. It's likely that this trend will continue, although it probably varies between cities and rural areas.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Dùng ngôn ngữ để giảm mức độ chắc chắn/giới hạn phạm vi khẳng định; giúp chính xác, thận trọng, phù hợp với bằng chứng.
2. Modal, động từ, trạng từ, tính từ, lượng từ/tần suất, cấu trúc phi ngôi.
3. Từ tăng cường mức độ chắc chắn (*clearly, undoubtedly*); dùng khi có bằng chứng mạnh hoặc khi nêu quan điểm rõ ràng.
4. *It is likely that + mệnh đề* hoặc *S + be likely to + V*.
5. Dùng chồng nhiều phương tiện rào đón trong một khẳng định (*might perhaps possibly...*), khiến câu mơ hồ, thiếu quan điểm.
