# B2-G026 · Answer Key — Common Collocations

> Bài học: [B2-G026](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G026-collocations.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. make** | *make a decision* (*take a decision* cũng gặp trong Anh–Anh nhưng *make* phổ biến hơn; trong bài này chọn *make*). |
| L1.2 | **C. do** | *do sb a favour*. |
| L1.3 | **B. heavy** | *heavy traffic*. |
| L1.4 | **A. highly** | *highly likely*. |
| L1.5 | **B. pay** | *pay attention*. |
| L1.6 | **C. tells** | *tell the truth*. |

| Câu | Đáp án |

> Chưa dùng: *deeply, serious* — gây nhiễu.

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | I **made** a lot of mistakes in the test. |
| L3.2 | There was **heavy** rain last night. (*There was a lot of rain* cũng đúng) |
| L3.3 | You should **take** this medicine three times a day. |
| L3.4 | At university, students **gain / acquire** a lot of knowledge. (hoặc *learn a lot*) |
| L3.5 | The price of this phone is very **high**. / This phone is very expensive. |
| L3.6 | She **told** a lie to her parents. / She **lied** to her parents. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **We need to take action immediately to solve / tackle this problem.** |
| L4.2 | **I was bitterly / deeply disappointed with the hotel's service.** |
| L4.3 | **She is doing research on Vietnamese culture.** |
| L4.4 | **The bad weather has caused serious damage to the crops.** |

**L4.5.** Collocation không phải quy tắc ngữ pháp mà là **thói quen kết hợp từ** của người bản ngữ. *big rain* không sai cấu trúc (tính từ + danh từ), nhưng người bản ngữ không dùng nó; họ nói *heavy rain*. Dùng kết hợp không tự nhiên làm câu nghe như dịch từ tiếng mẹ đẻ và bị đánh giá thấp về từ vựng.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ, collocation in đậm):

> *Dear Customer Service Manager,*
>
> *I am writing to **make a formal complaint** about my flight VN123 on 5 June.*
>
> *Firstly, the flight was delayed by four hours due to **heavy rain**, but passengers were not **given** any **information**. I fully understand that weather conditions cannot be controlled; however, staff should have **paid** more **attention** to passengers' needs.*
>
> *Secondly, my luggage was **badly damaged**. I **took photos** of the damage and reported it immediately, but nobody **took responsibility**.*
>
> *I was **bitterly disappointed**, as I had **highly recommended** your airline to my colleagues. I would like you to **take action** and **pay compensation** for the damage. It is **highly likely** that I will travel with you again, provided this matter is resolved.*
>
> *I look forward to your **prompt reply**.*
>
> *Yours faithfully,*
> *Le Thi Mai*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 10 collocation · ≥ 4 mẫu · collocation chính xác · văn phong email phàn nàn phù hợp · 130–160 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *Yesterday was terrible. I got stuck in heavy traffic and arrived late for an important meeting. I made a silly mistake in my presentation because I wasn't paying attention. Then I had an argument with a colleague. I took a deep breath and tried to calm down, but I deeply regret what I said...*

## Knowledge Check

1. Những từ thường đi cùng nhau theo thói quen của người bản ngữ.
2. *make*: *a decision, a mistake, progress, money*; *do*: *homework, research, business, a favour* (chấp nhận các ví dụ khác trong bảng).
3. *heavy rain, heavy/light traffic, strong/weak coffee*.
4. *highly likely, fully aware, bitterly/deeply disappointed*.
5. ~~do a mistake~~ → *make a mistake*; ~~big rain~~ → *heavy rain*; ~~say a lie~~ → *tell a lie* (cũng chấp nhận ~~learn knowledge~~, ~~drink medicine~~, ~~open the light~~).
