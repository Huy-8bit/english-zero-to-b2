## Level 4 — Production

**L4.1.** *While plastic bags are convenient, I believe they should be banned in supermarkets and shops, provided that affordable alternatives are available.*

**L4.2.**
> ***(P)** The main reason for banning plastic bags is their impact on the environment. **(E)** They take a very long time to break down, so they pollute rivers and oceans for generations. **(Ex)** For example, plastic waste is often seen on beaches in Vietnam after storms. **(L)** Therefore, reducing their use would help protect natural environments.*

**L4.3.** *Some people argue that a complete ban would be inconvenient for shoppers and small businesses. However, many cities have successfully replaced plastic bags with paper or reusable bags within a short time.*

**L4.4.** *In conclusion, although a ban may cause some short-term inconvenience, its long-term environmental benefits make it a worthwhile policy.*

**L4.5.** Số liệu không có nguồn là **bịa đặt**, làm giảm độ tin cậy và có thể bị giám khảo đánh giá là thiếu trung thực học thuật. Thay vào đó, dùng **ví dụ hợp lý**, **kinh nghiệm cá nhân**, hoặc **khẳng định có rào đón** (*Many studies suggest that... / A large proportion of...*).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~265 từ):

> *As cities grow, governments must decide how to use limited land and money. **I strongly agree that** investing in public parks should take priority over building more shopping centres.*
>
> ***Firstly**, parks benefit everyone, regardless of income. Shopping centres mainly serve people who have money to spend, **whereas** parks are free and open to all. **For example**, in many Vietnamese cities, parks are crowded in the early morning with elderly people exercising and families playing. **This shows that** green spaces meet a genuine public need.*
>
> ***Secondly**, parks **appear to** improve both physical and mental health. Walking among trees **can** reduce stress, and open spaces encourage people to exercise. **In the long run**, this **may** reduce pressure on hospitals. **Therefore**, investment in parks **is likely to** produce wider social benefits than another shopping mall.*
>
> ***Some people argue that** shopping centres create jobs and generate tax revenue for the city. **Admittedly**, they do contribute to the economy. **However, this argument overlooks** the fact that most cities already have plenty of shopping options, and **many** smaller shops are struggling to compete with large malls. **What cities lack is** not places to shop, but places to breathe.*
>
> ***In conclusion**, **while** shopping centres have some economic value, parks **clearly** offer greater benefits for public health and social equality. **For these reasons**, governments should give priority to creating and maintaining green spaces.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):**

| Tiêu chí | 2 điểm |

| Rào đón & tư duy phản biện | ≥ 4 phương tiện rào đón; không ngụy biện; không bịa số liệu |

**L5.2. Rubric Speaking (/10):** quan điểm rõ (2) · 2 lý do có ví dụ (2) · thừa nhận + phản bác (2) · ngôn ngữ lập luận đa dạng (2) · trôi chảy, mạch lạc (2).

Mẫu ý: *Personally, I believe students should be allowed to use AI tools for homework, but with clear rules. The main reason is that AI is already part of the workplace, so students need to learn to use it responsibly. For example, AI can explain difficult maths problems step by step. Admittedly, some students might simply copy answers. However, teachers can design tasks that require personal reflection, which AI cannot easily do. So, on balance, banning AI completely would be unrealistic.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Point – Explanation – Evidence/Example – Link.
2. Phản đề: *Some people argue that..., It could be argued that..., Admittedly...*; phản bác: *However, this argument overlooks..., Nevertheless..., While this may be true...*
3. Khái quát quá mức (*All young people are lazy*), nhân quả sai (*A xảy ra trước B nên A gây ra B*), dốc trượt (*nếu cho X thì xã hội sụp đổ*), công kích cá nhân (*ý sai vì người đó trẻ*).
4. Không — kết bài chỉ tóm tắt và nhắc lại lập trường; ý mới khiến bài thiếu mạch lạc và ý đó không được phát triển.
5. Dùng ví dụ hợp lý, kinh nghiệm cá nhân hoặc khẳng định có rào đón; không đưa số liệu cụ thể không có nguồn.
