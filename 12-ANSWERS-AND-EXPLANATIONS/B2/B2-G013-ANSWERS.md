# B2-G013 · Answer Key — Perfect Infinitives & Perfect Gerunds

> Bài học: [B2-G013](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G013-perfect-infinitives-gerunds.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. to have lost** | Mất chìa khóa trước thời điểm hiện tại. |
| L1.2 | **A. to be sleeping** | Đang diễn ra. |
| L1.3 | **B. having sent** | *deny* + V-ing / having V3. |
| L1.4 | **A. to have kept** | *sorry + to have V3*. C sai cấu trúc. |
| L1.5 | **B. to have been copied** | Tài liệu bị sao chép (bị động, trước). |
| L1.6 | **B. to have attended** | Mong muốn không thành trong quá khứ. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | He seems **to have forgotten my name**. |
| L2.2 | They appear **to be having an argument**. |
| L2.3 | She seems **to have been a famous singer in the 1990s**. |
| L2.4 | The house seems **to have been empty for years**. |
| L2.5 | The window appears **to have been broken from inside**. |
| L2.6 | He claims **to have climbed Everest**. |
| L2.7 | She regrets **not having studied harder**. |
| L2.8 | They accused him **of having cheated** (hoặc *of cheating*). |
| L2.9 | I would like **to have seen the fireworks**. |
| L2.10 | He seems **to have been working all night**. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | She seems **to have left** the party early last night. |
| L3.2 | He appears to have **been** ill for a long time. |
| L3.3 | The suspect denied **having been** at the scene. |
| L3.4 | The car seems to have **been** damaged in the accident. |
| L3.5 | I'm sorry **to have disturbed** you. / I'm sorry **for disturbing** you. |
| L3.6 | He pretended **not to have** seen me. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **He seems to have changed a lot since he went abroad to study.** |
| L4.2 | **She admitted having lost the documents.** (*admitted losing* cũng đúng) |
| L4.3 | **I would love to have met my grandfather, but he died before I was born.** |
| L4.4 | **The temple is believed to have been rebuilt several times.** |

**L4.5.** *She seems to be happy* — **bây giờ** cô ấy có vẻ hạnh phúc. *She seems to have been happy* — có vẻ cô ấy **đã từng** hạnh phúc (trong quá khứ, ví dụ khi nhìn ảnh cũ).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~135 từ):

> *When I opened the door of the shared kitchen on Sunday morning, it looked terrible. Someone **seemed to have cooked** a huge meal and left without cleaning. The frying pan **appeared to have been burned**, and there was rice all over the floor. The window **seemed to have been left** open all night, because the room was freezing.*
>
> *My flatmate Tuan was in the living room. He **appeared to be sleeping** on the sofa, but when I asked him about the kitchen, he immediately sat up. At first, he denied **having used** it, but then he admitted **having invited** a few friends over. He said he regretted **having left** such a mess and promised to clean it up. He **seems to have learned** his lesson — the kitchen has been spotless ever since.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 *seem/appear to have V3* · *to be V-ing* · *to have been V3* · ≥ 2 *having V3* · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *This man seems to have just finished a marathon — he's wearing a number and he appears to be exhausted. He seems to have been running in the rain, because his clothes are wet. The people around him appear to be congratulating him.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *to V* (cùng lúc/sau), *to be V-ing* (đang diễn ra), *to have V3* (trước), *to have been V-ing* (kéo dài đến trước/lúc đó).
2. Sau *admit, deny, regret, remember, mention* và sau giới từ, khi muốn nhấn mạnh hành động đã xảy ra trước.
3. *to have been + V3*.
4. Mong muốn một điều trong quá khứ nhưng điều đó đã không xảy ra.
5. *She seems to have been crying.*
