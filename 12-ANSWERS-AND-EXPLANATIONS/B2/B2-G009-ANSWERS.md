# B2-G009 · Answer Key — Inversion

> Bài học: [B2-G009](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G009-inversion.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. have I eaten** | Đảo trợ động từ *have*. |
| L1.2 | **B. does she go** | Thêm *does*, động từ nguyên mẫu. |
| L1.3 | **B. when** | *Hardly ... when*. |
| L1.4 | **A. than** | *No sooner ... than*. |
| L1.5 | **B. did we hear** | Đảo mệnh đề chính sau *Not until*. |
| L1.6 | **B. do you understand** | Đảo mệnh đề chính sau *Only when*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | Never **have I** been so embarrassed. |
| L3.2 | Seldom do they **visit** ... |
| L3.3 | Hardly had I finished speaking **when** ... (hoặc *No sooner had I finished speaking than* ...) |
| L3.4 | Not until she called me **did I know** she was in town. |
| L3.5 | Only after **I read / reading** the instructions **did I understand** the problem. |
| L3.6 | Not only **does she speak** French, but she also speaks Spanish. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Never have I seen so many people at a concert.** |
| L4.2 | **No sooner had we left the house than it started to pour (with rain).** |
| L4.3 | **Not until he lost his job did he realise the importance of saving (money).** |
| L4.4 | **Under no circumstances should/must employees share their passwords.** |

**L4.5.** Với *Only when* / *Not until*, phần ngay sau liên từ là **mệnh đề phụ** (bối cảnh thời gian) nên giữ trật tự bình thường *I got home*. Đảo ngữ áp dụng cho **mệnh đề chính** — nơi chứa thông tin được nhấn mạnh (*did I realise*).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ):

> *Last year I climbed Fansipan for the first time. **Never had I** done anything so physically demanding. **Rarely do I** exercise, so my friends were surprised when I signed up.*
>
> ***Hardly had we** started walking **when** it began to rain heavily. The path became so slippery that we had to hold on to trees. **Not only were** we soaking wet, **but** we were **also** freezing.*
>
> ***Little did I know** that the hardest part was still ahead. **No sooner had we** reached the last camp **than** our guide told us we had to leave at 4 a.m. to see the sunrise.*
>
> ***Only when** I finally stood at the top, above the clouds, **did I understand** why people climb mountains. **So incredible was** the view **that** I forgot how tired I was. **Under no circumstances will I** forget that morning.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 5 cấu trúc khác nhau · đúng nhóm yêu cầu · đảo đúng trợ động từ · văn phong phù hợp (kịch tính/trang trọng) · 130–160 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *It was an ordinary morning in the small fishing village. Little did the fishermen know that a storm was approaching. No sooner had they reached the open sea than the sky turned black. Only then did they realise the danger...*

## Knowledge Check

1. Trạng từ phủ định/hạn chế + trợ động từ + chủ ngữ + động từ chính.
2. Thêm *do/does/did* và đưa động từ chính về nguyên mẫu.
3. *Hardly/Scarcely ... when*; *No sooner ... than*.
4. Mệnh đề chính (mệnh đề sau).
5. Trang trọng, văn chương, diễn thuyết, văn viết nhấn mạnh.
