# B2-G007 · Answer Key — Reduced Relative Clauses

> Bài học: [B2-G007](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G007-relative-clause-reduction.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. talking** | Cô gái **đang nói** (chủ động). |
| L1.2 | **B. served** | Cà phê **được phục vụ** (bị động). |
| L1.3 | **C. to finish** | *the first + to V*. |
| L1.4 | **B. in which** | Giới từ + *which*; không *in that*. C thiếu giới từ (*which we stayed in* mới đúng). |
| L1.5 | **B. whom** | *many of whom* — người, sau giới từ. A tạo comma splice. |
| L1.6 | **B** | *that* là tân ngữ của *made* → chỉ có thể bỏ *that*: *The cake I made*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **The woman waiting outside wants to see you.** |
| L2.2 | **The products sold online are often cheaper.** |
| L2.3 | **He was the only person to pass the exam.** |
| L2.4 | **The bridge being built across the river will be finished next year.** |
| L2.5 | **Anyone wanting to attend the workshop must register by Friday.** |
| L2.6 | **The keys on the table are mine.** |
| L2.7 | **This is the company for which my father worked for 30 years.** |
| L2.8 | **The lawyer to whom I spoke was very helpful.** |
| L2.9 | **The class has 30 students, half of whom are from the countryside.** |
| L2.10 | **I bought three books, none of which was/were interesting.** (*none of* + số nhiều: cả *was* và *were* đều chấp nhận) |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | The book **written** by this author ... |
| L3.2 | People **living** in big cities ... |
| L3.3 | He was the youngest player **to score** in the final. |
| L3.4 | The city **in which** I was born ... (hoặc *The city (that) I was born in*) |
| L3.5 | I have many friends, most of **whom** speak English. |
| L3.6 | The man **who called** me last night ... (hành động đơn lẻ đã hoàn tất — không rút gọn V-ing) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **The dishes served at this restaurant are (all) made / cooked with local ingredients.** |
| L4.2 | **Students taking part in / participating in the competition will receive a certificate.** |
| L4.3 | **She is the first Vietnamese woman to win this award.** |
| L4.4 | **I have two older brothers, both of whom live abroad.** |

**L4.5.** Trong *The cake that I made*, *that* là **tân ngữ** của *made* (chủ ngữ là *I*). Rút gọn bằng phân từ chỉ áp dụng khi đại từ quan hệ là **chủ ngữ**. Với tân ngữ, cách rút gọn đúng là **bỏ *that***: *The cake I made*. *The cake making* sai nghĩa (cái bánh đang làm gì đó); *The cake made by me* đúng ngữ pháp nhưng kém tự nhiên vì nhấn mạnh không cần thiết.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ):

> ***Discover Hoi An Ancient Town***
>
> *Hoi An, a former trading port **located** on the Thu Bon River, is one of the most charming towns in Vietnam. Visitors **walking** through its narrow streets will see yellow houses **built** centuries ago by Chinese and Japanese merchants.*
>
> *The Japanese Covered Bridge, **constructed** more than 400 years ago, is the most famous landmark in the town. Tourists **wanting** a unique souvenir can visit tailors **offering** made-to-measure clothes in 24 hours.*
>
> *On full-moon nights, many shops in the old quarter switch off their electric lights, and lanterns **made** of silk light up the streets. This is the best time **to experience** the town's magic.*
>
> *Hoi An, a UNESCO World Heritage Site **of which** locals are very proud, is often described as one of the best places in Vietnam **to see** traditional architecture.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 V-ing · ≥ 3 V3 · ≥ 1 *first/only... to V* · ≥ 1 giới từ + *which/whom* · 130–160 từ.

> Ghi chú: mẫu chỉ minh họa ngữ pháp; trước khi dùng làm thông tin du lịch, người học nên kiểm tra các dữ kiện lịch sử từ nguồn chính thức.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *In this photo of a market, the woman wearing a conical hat is selling vegetables. The fruit displayed on the left looks very fresh. The children in the front row are watching a man making sugar figures...*

## Knowledge Check

1. V-ing khi danh từ thực hiện hành động (chủ động); V3 khi danh từ nhận hành động (bị động).
2. Đại từ quan hệ phải là chủ ngữ của mệnh đề quan hệ.
3. *to V* (hoặc *to be V3* nếu bị động).
4. *which* (vật), *whom* (người).
5. *I have two cats, both of which are black.*
