**L4.5.** *deny* = phủ nhận rằng mình **đã làm** điều gì (về sự thật): *He denied stealing the car.* *refuse* = từ chối **làm** điều gì (về hành động): *He refused to give back the car.* Hai từ khác cả nghĩa lẫn cấu trúc (*deny + V-ing*, *refuse + to V*).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~145 từ):

> ***Summary of a disagreement between two flatmates***
>
> *Last Sunday, Linh **complained that** the kitchen was always dirty and **accused** Mai **of leaving** dishes in the sink for days. Mai **denied being** the only one responsible and **pointed out that** Linh often forgot to take out the rubbish.*
>
> *Linh **admitted forgetting** a few times but **insisted on making** a cleaning schedule. At first, Mai **refused to sign** anything "official", but Linh **persuaded** her **to try** it for one month.*
>
> *Mai then **suggested buying** a dishwasher and **offered to pay** half. Linh **agreed to think** about it. In the end, both of them **apologised for raising** their voices, and they **promised to discuss** problems calmly in the future.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 8 động từ tường thuật · ≥ 4 mẫu cấu trúc · cấu trúc đúng · không lạm dụng *said/told* · 130–160 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *In the scene, the father refused to let his daughter go to the concert. She insisted on going and accused him of treating her like a child. He reminded her that she had exams the next week. Finally, they agreed to compromise: she promised to study all weekend, and he offered to drive her there.*

## Knowledge Check

1. + to V: *agree, offer, refuse, promise* (cũng chấp nhận *threaten, claim, decide*); + O + to V: *advise, warn, remind, persuade* (cũng chấp nhận *invite, encourage, order*).
2. *admit, deny, suggest, recommend* (cũng chấp nhận *mention, consider*).
3. *apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on*.
4. *suggest + V-ing*; *suggest (that) + S + (should) + V*.
5. *deny* = phủ nhận sự việc đã làm (+ V-ing); *refuse* = từ chối làm (+ to V).
