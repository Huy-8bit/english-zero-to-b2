## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B** | Ba V-ing. |
| L1.2 | **A** | *both* trước danh từ thứ nhất, *and* trước danh từ thứ hai. |
| L1.3 | **B** | So sánh thời tiết với thời tiết. |
| L1.4 | **A** | V-ing ↔ V-ing. |
| L1.5 | **B** | Ba tính từ. |
| L1.6 | **A** | *call ↔ send* (cùng V nguyên mẫu). |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | The job requires patience, creativity and **organisation / organisational skills**. |
| L3.2 | She is interested **in** and skilled at photography. |
| L3.3 | Learning online is more flexible than **studying** in a classroom. |
| L3.4 | He neither called me nor **sent** a message. |
| L3.5 | My phone is more expensive than my **brother's**. |
| L3.6 | The festival was fun, colourful and **crowded**. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **My job involves answering customers, processing orders and writing reports.** |
| L4.2 | **She is not only intelligent but also very hard-working.** |
| L4.3 | **Learning a language requires time, patience and regular practice.** |
| L4.4 | **House prices in Hanoi are higher than those in Hue.** (hoặc *than in Hue*) |

**L4.5.** Câu so sánh **khí hậu** của Đà Lạt với **cả thành phố** TP.HCM — hai đối tượng không cùng loại. Sửa: *The climate in Da Lat is cooler than **that of / in** Ho Chi Minh City.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~140 từ):

> *I am a recent graduate in international business with **strong communication skills**, **a solid understanding of marketing** and **a genuine passion for travel**. During my internship at a travel agency, I was responsible for **responding to customer enquiries**, **updating the company website** and **organising promotional events**.*
>
> *I am **not only** reliable **but also** adaptable, and I enjoy working **both** independently **and** in a team. My friends describe me as **friendly**, **curious** and **determined**.*
>
> *I am applying for this position because I want **to gain** international experience, **develop** my leadership skills and **contribute** to a growing company. I believe that working for your company would be more rewarding than **working** for a large corporation, where junior staff have fewer opportunities.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 danh sách song song · ≥ 2 cặp liên từ song song · so sánh song song · không lỗi song song · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *We need cleaner air, cleaner water and cleaner cities. Protecting the environment is not a choice; it is a responsibility. It's not about what governments do; it's about what each of us does every day. The more we recycle, the less we waste. Let's act today, not tomorrow.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Các thành phần được nối bằng liên từ/so sánh phải có cùng dạng ngữ pháp.
2. Ngay trước thành phần thứ nhất; *and* ngay trước thành phần thứ hai cùng loại.
3. So sánh khác loại đối tượng (khí hậu ↔ thành phố); cần *that in/of Hanoi* hoặc *than in Hanoi*.
4. Viết đủ cả hai giới từ: *interested in and good at*.
5. Ví dụ: *We need better schools, better hospitals and better roads.*
