## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **It's no use complaining / protesting — the decision has already been made.** |
| L4.2 | **I object to having to work overtime without being paid.** (hoặc *without pay*) |
| L4.3 | **This book is (really) worth reading.** |
| L4.4 | **It's difficult for foreigners to pronounce Vietnamese tones correctly.** |

**L4.5.** *I saw her cross the street* — tôi thấy **toàn bộ** hành động: cô ấy qua đường từ bên này sang bên kia. *I saw her crossing the street* — tôi thấy cô ấy **đang** qua đường (một khoảnh khắc), không nhất thiết thấy từ đầu đến cuối.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~140 từ):

> *Hi Jake,*
>
> *I'm so glad you're moving to Ho Chi Minh City! I'm really looking forward to **showing** you around.*
>
> *Here's some advice. First, **it's worth learning** a few Vietnamese phrases — people really appreciate **your trying**. Second, **there's no point (in) buying** a car; traffic is terrible, and motorbike taxis are much faster. At first you might **have difficulty crossing** the street, but you'll soon get used to **walking** confidently through the traffic.*
>
> *When it comes to **finding** a flat, District 3 and Binh Thanh are popular with foreigners. **It's important for you to check** the contract carefully before signing.*
>
> *Finally, you won't be able to help **falling** in love with the food!*
>
> *See you soon,*
> *Nam*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 cụm cố định + V-ing · ≥ 2 *to* giới từ + V-ing · *for + O + to V* · sở hữu + V-ing · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *When I started university in Hanoi, I wasn't used to living alone. I had difficulty cooking for myself, and I couldn't help feeling homesick. There was no point in calling my mum every day, so I joined a club. It was definitely worth doing — I made lots of friends.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Thay phần sau *to* bằng *it*; nếu được → *to* là giới từ → V-ing.
2. *It's no use, there's no point (in), can't help, be worth, have difficulty (in)* (cũng chấp nhận *can't stand, be busy, feel like, spend time*).
3. *see sb do* — chứng kiến toàn bộ; *see sb doing* — chứng kiến một phần/đang diễn ra.
4. Khi cần nêu rõ **ai** thực hiện hành động sau tính từ: *It's important for children to sleep well.*
5. Chủ ngữ *this question* đã là tân ngữ ngầm của *answer*; thêm *it* là lặp tân ngữ.
