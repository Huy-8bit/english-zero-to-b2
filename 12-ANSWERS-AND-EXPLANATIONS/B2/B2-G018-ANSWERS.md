# B2-G018 · Answer Key — Contrast & Concession

> Bài học: [B2-G018](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G018-contrast-concession.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. Exhausted** | *Adj + as + S + be*. |
| L1.2 | **A. Much as** | + mệnh đề với động từ cảm xúc. |
| L1.3 | **B. On the contrary** | Phủ nhận ý "bạn mệt". |
| L1.4 | **C. Nevertheless** | Trạng từ đầu câu mới. |
| L1.5 | **A. whereas** | Đối lập hai người. |
| L1.6 | **B. Despite** | *Despite the fact that*. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | Although the weather was bad, we went hiking. |
| L3.2 | **Tired as** she was, she went to the gym. |
| L3.3 | **Despite** the heavy traffic, ... (hoặc *There was heavy traffic. Nevertheless, we arrived on time.*) |
| L3.4 | The phone is cheap. **What's more / Moreover**, it has a great camera. |
| L3.5 | Despite **having** a cold / Despite **the fact that he had** a cold, ... |
| L3.6 | **Even though** it was late, ... |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Much as I love Hanoi, I can't stand the air pollution.** |
| L4.2 | **Young as she is, she is already a manager.** |
| L4.3 | **Admittedly, electric cars are expensive. However, they are cheaper in the long run.** |
| L4.4 | **The older generation saved money, whereas young people prefer experiences.** |

**L4.5.** *On the other hand* giới thiệu **mặt khác** của cùng một vấn đề — cả hai ý đều đúng: *The job is well paid. On the other hand, the hours are long.* *On the contrary* **phủ nhận** điều vừa nói và khẳng định điều ngược lại: *"Was it boring?" — "On the contrary, it was fascinating."*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~190 từ):

> *Choosing between a large corporation and a small company is a difficult decision for many graduates. **Admittedly**, large corporations offer higher salaries, clear career paths and better training. **However**, I believe small companies are often the better choice for young people.*
>
> *Firstly, employees in small firms usually gain wider experience. **Whereas** a junior employee in a corporation may do the same task for years, someone in a start-up might handle marketing, customer service and sales in a single month. **Small as** these companies are, they can be excellent places to learn.*
>
> *Secondly, small companies tend to be more flexible. **Despite the fact that** they cannot always pay as much, they often allow staff to work remotely or choose their own hours.*
>
> ***Much as** I appreciate the security of a large organisation, I think personal growth is more important at the start of a career. Small companies are, of course, riskier; some of them close within a few years. **Even so**, the skills you gain there will be valuable wherever you go next.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đủ cấu trúc yêu cầu · chuỗi nhượng bộ – phản bác rõ · lập luận mạch lạc · ngữ pháp chính xác · 160–200 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *I believe everyone should learn English. Admittedly, some people will never travel abroad, so they may not need it. But even at home, much of the information online is in English. Much as I understand that learning a language takes time, even a basic level opens many doors. Some say translation apps make learning unnecessary. Even so, apps can't help you build real relationships.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Tương phản: so sánh hai sự việc khác nhau; nhượng bộ: thừa nhận một điều nhưng kết quả/quan điểm vẫn trái với mong đợi.
2. *Although he was tired*.
3. *on the contrary* phủ nhận ý trước; *on the other hand* đưa ra mặt khác (cả hai đều đúng).
4. *still → even so → however → nevertheless/nonetheless* (cũng chấp nhận *all the same* ở mức thân mật, *notwithstanding* ở mức rất trang trọng).
5. Thừa nhận ý kiến đối lập → phản bác → khẳng định quan điểm của mình kèm lý do.
