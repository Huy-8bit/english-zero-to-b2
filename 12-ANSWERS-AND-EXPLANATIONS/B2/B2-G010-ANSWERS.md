# B2-G010 · Answer Key — Emphasis & Cleft Sentences

> Bài học: [B2-G010](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G010-cleft-sentences-emphasis.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. who** | Nhấn mạnh người. (*that* cũng đúng nhưng không có trong lựa chọn.) |
| L1.2 | **B. What** | Wh-cleft = điều mà. |
| L1.3 | **A. did** | Emphatic *did* phản bác. |
| L1.4 | **B. that** | It-cleft nhấn mạnh nơi chốn dùng *that* (chuẩn). |
| L1.5 | **B. was** | *did* (quá khứ) → *was*. |
| L1.6 | **B** | A thừa *it*; C hòa hợp sai. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **It was my father who/that taught me to swim.** |
| L2.2 | **It was my wallet that I lost at the station.** |
| L2.3 | **It was in 2015 that we moved to Da Nang.** |
| L2.4 | **It was because of the typhoon that the flight was cancelled.** |
| L2.5 | **What I love about this café is the atmosphere.** |
| L2.6 | **What surprised me was his rudeness.** |
| L2.7 | **What we need is more time.** |
| L2.8 | **All I want is to be happy.** |
| L2.9 | **The reason (why) I'm late is that I missed the bus.** |
| L2.10 | **I do like your cooking!** |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | It was Hoa **who/that** won the competition. |
| L3.2 | What I really want **is** to travel the world. |
| L3.3 | I did **finish** my homework! |
| L3.4 | It was in 1999 **that** the company was founded. |
| L3.5 | The reason she left is **that** she was unhappy. |
| L3.6 | **All** she said was a lie. / **What** she said was a lie. (*Everything she said was a lie* cũng đúng) |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **It was my English teacher who inspired me to become an interpreter.** |
| L4.2 | **What I like most about this job is meeting lots of / many people.** |
| L4.3 | **The reason (why) I study / am learning English is that I want to work for an international company.** |
| L4.4 | **I do apologise for what happened yesterday.** (hoặc *I really am sorry about...*) |

**L4.5.** *It-cleft* dùng khi muốn **nhấn mạnh một thành phần cụ thể**, thường để đối lập hoặc sửa sai: *It was Mai who called, not Lan.* *Wh-cleft* dùng khi muốn **giới thiệu/đặt trọng tâm** cho một ý ở cuối câu, hay dùng mở đầu bài nói: *What I want to talk about today is climate change.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~140 từ):

> *Good afternoon, everyone. **What I'd like to talk about today is** the future of street food in Vietnamese cities. **The reason** I chose this topic **is that** street food is disappearing from many central streets.*
>
> *Some people say **it's** tourism **that** is killing traditional food stalls. However, **what** my research shows **is that** rising rents are the main problem. **It was** only after I interviewed twenty vendors **that** I understood how difficult their situation is.*
>
> *I **do** believe that street food is part of our cultural identity, and it deserves protection. **All** we need **is** a little creativity from city planners. In the next ten minutes, I'll suggest three practical solutions.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 2 It-cleft · ≥ 2 Wh-cleft · *The reason ... is that* và *All ... is* · emphatic *do* · văn phong thuyết trình mạch lạc.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *No, it was Phu Quoc that I went to, not Da Lat. And it was with Linh that I travelled, not Nam. It wasn't last summer either — it was in December that we went. And what we did there was mostly diving, not hiking!*

## Knowledge Check

1. *It + is/was + [thành phần nhấn mạnh] + that/who + phần còn lại.*
2. *What + S + V + is/was + [thành phần nhấn mạnh].*
3. *that* (chuẩn); *when/where* có gặp trong văn nói nhưng không phải lựa chọn an toàn.
4. Nhấn mạnh tính đúng đắn của câu khẳng định, phản bác nghi ngờ, hoặc thể hiện cảm xúc chân thành/lời mời lịch sự.
5. *The reason* đã mang nghĩa lý do; *because* lặp ý. *... is that* + mệnh đề là cấu trúc chuẩn (*is because* có gặp trong văn nói nhưng thường bị xem là kém trang trọng).
