# B2-G021 · Answer Key — Nominalisation

> Bài học: [B2-G021](../../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G021-nominalisation.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L1.1 | **reduction · improvement · failure** |
| L1.2 | **awareness · strength · possibility** |
| L1.3 | **B. in** |
| L1.4 | **A. on** |
| L1.5 | **B. for** |
| L1.6 | **C. to** |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | There was **a sharp rise / increase in unemployment last year**. |
| L2.2 | The closure **of the factory led to / resulted in the loss of 500 jobs**. |
| L2.3 | The government's **decision to ban fireworks was unpopular**. |
| L2.4 | There is a lack **of awareness of the risks**. |
| L2.5 | The team's poor **performance disappointed the fans**. (hoặc *was a disappointment to the fans*) |
| L2.6 | Tourism **has an impact on local culture**. |
| L2.7 | What is the **depth of the lake**? |
| L2.8 | The company's **rapid expansion into Asia** ... (hoàn thành câu tùy ý, ví dụ *...surprised its competitors*) |
| L2.9 | A careful **analysis of the results shows a clear pattern**. |
| L2.10 | The arrival **of the new manager changed everything**. (hoặc *brought about many changes*) |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | ... a big increase **in** house prices. |
| L3.2 | ... a huge effect **on** the economy. |
| L3.3 | We **made** a decision to move abroad. |
| L3.4 | The **rapid** development of technology ... |
| L3.5 | We need a solution **to** traffic congestion. |
| L3.6 | The **strength** of the team is its defence. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **The government's introduction of the new law has caused / provoked a lot of controversy.** |
| L4.2 | **Young people's dependence on smartphones is a growing concern.** |
| L4.3 | **(A) lack of experience was the main reason for the failure of the project / the project's failure.** |
| L4.4 | **There has been a significant decrease / fall in the number of international tourists.** |

**L4.5.** Lợi ích: (1) văn bản **gọn và khách quan** hơn, tập trung vào sự việc; (2) danh từ có thể làm **chủ ngữ** hoặc được **quy chiếu** ở câu sau (*This decision...*), giúp liên kết đoạn văn. Rủi ro: lạm dụng khiến câu **nặng nề, mơ hồ** (không rõ ai làm gì), khó đọc — đặc biệt không phù hợp văn nói/thân mật.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~110 từ):

> *The large-scale **migration** of people to urban areas has resulted in the rapid **growth** of Vietnamese cities. This **expansion** has led to a sharp **increase in** housing prices and severe **congestion** on the roads. In response, the government has begun the **construction** of new satellite towns as a **solution to** these problems. However, there is still a lack **of awareness of** these towns among the public. Furthermore, there are **concerns about** the **availability** of schools and hospitals, which may limit their **attractiveness** to young families. The long-term **success** of this policy will therefore depend on **investment in** public services.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 6 danh từ hóa · ≥ 4 cặp danh từ + giới từ đúng · giữ đủ ý gốc · văn phong học thuật mạch lạc · 100–130 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *The announcement of a new metro line in Hanoi has attracted public attention. According to officials, the completion of the project is expected within five years. The decision follows a significant increase in traffic congestion. However, there are concerns about the cost of construction and its impact on local businesses.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *-tion/-sion, -ment, -ance/-ence, -ity, -ness* (cũng chấp nhận *-al, -ure, -th, -ing*).
2. Trạng từ trở thành tính từ đứng trước danh từ (*rose sharply → a sharp rise*).
3. *increase in, impact on, solution to, demand for*.
4. *make a decision, carry out/conduct research, make progress*.
5. Trong văn nói, email thân mật, hoặc khi danh từ hóa làm câu khó hiểu, không rõ ai làm gì.
