## Gợi ý đóng vai

**Dialogue 2 — phàn nàn điều hòa (mẫu mở đầu):** *Hi, this is room 305. I'm afraid the air conditioning isn't working — it's really hot in here. Could someone take a look, or would it be possible to move to another room?*

**Dialogue 4 — đặt bàn không có tên (mẫu):** *I'm sure I booked a table for eight at seven o'clock — I called on Monday and spoke to a member of staff. Could you check again, perhaps under my first name? If there's been a mistake, is there any way you could find us a table?*
