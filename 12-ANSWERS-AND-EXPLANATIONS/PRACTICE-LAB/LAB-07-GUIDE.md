## Cấu trúc ngữ pháp nên dùng theo tuần

| Tuần | Cấu trúc |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hiện tại đơn, trạng từ tần suất, *like/enjoy + V-ing*, so sánh |
| 2 | Quá khứ đơn/tiếp diễn/hoàn thành, *used to*, loại 3 |
| 3 | *I think / In my view*, *Although / However*, *The more..., the more...* |
| 4 | *will / going to / will have done*, loại 2, *I wish*, *should / could* |

## Ví dụ mẫu (ngày 12 — nâng cao)

*One of the most important decisions I've ever made was to change my major at university. I started studying accounting because my parents wanted me to, but after a year I realised I wasn't interested in it at all. Although I was worried about disappointing them, I switched to graphic design. At first it was difficult — I had to catch up on a lot of skills. But now I work as a designer, and I genuinely love my job. If I hadn't made that decision, I'd probably be stuck in an office doing something I hate. Looking back, it taught me that it's important to follow your own interests.*
