**14. Mẫu:** *Young people can protect themselves from fake news in several ways. First, they should check the source: is it a reliable news organisation or an anonymous account? Second, they should be careful with sensational headlines and clickbait, which are often designed to provoke strong emotions. Third, it is wise to verify a story by comparing several sources before sharing it. Finally, schools should teach media literacy so that students learn to think critically about what they read online.*
