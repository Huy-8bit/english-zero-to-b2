**14. Mẫu:** *Technology has made communication faster and cheaper than ever. We can video-call friends on the other side of the world for free and keep in touch with relatives every day. However, some experts argue that online communication lacks body language and tone, which can lead to misunderstandings. Many people also spend more time chatting online than having face-to-face conversations. Overall, technology has improved communication, but it should not replace real-life interaction.*

**15. Mẫu ý:** *Once I texted my colleague "Fine." when she asked if I could change my shift. I meant "OK, no problem", but she thought I was annoyed. It was a misunderstanding caused by the lack of tone in text messages. Since then, I try to clarify my meaning...*
