**14. Mẫu (~120 từ):** *Many people believe that higher education should be free. If tuition fees were abolished, students from poor families would have the same opportunities as wealthy students. In addition, a well-educated workforce benefits the whole economy. However, free university education is extremely expensive for governments. Some argue that the money would be better spent on primary schools or vocational training, which also leads to good jobs. In my opinion, university should not be completely free, but scholarships and low-interest loans should be available so that no talented student has to drop out because of money.*

**Rubric (0–2):** ≥ 6 từ B1–B2 · quan điểm rõ có lý do · collocation chính xác.
