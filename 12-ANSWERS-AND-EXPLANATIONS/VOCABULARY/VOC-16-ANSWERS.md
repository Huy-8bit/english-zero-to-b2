**16. Mẫu:** *What a week! On Monday my laptop broke down, so I had to take it back to the shop. On Tuesday my boss asked me to come up with a new marketing plan. I worked until midnight to sort it out. On Wednesday I ran into an old classmate, and we caught up over coffee. The weekend trip was called off because of the rain, so I stayed home, turned my phone off and finally caught up on sleep. Next week I'm going to cut down on overtime!*

**Rubric (0–2):** ≥ 8 phrasal verb · vị trí đại từ đúng · thì đúng.
