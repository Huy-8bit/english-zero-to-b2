**14. Mẫu (~150 từ):** *One significant social issue in Vietnam is the ageing population. Thanks to better healthcare, people are living longer, while families are having fewer children. As a result, the proportion of elderly people is increasing. This trend has several consequences. Firstly, it puts pressure on public services such as hospitals and pension systems. Secondly, many older people in rural areas live alone because their children have migrated to cities for work, which can lead to loneliness. To tackle this problem, the government could invest in community care centres and encourage flexible working so that adult children can care for their parents. In addition, raising the retirement age gradually would help the economy. Overall, an ageing population is a challenge, but with the right policies, society can ensure a good standard of living for people of all ages.*
