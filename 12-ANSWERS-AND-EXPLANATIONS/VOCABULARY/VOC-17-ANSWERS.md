**14. Mẫu:** *My most memorable day was my university graduation. I had __made a huge effort__ during my final year, and I was __deeply moved__ when my name was called. The ceremony __took place__ in the main hall, and my parents __paid a visit__ from Hue. When I gave my short speech, I received __a round of applause__. Afterwards we __took__ lots of __photos__ and __had a great time__ at a restaurant. It was __a golden opportunity__ to thank my teachers, and I __made a promise__ to __keep in touch__.*

**Rubric (0–2):** ≥ 10 collocation đúng · tự nhiên trong ngữ cảnh · ngữ pháp đúng.
