> Chưa dùng ở phần B: *fall out*.

**14. Mẫu (~100 từ):** *I come from a close-knit family of five. My parents have always been very supportive, especially when I decided to study abroad. I have two siblings: an older brother, who takes after my father, and a younger sister. We sometimes argue, but we always make up quickly. My grandparents live nearby, so we often have family gatherings at weekends. Our most important tradition is the Tet reunion, when all our relatives come together. Although I now live in another city, I keep in touch with everyone through a family chat group.*

**Rubric (0–2):** ≥ 6 từ/collocation · ngữ pháp/giới từ đúng (*married to, get on with*) · mạch lạc.

**15. Mẫu ý:** *In Vietnam, filial piety is a strong value, so many people believe elderly parents should live with their extended family. This keeps the bond between generations strong. On the other hand, in busy nuclear families, nobody may be at home during the day, and a good care home can provide professional support. Personally, I think living together is ideal if the family is supportive, but it shouldn't make relationships strained.*
