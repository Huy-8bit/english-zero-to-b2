**14. Mẫu:** *One serious environmental problem in my city is plastic waste. Many people still use single-use bags and bottles, and rubbish often ends up in rivers and canals, which can cause flooding when drains are blocked. One solution is to introduce a fee for plastic bags in supermarkets, as some countries have done. Another is to raise awareness through school campaigns, so that children learn to recycle from an early age. Both measures would help to protect the environment and keep our city cleaner.*
