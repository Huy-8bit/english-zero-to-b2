**14. Mẫu:** *Technology has undoubtedly made many aspects of life easier. We can work remotely, shop online and keep in touch with friends abroad. Cutting-edge medical technology also saves lives. However, there are drawbacks. Many people are addicted to social media, and excessive screen time may affect our mental health. Privacy is another concern, as companies collect huge amounts of personal data. Moreover, the digital divide means that not everyone benefits equally. Overall, I believe technology has improved our lives, provided that we use it responsibly.*
