**14. Mẫu:** *Tet, or the Lunar New Year, is the most important festival in Vietnam. It usually takes place in late January or February. Before Tet, families clean and decorate their homes with peach or apricot blossoms. On New Year's Eve, people worship their ancestors and gather for a big family meal. During the holiday, children receive lucky money in red envelopes, and people visit relatives to wish them good health and luck. It is said that the first visitor of the year brings fortune for the whole year, so families choose this person carefully.*
