**14. Mẫu:** *If you want to live a healthier life, you should start with a balanced diet. Try to cut down on sugar and processed food, and eat more fruit and vegetables. You should also do exercise at least three times a week — even a 30-minute walk helps you keep fit. Don't forget your mental health: get enough sleep and take breaks from your phone. Finally, see a doctor for regular check-ups, because prevention is better than cure.*
