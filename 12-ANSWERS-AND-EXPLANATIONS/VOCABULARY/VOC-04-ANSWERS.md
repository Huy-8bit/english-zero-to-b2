**14. Mẫu (~95 từ):**
> *Dear Ms Tran,*
>
> *I am writing to apply for the position of Marketing Executive advertised on your website. I graduated from the University of Economics with a degree in marketing in 2022 and have since gained two years of experience in digital marketing. In my current role, I am in charge of social media campaigns for several major clients, and last year I helped increase online sales by 30%. I believe my skills in data analysis and content creation would make me a valuable member of your team.*

**15. Mẫu ý:** *For me, job satisfaction is more important. If you hate your job, even a high salary won't make you happy, and you might suffer from burnout. That said, salary does matter — you need to earn a decent living. Ideally, I'd like a job with reasonable pay, flexible working and a clear career path.*
