## D — Listening (15 điểm)

### Recording 1 — Chỉ đưa đoạn dưới vào TTS

> Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming. My name is Thu, and I'd like to tell you a little about the Green Lane Community Library. We started the project in 2019, when a group of parents realised that there was nowhere nearby for children to borrow books. At first, the "library" was just two shelves in the corner of a café, which the owner kindly let us use for free. Today, thanks to donations, we have our own small room above the community centre and around four thousand books.
>
> We're open five days a week, and on Saturday mornings we run a storytelling session for children under eight. It's become so popular that we sometimes have to turn families away.
>
> I won't pretend everything is easy, though. Our biggest problem at the moment is a shortage of volunteers. Most of us have full-time jobs, and it's becoming difficult to keep the library open in the afternoons. So, if you can spare even two hours a week, please come and talk to me after this meeting. And if you can't give time, you could help by donating children's books in good condition — especially books in English, which are always in demand.
>
> Before I finish, I'd like to share what has surprised me most. I expected the children to enjoy the library, of course. What I didn't expect was how many elderly people would come in every day — not just to read, but to talk to each other. The library has become a meeting place for the whole neighbourhood.

### Recording 2 — Chỉ đưa hội thoại dưới vào TTS

> **Chi:** Minh, have you seen the email about the regional marketing conference?
> **Minh:** I saw it, but I haven't read it properly. Where is it this year?
> **Chi:** In Da Nang. It's on the 14th and 15th of November.
> **Minh:** What's the main topic?
> **Chi:** Digital marketing and artificial intelligence. There are some really interesting speakers.
> **Minh:** Hmm. To be honest, I'm not sure I should go. We've got the product launch at the end of November, and I'm worried I'll fall behind.
> **Chi:** I understand, but think about it — the whole launch campaign is going to be digital. If you go, you'll probably come back with ideas we can actually use. It could save us time in the long run.
> **Minh:** That's a fair point. What about the cost?
> **Chi:** The early-bird price is available until the 20th of October, so we'd need to register soon. And the company is paying for the hotel; we only need to cover our own meals.
> **Minh:** OK, that makes it easier. Let's do this: I'll ask our manager if Tuan can cover my tasks for those two days, and if she agrees, we'll register together this week.
> **Chi:** Perfect.

## E — Writing (20 điểm)

### Rubric E1 (/8)

| Tiêu chí | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nội dung | Thiếu ≥ 2 ý | Thiếu 1 ý (vấn đề / đã thử / yêu cầu) | Đủ cả 3, chi tiết |
| Tổ chức | Không có đoạn | Có đoạn, liên kết đơn giản | Mở – thân – kết rõ, liên kết tốt |
| Ngôn ngữ | Nhiều lỗi | Lỗi thường gặp nhưng hiểu được | Chính xác, đa dạng, có cấu trúc B2 |
| Văn phong | Thân mật | Không nhất quán | Trang trọng, lịch sự, nhất quán |

### Mẫu E1 (~170 từ)

> *Dear Customer Service Team,*
>
> *I am writing to complain about the Advanced Business English course (order no. 7782), which I purchased on 2 September.*
>
> *Since enrolling, I have experienced several problems. Firstly, the videos in Modules 3 and 4 cannot be played; an error message appears every time I try to open them. Secondly, I have sent three questions to the instructor through the course forum, none of which has received a reply. Finally, the materials do not match the description on your website, which promised downloadable worksheets and weekly live sessions.*
>
> *I have already tried using a different browser and device, and I have cleared my cache, but the videos still do not work.*
>
> *I would therefore be grateful if you could either fix these issues within seven days or provide a full refund. Should neither option be possible, I would appreciate a partial refund reflecting the missing content.*
>
> *I look forward to your prompt reply.*
>
> *Yours faithfully,*
> *Nguyen Hoang Anh*

### Rubric E2 (/12)

| Tiêu chí | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task response | Lạc đề | Chỉ bàn một quan điểm | Bàn cả hai, ý kiến chưa rõ | Bàn công bằng cả hai, ý kiến rõ, ý phát triển tốt |
| Coherence & cohesion | Rời rạc | Có đoạn, liên kết máy móc | Mạch lạc, liên kết đa dạng | Mạch lạc tự nhiên, quy chiếu tốt |
| Lexical resource | Rất hạn chế | Lặp, sai collocation | Đủ đa dạng, ít lỗi | Đa dạng, chính xác, collocation tự nhiên |
| Grammar | Lỗi gây khó hiểu | Câu đơn giản, nhiều lỗi | Đa dạng, lỗi không cản trở | Đa dạng cấu trúc B2, chính xác cao |

### Mẫu E2 (~270 từ)

> *Public health has become a major concern in many countries as rates of obesity and stress continue to rise. While some people believe that building more sports facilities is the most effective solution, others argue that this alone would achieve little.*
>
> *Those in favour of more sports facilities point out that many people do not exercise simply because they have nowhere to go. In crowded cities, for example, parks and public sports centres are often far from residential areas or too expensive. If free or low-cost facilities were available in every neighbourhood, more people would be likely to exercise regularly. Moreover, such facilities can bring communities together, encouraging people to stay active through team sports.*
>
> *On the other hand, critics argue that access is not the main barrier. Many people who live near gyms or parks still do not use them, often owing to long working hours, unhealthy diets or a lack of motivation. In their view, public health would benefit more from measures such as health education in schools, taxes on sugary drinks or flexible working hours that give people time to exercise.*
>
> *In my opinion, both arguments have merit, but I believe facilities are only part of the solution. Admittedly, it is difficult to stay active without suitable places to do so. However, a new sports centre will make little difference if people are too tired or uninformed to use it. What is needed, therefore, is a combination of better facilities and policies that address the underlying causes of unhealthy lifestyles.*
>
> *In conclusion, while sports facilities can certainly help, they are unlikely to transform public health on their own.*

## F — Speaking (15 điểm)

### Câu hỏi cho người hỗ trợ (Part 3) — dùng nếu có người đóng vai giám khảo

- *Why do some people find it difficult to make important decisions?* → tiếp: *Do you think it's better to decide quickly or slowly?*
- *Should young people follow their parents' advice when choosing a career?* → tiếp: *What if parents and children disagree?*
- *Do people today have more choices than previous generations?* → tiếp: *Can too many choices make people unhappy?*

### Rubric (/15)

| Tiêu chí | 0–1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trôi chảy & mạch lạc | Ngắt quãng nhiều, khó theo dõi | Có ngập ngừng nhưng giữ được mạch | Nói liên tục, dùng discourse markers tự nhiên |
| Từ vựng | Hạn chế, lặp | Đủ cho chủ đề | Đa dạng, collocation, paraphrase khi cần |
| Ngữ pháp | Chủ yếu câu đơn, nhiều lỗi | Có câu phức, lỗi thường gặp | Đa dạng cấu trúc B2 (điều kiện hỗn hợp, bị động, nhượng bộ), chính xác |
| Phát âm | Khó hiểu | Hiểu được, lỗi âm cuối/trọng âm | Rõ ràng, trọng âm và ngữ điệu hợp lý |
| Tương tác & phát triển ý | Trả lời quá ngắn | Có lý do | Lý do + ví dụ + mở rộng, phản hồi câu hỏi tiếp nối |

### Mẫu tham khảo F2 (trích)

*The decision I'd like to talk about is moving from Hue to Ho Chi Minh City after I graduated. I made it in 2020, mainly because there were very few jobs in my field at home. At first, it was really hard — I didn't know anyone, and the cost of living was much higher than I'd expected. But I managed to find a job in a design company, and now I'm a team leader. If I hadn't moved, I probably wouldn't have such a good career now. Would I do it again? Definitely — although I'd probably save more money before going!*
