## Transcript — Part D

**D1 (51–55):**

> Hello, this is Emma Clarke — that's C-L-A-R-K-E. I'm calling about my online order, number 4-5-8-2. The T-shirt arrived on Tuesday, but the colour is wrong. I ordered a blue one, size medium, but you sent a red one. Can you send the blue T-shirt, please? I'm at home on Thursday, so Thursday is the best day for delivery. My phone number is oh nine eight three, two one six, seven oh four. Thank you!

**D2 (56–60):**

> **Clerk:** Good morning, Hanoi Station ticket office. How can I help you?
> **Man:** Hi, I'd like a ticket to Hue, please. For tomorrow morning.
> **Clerk:** Let me check... The seven fifteen train is full, I'm afraid. There's one at seven fifty.
> **Man:** Seven fifty is fine. How much is it?
> **Clerk:** A soft seat is five hundred and forty thousand dong. A bed is more expensive.
> **Man:** A seat is OK. Can I have a window seat?
> **Clerk:** Sure. Window seat, carriage 6. The train leaves from Platform 2.
> **Man:** Great. Can I pay by card?
> **Clerk:** Yes, of course.
