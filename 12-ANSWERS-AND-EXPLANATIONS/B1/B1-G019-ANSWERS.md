# B1-G019 · Answer Key — Conjunctions & Complex Sentences

> Bài học: [B1-G019](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G019-complex-sentences.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **S** | Một mệnh đề. |
| L1.2 | **C** | Hai mệnh đề độc lập + *but*. |
| L1.3 | **X** | Mệnh đề phụ *Because...* + mệnh đề chính. |
| L1.4 | **X** | Mệnh đề danh từ *what time it is* làm tân ngữ. |
| L1.5 | **B. what he means** | Trật tự câu kể trong mệnh đề danh từ. |
| L1.6 | **B** | Hai mệnh đề độc lập nối chỉ bằng dấu phẩy. |

| Câu | Đáp án |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lỗi |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | **We went home because the shop was closed.** (hoặc nối với một mệnh đề chính khác) | Fragment |
| L3.2 | I love my job**. It** is very interesting. / I love my job **because** it is very interesting. | Run-on |
| L3.3 | She studied hard**, so** she passed the exam. | Comma splice |
| L3.4 | I wonder why **she is** crying. | Trật tự trong mệnh đề danh từ |
| L3.5 | What I want **is** a quiet life. | Thừa *it* |
| L3.6 | Although he was tired**,** he finished the report. | Hai liên từ |

## Level 4 — Production

**L4.1.** Mẫu: *When I moved to Hanoi, I didn't know anyone, so I joined a club. That's where I met my best friend, who helped me a lot because she understood how lonely I felt.*
✅ Có câu ghép (*..., so ...*) và câu phức (*When..., who..., because..., how...*).

**L4.2.** ***What I like most about this city is the people.***

**L4.3.** *I'm not sure **whether** she will come to the party.*

**L4.4.** Mẫu: *When I arrived at work, my boss was already waiting, so I started the meeting immediately, although I hadn't had breakfast.*

**L4.5.** Câu *I was tired, I went to bed* nối **hai mệnh đề độc lập chỉ bằng dấu phẩy** (comma splice) — trong văn viết chuẩn, dấu phẩy không đủ để nối hai câu hoàn chỉnh. Ba cách sửa: (1) tách thành hai câu: *I was tired. I went to bed.*; (2) dùng liên từ đẳng lập: *I was tired, so I went to bed.*; (3) dùng liên từ phụ thuộc: *Because I was tired, I went to bed.* (cũng có thể dùng dấu chấm phẩy).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~135 từ):

> *Three years ago, I had to decide whether I should stay in my hometown or move to Ho Chi Minh City. My parents wanted me to stay, but I wanted more opportunities. Although I loved my family, I knew that I needed a change. When I finally told them about my plan, my mother cried, and my father didn't say anything. What worried me most was that they would feel lonely. However, I promised that I would call every day. After I moved, I found a job quickly because many companies needed accountants, so I was able to send money home. Now my parents understand why I left, and they visit me every summer.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 câu ghép đúng · ≥ 4 câu phức, ≥ 3 liên từ phụ thuộc · ≥ 2 mệnh đề danh từ · không fragment/run-on/comma splice · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu chuỗi:
1. *I learn English.*
2. *I learn English because I want a better job.*
3. *Every evening, after I finish work, I learn English because I want a better job.*
4. *Although I'm often tired, I learn English every evening after work because I want a better job.*
5. *Although I'm often tired, I learn English every evening after work so that I can get a better job in an international company.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Câu đơn: 1 mệnh đề độc lập; câu ghép: 2+ mệnh đề độc lập nối bằng FANBOYS; câu phức: mệnh đề chính + mệnh đề phụ.
2. *for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so*.
3. Khi mệnh đề phụ đứng đầu câu.
4. Fragment — mệnh đề phụ đứng một mình; run-on — hai mệnh đề dính liền không dấu/liên từ; comma splice — nối hai mệnh đề độc lập chỉ bằng dấu phẩy.
5. Trật tự câu kể (S + V): *I don't know where she lives.*
