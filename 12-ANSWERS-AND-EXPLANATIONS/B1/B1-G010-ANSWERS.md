# B1-G010 · Answer Key — Reported Speech: Statements

> Bài học: [B1-G010](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G010-reported-statements.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. told** | Có người nghe *me* ngay sau → *told*. *talked/spoke* không đi trực tiếp với *me that*. |
| L1.2 | **B. said** | Không có người nghe → *said*. D sai (*said me*). |
| L1.3 | **B. would** | *will → would*. |
| L1.4 | **B. had finished** | HTHT → QKHT. D là bị động, sai nghĩa. |
| L1.5 | **B. could** | *can → could*. |
| L1.6 | **B. the next day** | Kể lại sau một tuần → *tomorrow* không còn đúng. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án | Thay đổi |
|---|---|---|
| L2.1 | Hung said (that) he **was** tired. | *I → he*; *am → was* |
| L2.2 | My mother said she **didn't like** spicy food. | *don't → didn't* |
| L2.3 | Linh said she **had never been** to Japan. | HTHT → QKHT |
| L2.4 | The manager said they **would open** a new branch **the following year**. | *we → they*; *will → would*; *next year → the following year* (giữ *next year* nếu kể trong cùng năm) |
| L2.5 | Tom told me he **couldn't find** his keys. | *can't → couldn't*; *my → his* |
| L2.6 | Mai said she **was meeting** her sister **that night**. | (*tonight* nếu kể trong cùng ngày) |
| L2.7 | Dad said he **had lost** his wallet **the day before**. | QKĐ → QKHT (giữ *lost* cũng chấp nhận); *yesterday → the day before* |
| L2.8 | The doctor told me I **had to** rest for a week. | *you → I*; *must → had to* (*must* giữ nguyên cũng đúng) |
| L2.9 | **told** | *tell a story*. |
| L2.10 | **said** | *say goodbye*. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | She **told me** that she was busy. / She **said** that... | *say* không đi trực tiếp với người. |
| L3.2 | He **told me** (us) that he would be late. / He **said** that... | *tell* cần người nghe. |
| L3.3 | Lan said that **she was** going to Hanoi. | Đổi đại từ. |
| L3.4 | They said they **would** arrive the following day. | *will → would* (khớp *the following day*). |
| L3.5 | He said that he **had lived** there for ten years. | Không còn đúng → lùi thì; *here → there*. |
| L3.6 | She said that she was hungry. | Bỏ ngoặc kép. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | She **told me** (that) she **had found** a new job. |
| L4.2 | He **said** he **would call** me back **the next day**. |
| L4.3 | She said, "**I was ill last week**." (hoặc *"I've been ill this week"* tùy ngữ cảnh; *the week before* ↔ *last week*) |
| L4.4 | Mẫu: *My sister told me she was getting a new job. My dad said he wanted to retire soon. My friend said she would visit me in May.* |

**L4.5.** Vì đây là **sự thật khoa học luôn đúng**, kể cả lúc tường thuật. Lùi thì dùng để đánh dấu lời nói thuộc về quá khứ; khi thông tin vẫn đúng ở hiện tại, giữ thì hiện tại là tự nhiên (lùi thì *boiled* cũng không sai về ngữ pháp).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~110 từ):

> *Hi Lan,*
>
> *Here's a quick summary of this morning's meeting with the design agency. Ms Ha told us that the new logo was almost ready. She said her team had tested three versions with customers and that most people preferred the blue one. However, she said the website would take longer because their developer was on sick leave. She told me she couldn't promise a date yet, but she would send us a plan by Friday. Finally, she said the total cost hadn't changed. I told her we were happy with the progress.*
>
> *Best, Minh*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 6 câu tường thuật đúng cấu trúc · dùng đúng cả *said* và *told + người* · lùi thì/đổi đại từ nhất quán · 100–120 từ, văn phong email công việc.

**L5.2.**
- *Nam said (that) he was getting married.*
- *Lan told me she had bought a new car.*
- *Hoa said she would travel to Korea the following month.* (*next month* nếu kể trong cùng tháng)
- *Duc said he couldn't stand his new boss.*
- *Mai said she hadn't slept the night before.* (*didn't sleep last night* nếu kể ngay hôm sau — vẫn chấp nhận)

## Knowledge Check

1. *say (that) + mệnh đề*; *tell + người + (that) + mệnh đề*.
2. *will → would*, *can → could*, *must → had to* (hoặc giữ), *have done → had done*.
3. *tomorrow → the next/following day*, *yesterday → the day before/the previous day*, *here → there*.
4. Khi động từ tường thuật ở hiện tại, hoặc thông tin vẫn còn đúng lúc kể.
5. *Hoa said (that) she was working from home that day.* (*today* nếu kể trong cùng ngày)
