# B1-G015 · Answer Key — Modal Perfects

> Bài học: [B1-G015](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G015-modal-perfects.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. must have rained** | Suy luận về quá khứ từ bằng chứng hiện tại. A là dạng hiện tại; C là phê bình; D ngược nghĩa. |
| L1.2 | **B. should have studied** | Hối tiếc. D là lời khuyên hiện tại. |
| L1.3 | **B. can't have read** | Gần như chắc chắn không đọc (laptop chưa mở). D không dùng để suy luận. |
| L1.4 | **B. could have** | |
| L1.5 | **B** | *could have been* = khả năng nguy hiểm đã không xảy ra. |
| L1.6 | **A. might have left** | Khả năng. D ngược nghĩa với ngữ cảnh. |

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | You **should have** told me earlier. | Không *should of*. |
| L3.2 | She must have **taken** the wrong train. | V3. |
| L3.3 | He **can't have** heard the alarm. | Suy luận phủ định. |
| L3.4 | I should **have called** you yesterday. | Thiếu *have*. |
| L3.5 | They might **have** gone to the beach. | *have* nguyên mẫu. |
| L3.6 | Last night she **must have been** very tired. | Quá khứ. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | He **must have forgotten** my birthday. |
| L4.2 | I **shouldn't have eaten** so much. |
| L4.3 | She **can't have lied** — she's a very honest person. |
| L4.4 | We **could have won** that match. |

**L4.5.** *He must have called* = suy luận rằng anh ấy **chắc hẳn đã gọi** (ví dụ thấy cuộc gọi nhỡ). *He should have called* = anh ấy **đã không gọi**, và người nói cho rằng lẽ ra anh ấy nên gọi (phê bình).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~115 từ):

> *Last Monday was a terrible day. I arrived at the airport and found that my flight to Hanoi had already left. I must have read the wrong time on my ticket — it said 7:00, not 9:00. The airline staff were kind, but the next flight was full. I should have checked the ticket the night before, and I shouldn't have stayed up so late. My phone battery was also dead, so I couldn't call my boss. He must have been very angry when I didn't arrive at the meeting. I could have taken a bus, but it would have been too slow. In the end, I learned to set two alarms.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 suy luận *must/might/can't have* · ≥ 3 câu *should(n't)/could have* · kể sự việc rõ ràng · 100–120 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *Someone must have come in through the window. The food is gone, so they must have been hungry — or the cat might have eaten it! The muddy footprints are too big to be the cat's, so a person must have walked across the floor. The cat must have been frightened — that's why it's hiding. I should have closed the window before I left.*

**L5.3.** Mẫu: *You should have kept your passport in a safe place. You shouldn't have put it in your back pocket. You should have made a copy before the trip. You could have left it in the hotel safe. Next time, you should take a photo of it on your phone.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Quá khứ.
2. *must have* — gần chắc chắn đã xảy ra; *can't have* — gần chắc chắn đã không xảy ra.
3. Hối tiếc hoặc phê bình việc lẽ ra nên làm (nhưng đã không làm).
4. (1) Suy luận: có thể đã xảy ra; (2) khả năng/cơ hội đã có nhưng không thành hiện thực.
5. *should've* chỉ nghe giống *should of*; dạng đúng là *should have*.
