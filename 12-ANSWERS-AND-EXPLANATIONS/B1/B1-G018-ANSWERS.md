# B1-G018 · Answer Key — Linking Words

> Bài học: [B1-G018](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G018-linking-words.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. Despite** | Theo sau là cụm danh từ *the traffic*. A cần mệnh đề; C cần đứng đầu câu mới riêng; D sai nghĩa. |
| L1.2 | **B. due to** | + danh từ *bad weather*. A cần mệnh đề. |
| L1.3 | **C. However** | Đầu câu mới, có dấu phẩy. |
| L1.4 | **C. Although** | + mệnh đề *she was tired*. A, B cần N/V-ing. |
| L1.5 | **A. whereas** | Đối lập hai người. |
| L1.6 | **B** | A ghép đôi *although...but*; C *despite* + mệnh đề. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **Despite the cold (weather)**, they went swimming. / *Despite it being cold* (văn nói) |
| L2.2 | **Although he was injured / had an injury**, he played the whole match. |
| L2.3 | She studied hard. **However**, she failed the test. |
| L2.4 | We stayed at home **because there was a storm / it was stormy**. |
| L2.5 | The company lost money. **As a result**, 100 people lost their jobs. |
| L2.6 | **In spite of the fact that** she earns a lot, she isn't happy. |
| L2.7 | **Moreover** |
| L2.8 | **Since** |
| L2.9 | **for example** (có thể thay bằng *such as* nếu bỏ dấu phẩy: *problems such as...*) |
| L2.10 | **so** |

> Chưa dùng: *while, in conclusion* — hai từ gây nhiễu.

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | Although the film was long, it was interesting. | Bỏ *but*. |
| L3.2 | **Although** it was raining, ... / **Despite the rain**, ... | *despite* + N. |
| L3.3 | **In spite of** the bad weather, ... | Thiếu *of*. |
| L3.4 | He is very talented. **However,** he is lazy. (hoặc *talented; however, he...*) | Dấu câu với *however*. |
| L3.5 | **Because** the price was too high, ... / **Because of the high price**, ... | |
| L3.6 | **Despite** his age, ... | Bỏ *of*. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **Although** English is difficult, I really enjoy learning it. |
| L4.2 | **Despite having lived** (*living*) in Australia for ten years, he still misses Vietnamese food. |
| L4.3 | The city has built more metro lines. **As a result**, traffic has improved. |
| L4.4 | Smartphones help us stay in touch with people. **On the other hand**, they can be very distracting. |

**L4.5.** *Despite* là giới từ nên chỉ đi với danh từ/V-ing, không đi trực tiếp với mệnh đề có S + V (*it was raining*). Cụm *the fact that* biến mệnh đề thành một cụm danh từ, nên *Despite the fact that it was raining* đúng.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ):

> *Many students in Vietnam now work part-time while studying. There are several advantages. **Firstly**, they earn money, **so** they depend less on their parents. **In addition**, they gain real experience, **such as** customer service and teamwork. **Moreover**, employers often prefer graduates who have worked before.*
>
> ***However**, part-time jobs also have disadvantages. **Although** students learn useful skills, they may have less time to study. **As a result**, their grades can fall. **Furthermore**, some jobs are tiring **because of** long hours at night.*
>
> ***Despite** these problems, I believe part-time work is beneficial if students choose carefully. **In conclusion**, students should work no more than fifteen hours a week, **so that** their studies come first.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):**

| Tiêu chí | 2 điểm |
|---|---|
| Nội dung | Có ưu điểm, nhược điểm, kết luận rõ |
| Đa dạng từ nối | ≥ 8 từ nối thuộc ≥ 5 chức năng |
| Chính xác cấu trúc | Đúng cấu trúc theo loại từ; có *although/despite* |
| Dấu câu | Dấu phẩy/chấm đúng với trạng từ liên kết |
| Độ dài | 130–160 từ |

**L5.2.** Mẫu ý: *Firstly, big cities offer more jobs. Moreover, there are better hospitals and schools. However, living costs are high. Although salaries are higher, rent takes a large part of them. On the other hand, small towns are cheaper but have fewer opportunities. Overall, I think big cities are better for young people.*

## Knowledge Check

1. Liên từ + mệnh đề; giới từ + danh từ/V-ing; trạng từ liên kết đứng đầu câu mới (hoặc sau ;) + dấu phẩy.
2. *although* + mệnh đề; *despite* + danh từ/V-ing/*the fact that* + mệnh đề.
3. Dấu chấm (hoặc chấm phẩy) trước, dấu phẩy sau: *. However,*
4. *because* + mệnh đề; *because of* + danh từ/cụm danh từ.
5. Tiếng Anh chỉ dùng một từ nối cho một quan hệ tương phản; *although* đã đủ.
