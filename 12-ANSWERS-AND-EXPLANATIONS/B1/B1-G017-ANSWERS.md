# B1-G017 · Answer Key — Causative Have / Get

> Bài học: [B1-G017](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G017-causative-have-get.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. have it fixed** | Thợ sửa → *have + O + V3*. C nghĩa là tự sửa (HTHT); D thiếu V3. |
| L1.2 | **A. had** | *have one's photo taken*. *made/let* cần V nguyên mẫu, không + V3 trong nghĩa này. |
| L1.3 | **B. to tidy** | *get + người + to V*. |
| L1.4 | **C. arrange** | *have + người + V* (không *to*). |
| L1.5 | **B** | Trải nghiệm không mong muốn. |
| L1.6 | **B. Did** | *have* là động từ thường → *Did*. |

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | I **had/got my hair cut** yesterday. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | I **had my hair cut** at a salon yesterday. | Người khác làm. |
| L3.2 | We **had our house painted** last month. | Trật tự *have + O + V3*. |
| L3.3 | She is having her dress **made** in Hoi An. | V3. |
| L3.4 | **Did you have** your car washed? | *have* động từ thường. |
| L3.5 | I got my friend **to lend** me his bike. | *get + người + to V*. |
| L3.6 | The manager had the cleaner **clean** the meeting room. | Bỏ *to*. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | I **had/got my phone repaired** last week. |
| L4.2 | We **are having/getting new windows installed**. |
| L4.3 | You should **get your eyes tested**. |
| L4.4 | I **got my brother to drive** me to the airport. |

**L4.5.** *I repaired my bike* = tôi **tự tay** sửa. *I had my bike repaired* = tôi **nhờ/thuê** người khác sửa.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~90 từ):

> *Every year, my family gets very busy before Tet. Last week, we had the whole house cleaned by a professional company. Right now, we're having the gate repainted red. My mother is going to have a new áo dài made at her favourite tailor. I usually get my hair cut a few days before the holiday. However, we do some things ourselves: my father decorates the peach tree, and my sister and I wrap bánh chưng together. That's my favourite part of the preparations.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 5 câu nhờ bảo đúng · ≥ 3 thì khác nhau · phân biệt rõ việc tự làm · 80–100 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *I have my hair cut every month at a small barbershop near my house. I get my motorbike serviced every three months. The last time I had it serviced was in May, and I also had the tyres changed. Every year, I have my teeth cleaned at the dentist's...*

## Knowledge Check

1. *have + O + V3*.
2. *did* (*Did you have...?*).
3. *have someone do* — bảo người có trách nhiệm làm; *get someone to do* — thuyết phục/nhờ được ai làm.
4. Điện thoại của anh ấy bị lấy cắp (trải nghiệm không mong muốn).
5. *We are having our sink fixed.*
