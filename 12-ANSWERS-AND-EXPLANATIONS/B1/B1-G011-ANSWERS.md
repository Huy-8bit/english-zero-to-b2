# B1-G011 · Answer Key — Reported Questions, Requests & Commands

> Bài học: [B1-G011](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G011-reported-questions-commands.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. I worked** | Trật tự câu kể, lùi thì. A, C giữ đảo; D có dấu ? và chưa lùi thì. |
| L1.2 | **B. if** | Câu hỏi Yes/No. |
| L1.3 | **C. to wait** | *ask + O + to V*. |
| L1.4 | **C. not to touch** | Mệnh lệnh phủ định. |
| L1.5 | **B** | Không đảo, không dấu ?. |
| L1.6 | **A. had taken** | *who* là chủ ngữ → giữ trật tự, lùi *took → had taken*. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | Lan asked (me) **if/whether I liked** jazz. |
| L2.2 | The officer asked (me) **where I was going**. |
| L2.3 | My boss asked (me) **if I had finished** the report. |
| L2.4 | Tom asked (me) **why I had left** early. (*why I left* cũng chấp nhận) |
| L2.5 | The receptionist asked (me) **how long I would stay**. |
| L2.6 | My friend asked **if I could lend** him/her some money. / My friend asked **me to lend** him/her some money. |
| L2.7 | The teacher told the students **to open** their books. |
| L2.8 | Mum told me **not to stay** out late. |
| L2.9 | My colleague asked me **to check** her/his email. |
| L2.10 | The doctor told the patient **not to eat** fried food. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | She asked me where **I had bought / I bought** my shoes. | Không đảo. |
| L3.2 | He asked me **if I wanted** some tea. | *if* + lùi thì. |
| L3.3 | They asked me what **my job was**. | Không đảo, bỏ ?. |
| L3.4 | The teacher told us **not to talk**. | |
| L3.5 | My father told me **to finish** my homework. | |
| L3.6 | She asked **if** I could come. | Bỏ *that*. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | She asked me **how long I had worked** there. |
| L4.2 | He asked me **if/whether I wanted** to go to the cinema. |
| L4.3 | The boss told us **not to use** our phones in the meeting. |
| L4.4 | Mum asked me **to buy** some eggs. |

**L4.5.** Câu đầu là một **câu hỏi thật** (người nói đang hỏi), chỉ có phần *where the station is* ở dạng gián tiếp — nên cả câu vẫn kết thúc bằng dấu ?. Câu sau là **câu kể** thuật lại điều người khác đã hỏi, nên dùng dấu chấm.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~110 từ):

> *Yesterday I went to see a doctor because of my back pain. First, she asked me how long I had had the problem. I told her it had started two weeks before. Then she asked whether I sat at a computer all day, and I said I did. She asked if I did any exercise, and I admitted that I didn't. After examining me, she told me to take a break every hour and asked me not to carry heavy bags for a while. She also told me to come back if the pain didn't improve. I promised I would start doing yoga.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** ≥ 3 câu hỏi tường thuật đúng (có Yes/No và Wh-) · ≥ 2 yêu cầu/mệnh lệnh tường thuật đúng · lùi thì/đổi đại từ nhất quán · 100–120 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *He asked me where I was from and what I did. Then he asked if I liked my job and how long I had lived here. He asked whether I could cook — I said I couldn't! Finally, he asked if I would come to his party.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *if* hoặc *whether*.
2. Vì nó đã trở thành một phần của câu kể, không còn là câu hỏi.
3. *tell + O + not to + V*.
4. *ask* = nhờ/đề nghị (lịch sự); *tell* = ra lệnh/chỉ dẫn.
5. *He/She asked (me) who had called me.* (*who called me* cũng chấp nhận)
