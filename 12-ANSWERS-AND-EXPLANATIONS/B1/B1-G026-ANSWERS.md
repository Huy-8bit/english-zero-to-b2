# B1-G026 · Answer Key — Expressing Opinions

> Bài học: [B1-G026](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G026-expressing-opinions.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. So do I.** | Câu khẳng định, động từ thường → *So do I*. |
| L1.2 | **B. Neither can I.** | Phủ định + modal *can*. |
| L1.3 | **B** | *agree* là động từ. |
| L1.4 | **B** | Phủ định chuyển lên *think*. A đúng ngữ pháp nhưng kém tự nhiên hơn; C sai. |
| L1.5 | **B** | A quá thẳng; C là đồng ý. |
| L1.6 | **B. So was I.** | Khớp *was*. C sai trật tự (nghĩa khác: "đúng là tôi đã vậy"). |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | **In my opinion / Personally**, smartphones are necessary. |
| L3.2 | In my opinion, the price is too high. / I think the price is too high. |
| L3.3 | "**Neither do I.**" |
| L3.4 | I **don't agree** with this idea. |
| L3.5 | "**So can I.**" |
| L3.6 | "**Neither am I.**" |

## Level 4 — Production

**L4.1.**
- Thân mật: *I reckon students should wear uniforms.*
- Trung tính: *In my opinion, students should wear uniforms.*
- Trang trọng: *As far as I'm concerned, students should be required to wear uniforms.*

**L4.2.** *I agree up to a point — coding teaches logical thinking. However, I'm not sure every student needs it, especially those who want to study art or literature.*

**L4.3.** *I see what you mean — tourism can cause pollution and higher prices. But I'm not sure I agree that it **only** brings problems. It also creates jobs and helps local businesses.*

**L4.4.** *What do you think about working four days a week? / How do you feel about a four-day working week?* (cũng chấp nhận *What's your view on...?*)

**L4.5.** Trong văn hóa giao tiếp tiếng Anh, nói thẳng *"You are wrong"* dễ bị coi là thô lỗ và làm cuộc thảo luận căng thẳng. Nên thừa nhận ý của người kia trước rồi mới nêu ý khác: *I see your point, but... / I'm not sure I agree... / I see it a bit differently.*

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~150 từ):

> *In my opinion, students should be allowed to use phones in class, but only for learning purposes. The main reason is that phones give students instant access to information. For example, they can look up new words or check facts during a lesson. In addition, many useful learning apps, such as dictionaries and quiz tools, are only available on phones.*
>
> *Admittedly, some people believe that phones distract students, and I agree with this up to a point. It's true that some students might check social media instead of listening. However, I don't think banning phones completely is the answer. As far as I'm concerned, it's better to teach students how to use technology responsibly.*
>
> *So overall, I believe phones can be a valuable learning tool, as long as teachers set clear rules.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đúng cấu trúc 5 bước · ≥ 4 cụm quan điểm khác nhau · có nhượng bộ ý kiến trái chiều · ngữ pháp chính xác · 130–160 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu đoạn hội thoại:
> *A: I think buying a house is always better — you're investing in your future.*
> *B: I agree up to a point, but house prices in big cities are extremely high.*
> *A: That's true. Still, rent is money you never get back.*
> *B: I see what you mean, but renting gives you more flexibility if you need to move for work.*
> *A: You may be right about flexibility. However, I don't think renting is a good long-term plan.*

## Knowledge Check

1. *As far as I'm concerned, It seems to me that, I'm convinced that* (cũng chấp nhận *In my view*).
2. Đặt phủ định vào động từ quan điểm thay vì mệnh đề sau: *I don't think it's true* (thay vì *I think it isn't true*).
3. *Neither do I / Neither am I / Neither can I...* (khớp trợ động từ) hoặc *Me neither*.
4. *agree with* + người/ý kiến; *agree on* + vấn đề chung đi đến thống nhất; *agree to* + đề nghị/động từ.
5. *According to* dùng để dẫn nguồn khác (người khác, tài liệu), không dùng cho ý kiến của chính mình.
