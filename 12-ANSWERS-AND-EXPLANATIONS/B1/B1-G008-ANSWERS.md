# B1-G008 · Answer Key — Third Conditional

> Bài học: [B1-G008](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G008-third-conditional.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. had known** | Vế *if* loại 3: *had + V3*. A là loại 2 (sai thời gian); C dùng *would* trong vế *if*; D là hiện tại hoàn thành. |
| L1.2 | **C. would have helped** | Vế chính loại 3. A là loại 2; B loại 1; D sai vị trí *had*. |
| L1.3 | **B. had, would** | Trước V3 trong vế *if* → *had*; trước *have* trong vế chính → *would*. |
| L1.4 | **B** | *had had... would have helped* → quá khứ không có thật. A là loại 2 (hiện tại); C loại 1. |
| L1.5 | **B** | Không có *would of / would had*. |
| L1.6 | **B. hadn't been** | Vế chính *would have gone* → vế *if* cần QKHT. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **had left** ... **wouldn't have missed** |
| L2.2 | **would have won** ... **hadn't been** |
| L2.3 | **had known** ... **would have visited** |
| L2.4 | **would you have done** ... **hadn't given** |
| L2.5 | **could have bought** ... **had saved** |
| L2.6 | **If I had brought my umbrella, I wouldn't have got wet.** |
| L2.7 | **If he had checked his email, he would have seen the message.** |
| L2.8 | **If they had booked a table, they could have eaten at that restaurant.** |
| L2.9 | **If I hadn't forgotten her birthday, she wouldn't have been upset.** |
| L2.10 | **If we hadn't taken a map, we would have got (might have got) lost.** |

> Quy tắc kiểm tra: mỗi câu L2.6–L2.10 đảo khẳng định ↔ phủ định so với thực tế. *got* (Anh) / *gotten* (Mỹ) đều đúng.

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa | Lý do |
|---|---|---|
| L3.1 | If I **had seen** you, I would have said hello. | Vế *if* không có *would*. |
| L3.2 | ...she **would have passed** the exam last year. | Quá khứ → *would have + V3*. |
| L3.3 | I would **have** come if you had invited me. | Không viết *would of*. |
| L3.4 | If they had **gone** to the party, ... | V3. |
| L3.5 | If I **hadn't lost** my keys yesterday, ... | Quá khứ → *had + V3*. |
| L3.6 | If we had **taken** a taxi, ... | V3. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án mẫu |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | If I**'d known** you were coming, I**'d have cooked** dinner. |
| L4.2 | If he **hadn't driven** so fast, the accident **wouldn't have happened**. |
| L4.3 | If we **had booked** the tickets earlier, we **could have saved** a lot of money. |
| L4.4 | What **would** you **have done** if you **had missed** that flight? |

**L4.5.** *If I had money, I'd buy it* (loại 2): bây giờ tôi không có tiền, nên giả định ở **hiện tại**; vẫn có thể thay đổi trong tương lai. *If I'd had money, I'd have bought it* (loại 3): **lúc đó** (quá khứ) tôi không có tiền nên đã không mua; không thể thay đổi nữa.

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~120 từ):

> *When I was 22, I decided to turn down a well-paid job in a bank and join a small start-up instead. My parents were shocked. The start-up failed after two years, but I learned more there than anywhere else. If I had taken the bank job, I would have had a stable salary, and I might have bought a flat earlier. However, I wouldn't have learned how to manage a team, and I could never have started my own business. If the start-up hadn't failed, I wouldn't have understood how important planning is. Looking back, I don't regret my decision — it made me who I am today.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** kể sự việc bằng quá khứ đơn rõ ràng · ≥ 4 câu loại 3 đúng · có *would/could/might have* · 100–130 từ, mạch lạc.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *I regret that I didn't learn English earlier. If I had started at school, I would have got a better job sooner. Also, if I hadn't stayed up so late before my final exam, I would have got a higher score. On the other hand, I'm lucky: if I hadn't joined that volunteer club, I wouldn't have met my best friend...*

**L5.3.** Mẫu chuỗi: *If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have walked to work. If I hadn't walked to work, I wouldn't have passed the new bakery. If I hadn't passed the bakery, I wouldn't have bought a croissant. If I hadn't bought a croissant, I wouldn't have met the baker. If I hadn't met the baker, I wouldn't have found my new part-time job!*

## Knowledge Check

1. *If + had + V3, would/could/might + have + V3*.
2. Quá khứ; không thể thay đổi — chỉ tưởng tượng.
3. *'d* đầu = *had*; *'d* sau = *would*.
4. *would've* chỉ nghe giống *would of*; dạng đúng là *would have*.
5. Loại 2: *If I knew her number, I'd call her* (bây giờ). Loại 3: *If I'd known her number, I'd have called her* (lúc đó, đã qua).
