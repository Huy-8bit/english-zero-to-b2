# B1-G024 · Answer Key — So / Such ... that

> Bài học: [B1-G024](../../04-B1-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B1-G024-so-such.md)

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **B. such** | *such a long journey* — có *a* + danh từ. |
| L1.2 | **A. so** | Tính từ *excited* + *that*. *too* không đi với *that*. |
| L1.3 | **B. such** | *friends* số nhiều → không *a*. |
| L1.4 | **B. so many** | *people* đếm được số nhiều. |
| L1.5 | **B. old enough** | *enough* sau tính từ. C sai nghĩa (quá già). |
| L1.6 | **B. mục đích** | *so that* đi liền nhau = để. |

## Level 2 — Controlled Practice

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L2.1 | **such** (*weather* không đếm được) |
| L2.2 | **such a** |
| L2.3 | **so** |
| L2.4 | **so** (*so little time*) |
| L2.5 | **such an** (*honest* bắt đầu bằng âm nguyên âm /ɒ/) |
| L2.6 | It was **such an interesting book that I read it in one night**. |
| L2.7 | The box was too **heavy for me to lift**. |
| L2.8 | The water isn't **warm enough for us to swim in**. |
| L2.9 | He isn't **old enough to drive**. |
| L2.10 | It was **such a funny film that we laughed all the time**. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | It was **such a** hot day that ... |
| L3.2 | My grandmother is **so** generous. |
| L3.3 | They were **such kind people**. |
| L3.4 | The soup was too salty to eat. |
| L3.5 | There was **so much** rain that ... |
| L3.6 | She is **tall enough** to reach the top shelf. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **The lecture was so boring that half the class fell asleep.** |
| L4.2 | **It's such a famous restaurant that you have to book a table a week in advance.** |
| L4.3 | **It was too dark for us to see the road.** |
| L4.4 | **The room isn't big enough for 20 people.** |

**L4.5.** Câu 1 (*so hard that*) nói **kết quả**: anh ấy làm việc chăm đến mức đã được thăng chức. Câu 2 (*so that*) nói **mục đích**: anh ấy làm việc chăm **để** được thăng chức (chưa chắc đã được).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~115 từ):

> *Last weekend I stayed at Sunrise Homestay in Sa Pa. The view from my room was **so** amazing **that** I woke up at 5 a.m. just to watch the sunrise. The owners were **such** warm people **that** I felt like part of the family. Dinner was **so** delicious **that** I asked for the recipe. However, the bathroom was **too** small **to** turn around in, and the Wi-Fi wasn't strong **enough** to make video calls. It was **such** a cold night **that** I needed three blankets. Overall, it was **so** peaceful **that** I'd definitely recommend it.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đủ số cấu trúc · đúng *so/such/a* · *too/enough* đúng vị trí · văn phong review tự nhiên · 100–130 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu: *It was such a disaster! First, I was so tired that I slept through my alarm. Then there was so much traffic that I arrived at work an hour late. My boss was so angry that she didn't speak to me all day...*

## Knowledge Check

1. *so* + tính từ/trạng từ (và *many/much/few/little + N*); *such* + cụm danh từ.
2. *such a/an* + danh từ đếm được số ít; *such* + số nhiều hoặc không đếm được.
3. *so many* + đếm được số nhiều; *so much* + không đếm được.
4. Sau tính từ (*old enough*); trước danh từ (*enough money*).
5. *so ... that* — kết quả (tính từ ở giữa); *so that* — mục đích.
