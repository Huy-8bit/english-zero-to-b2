# B1-G022 · Answer Key — Comparison Structures

## Level 1 — Recognition

| Câu | Đáp án | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| L1.1 | **A. less** | *sleep* — lượng không đếm được → *less*. |
| L1.2 | **B. higher and higher** | Tính từ ngắn → -er and -er. |
| L1.3 | **B. much lighter** | *very* và *more* không đi với *lighter*. |
| L1.4 | **A** | *three times as much as*. |
| L1.5 | **C. as** | *the same as*. |
| L1.6 | **B. Fewer and fewer** | *people* đếm được số nhiều. |

## Level 3 — Error Correction

| Câu | Sửa |
|---|---|
| L3.1 | The more you exercise, **the healthier you are/become**. |
| L3.2 | It's getting **colder and colder**. |
| L3.3 | This book is **much/far** more interesting than the film. |
| L3.4 | My brother is twice **as old as** me. |
| L3.5 | Your phone is similar **to** mine. |
| L3.6 | **Fewer and fewer** students walk to school nowadays. |

## Level 4 — Production

| Câu | Đáp án |
|---|---|
| L4.1 | **The more vocabulary you learn, the easier it is to understand what you read.** (hoặc *the easier reading becomes*) |
| L4.2 | **The air in Hanoi is becoming/getting more and more polluted.** |
| L4.3 | **This house is three times as big as my flat.** (*three times bigger than* cũng được chấp nhận rộng rãi) |
| L4.4 | Mẫu: *My phone is slightly heavier than Linh's. However, her camera is far better than mine.* |

**L4.5.** *very* chỉ bổ nghĩa cho tính từ ở dạng nguyên (*very cheap*). Với dạng so sánh hơn, phải dùng *much, far, a lot* (*much cheaper*).

## Level 5 — Real Communication

**L5.1.** Mẫu (~140 từ):

> *Life in my hometown, Quang Ngai, is very different from what it was twenty years ago. People are much richer now, and the roads are far better. Twenty years ago, few families had a motorbike, but today there are probably five times as many motorbikes as before. Houses are getting bigger and bigger, and more and more young people are moving to big cities for work. However, life is not as peaceful as it used to be. The air is slightly more polluted, and traffic is becoming more and more dangerous. In my opinion, the more a town develops, the more it loses its traditional character. Still, I think most people prefer life today because they have better healthcare and education.*

**Rubric (0–2 mỗi tiêu chí):** đủ số cấu trúc yêu cầu · chính xác hình thức · nội dung so sánh rõ hai thời kỳ · mạch lạc · 120–150 từ.

**L5.2.** Mẫu ý: *A dormitory is much cheaper than a private room — often half as expensive. However, a private room is far more comfortable. The more freedom you want, the more you'll have to pay. Dorms aren't as quiet as private rooms, but they are slightly safer...*

## Knowledge Check

1. *The + so sánh hơn (+ S + V), the + so sánh hơn (+ S + V)*.
2. Ngắn: *-er and -er*; dài: *more and more + adj*.
3. Được: *much, far, a lot, a bit, slightly, a little, considerably*; không được: *very*.
4. *twice as + adj + as* (hoặc *twice as much/many + N + as*).
5. *the same as, similar to, different from*.
