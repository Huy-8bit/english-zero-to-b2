### A3. Biến đổi câu với từ khóa (3–6 từ, không đổi từ khóa) (A16–A25)

**A16.** It's possible that she didn't hear the doorbell. **MIGHT**
She ______ the doorbell.

**A17.** I regret telling him my secret. **WISH**
I ______ him my secret.

**A18.** People believe that the painting was stolen by an employee. **BELIEVED**
The painting ______ by an employee.

**A19.** "You should take a break," the doctor said to me. **ADVISED**
The doctor ______ a break.

**A20.** I've never seen such a beautiful sunset. **NEVER**
______ such a beautiful sunset.

**A21.** It wasn't necessary for us to bring food, but we did. **NEEDN'T**
We ______ food.

**A22.** I'd prefer you not to smoke in the car. **RATHER**
I'd ______ in the car.

**A23.** Although she is young, she is very experienced. **AS**
Young ______ very experienced.

**A24.** The noise annoys me more than anything else. **WHAT**
______ is the noise.

**A25.** The film was too boring for us to watch till the end. **SO**
The film was ______ we couldn't watch it till the end.

## C — Reading

### Text 1 — The four-day school week

> In recent years, a number of schools in several countries have experimented with a four-day school week. The idea, which originally emerged in rural areas as a way to cut transport and heating costs, has more recently been promoted as a way to improve teacher well-being and attract staff to schools struggling with recruitment.
>
> Supporters argue that a longer weekend gives pupils time to rest, pursue hobbies and spend time with their families. Teachers, they claim, return to work refreshed and are less likely to leave the profession. In some districts, schools reported that it became noticeably easier to fill vacant teaching posts after the change was introduced.
>
> However, research on the effects of a shorter week on learning remains limited and the results are mixed. Some studies have found small declines in test scores, particularly in mathematics, while others have found little or no difference. Researchers caution that results may depend heavily on how the extra day is used and whether the remaining four days are lengthened to make up for lost teaching time.
>
> Perhaps the most serious concern relates to families. For parents who work five days a week, an extra day without school can mean additional childcare costs. Children from low-income households, who may rely on school meals, could also be disadvantaged. Critics argue that a policy designed partly to benefit teachers should not shift costs onto those least able to afford them.
>
> Some schools have attempted to address these concerns by offering optional activities, such as sports clubs or tutoring, on the fifth day. Whether such measures are sufficient is still debated. What seems clear is that a four-day week is not a simple solution that can be copied from one community to another without careful consideration of local circumstances.

> **Learning a language as an adult**
>
> Many adults believe that it is too late for them to learn a new language. (**C09**) ___ While it is true that children often develop a more native-like accent, adults have several advantages.
>
> First, adults usually have better study skills. (**C10**) ___ This allows them to make faster progress in the early stages, particularly with grammar and vocabulary.
>
> Second, adults often have clearer motivation. (**C11**) ___ Research on motivation consistently suggests that learners with a concrete purpose are more likely to persist.
>
> Of course, adults also face challenges. (**C12**) ___ Many learners find that their progress slows down after the first few months, which can be discouraging.
>
> The key, according to many teachers, is consistency rather than intensity. (**C13**) ___ Short, regular practice helps the brain retain new information more effectively.
>
> Another useful strategy is to make the language part of everyday life. (**C14**) ___ Even listening to a podcast while commuting can add hours of exposure each week.
>
> Finally, adults should be patient with themselves. (**C15**) ___ Every mistake, after all, is evidence that you are trying.
>

## E — Writing

**E1 — Article (8 điểm), 180–200 từ.** Một tạp chí sinh viên mời bài viết với chủ đề *"A skill everyone should learn before they leave school"*. Viết bài báo: tiêu đề hấp dẫn, giới thiệu kỹ năng, giải thích vì sao quan trọng (≥ 2 lý do), cách học kỹ năng đó, kết bài gây ấn tượng.

**E2 — Essay (12 điểm), 250–280 từ.** *"In many countries, people are living longer than ever before. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?"*
