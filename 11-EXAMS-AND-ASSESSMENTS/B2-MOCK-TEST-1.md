# B2 Mock Test 1 — Đề luyện tập tổng hợp cấp B2

Đề **tự biên soạn** để kiểm tra mức sẵn sàng sau 30 bài ngữ pháp B2 và ba [unit kỹ năng B2](../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/B2-SKILLS-PRACTICE.md). **Không phải đề CEFR/VSTEP chính thức, không quy đổi ra điểm thi chuẩn hóa và không cấp chứng nhận.** Không xem [đáp án, transcript và rubric](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/EXAMS/B2-MOCK-TEST-1-ANSWERS.md) trước khi làm.

## Cách tổ chức và điểm

| Phần | Nội dung | Điểm | Thời gian gợi ý |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| A — Use of English | Trắc nghiệm, cấu tạo từ, biến đổi câu | 25 | 30 phút |

**Điều kiện làm bài:** không từ điển, không AI, tính giờ. Người hỗ trợ tạo âm thanh từ transcript trong đáp án; nếu tự tạo TTS và đã đọc transcript, ghi **nghe có xem trước** và không dùng điểm đó làm bằng chứng nghe độc lập.

**Mốc gợi ý nội bộ** (không phải thang điểm chính thức): A ≥ 17/25; B ≥ 7/10; **C, D ≥ 10/15; E ≥ 13/20; F ≥ 10/15**. Xét **từng phần**, không cộng gộp để bỏ qua kỹ năng yếu.

## A — Use of English

### A1. Chọn đáp án đúng (A01–A10)

**A06.** I'd rather you ___ my laptop without asking.  A. won't use  B. didn't use  C. not use  D. don't use

**A07.** The manager insisted that every employee ___ the safety training.  A. complete  B. completes  C. to complete  D. completed

### A2. Cấu tạo từ — viết dạng đúng của từ in hoa (A11–A15)

**A11.** The ___ of the new metro line has reduced traffic in some districts. (INTRODUCE)

**A12.** Many experts are ___ about the long-term effects of the policy. (DOUBT)

**A13.** Lack of sleep can have a serious ___ on students' concentration. (EFFECTIVE → danh từ)

**A14.** The hotel staff were ___ helpful, so we left a positive review. (EXCEPTION)

**A15.** The rapid ___ of the tourism industry has created thousands of jobs. (GROW)

### A3. Biến đổi câu với từ khóa — dùng 3–6 từ, không thay đổi từ khóa (A16–A25)

**A16.** I'm sure he didn't see the message. **CAN'T**
He ______ the message.

**A17.** It's a pity I didn't learn to swim when I was young. **WISH**
I ______ to swim when I was young.

**A18.** People say that the company is planning to close the factory. **SAID**
The company ______ to close the factory.

**A19.** Although it was raining heavily, the match continued. **DESPITE**
The match continued ______ rain.

**A20.** "I'm sorry I didn't call you back," Lan said to me. **APOLOGISED**
Lan ______ me back.

**A21.** As soon as we arrived at the hotel, it started to rain. **SOONER**
No ______ at the hotel than it started to rain.

**A22.** It was such an interesting book that I read it in one day. **SO**
The book ______ that I read it in one day.

**A23.** A mechanic is repairing my car this week. **HAVING**
I ______ this week.

**A24.** My grandmother taught me how to cook. **WHO**
It ______ me how to cook.

**A25.** If you don't book in advance, you won't get a table. **UNLESS**
You won't get a table ______ advance.

## C — Reading

### Text 1 — The rise of the "slow travel" movement

> For decades, the typical holiday followed a familiar pattern: fly to a destination, visit as many famous sights as possible in a few days, take hundreds of photos and fly home exhausted. Recently, however, a growing number of travellers have begun to question this approach. Calling themselves "slow travellers", they prefer to spend longer in fewer places, often travelling by train or bus rather than by plane.
>
> Supporters of slow travel argue that it offers a deeper and more authentic experience. By staying in one town for a week or more, visitors have time to explore local markets, learn a few words of the language and form genuine connections with residents. Rather than simply observing a culture, they participate in it, if only in a small way.
>
> Environmental concerns are another motivation. Air travel is widely regarded as one of the most carbon-intensive ways to travel, and some slow travellers deliberately avoid flying altogether. Even those who do fly tend to take fewer, longer trips, which reduces the total number of flights they take each year.
>
> Not everyone is convinced, though. Critics point out that slow travel is something of a luxury: it requires both time and money, and many workers simply cannot take several weeks off. For families with young children or people with limited holiday allowances, a short break may be the only realistic option. Others note that the growing popularity of long stays has contributed to rising rents in some popular towns, as properties are converted into short-term holiday accommodation.
>
> Nevertheless, the movement appears to be influencing the wider tourism industry. Some hotels now offer discounts for stays of a week or longer, and a number of travel companies have started promoting rail journeys as experiences in themselves rather than merely ways of getting from one place to another. Whether slow travel becomes mainstream or remains a niche choice, it has at least encouraged many people to reconsider what a holiday is for.

### Text 2 — Matching: Four people describe their workplaces

Đọc bốn đoạn. Với mỗi câu C09–C15, chọn người phù hợp (**A, B, C** hoặc **D**). Mỗi người có thể được chọn nhiều lần.

> **A — Linh, software developer:** *I work for a company that allows us to choose where we work. Most weeks I'm in the office twice. Honestly, I'd rather be at home every day — I concentrate much better there — but my manager insists that we meet in person sometimes. I suppose she has a point: ideas do come more easily when we're all in the same room.*
>
> **B — Duc, hospital nurse:** *Working from home isn't an option in my job, obviously. Shifts are long and the work is exhausting, but I've never once regretted becoming a nurse. What keeps me going is the patients. Last year we were short of staff for months, which was really tough, but the hospital has since hired more people.*
>
> **C — Mai, marketing assistant:** *I joined a start-up straight after university. The salary isn't great, but I've learned more in one year than many of my friends have in three. Everyone does a bit of everything. The downside is that there's no clear structure — sometimes I don't know who to ask for help.*
>
> **D — Hung, bank manager:** *I've been at the same bank for eighteen years. People sometimes assume that's boring, but my role has changed completely. Twenty years ago, branches were full of customers; now most transactions happen online, so my job is mainly about advising businesses. I had to retrain, which wasn't easy at my age, but I'm glad I did.*

**C09.** Who would prefer more flexibility than they currently have?
**C10.** Who mentions a temporary problem that has now been solved?
**C11.** Who says their job is very different from what it used to be?
**C12.** Who accepts that someone else's view has some merit?
**C13.** Who feels they have developed faster than their peers?
**C14.** Who had to learn new skills later in their career?
**C15.** Who mentions a lack of organisation at work?

## E — Writing

**E1 — Email (8 điểm), 150–180 từ.** Bạn đã đăng ký một khóa học trực tuyến nhưng gặp vấn đề: video không xem được, giảng viên không trả lời câu hỏi, và tài liệu không giống mô tả. Viết email cho bộ phận chăm sóc khách hàng: giải thích vấn đề, nói bạn đã thử làm gì, và nêu rõ bạn muốn họ giải quyết thế nào. Văn phong trang trọng.

**E2 — Essay (12 điểm), 250–280 từ.** *"Some people believe that the best way to improve public health is to increase the number of sports facilities. Others think this would have little effect and that other measures are needed. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."*

Dành 5 phút cuối kiểm tra hòa hợp chủ – vị, thì, từ nối và dấu câu.

## F — Speaking

Ghi âm hoặc nhờ người hỗ trợ đóng vai giám khảo. Chuẩn bị tối đa 1 phút cho Part 2 và Part 3, chỉ ghi từ khóa.

**F1 — Interview (3 phút).** Trả lời ngắn (3–4 câu mỗi câu):
1. *What do you enjoy most about the place where you live?*
2. *How has the way you learn English changed over the years?*
3. *If you could take a month off work or study, what would you do?*

**F2 — Long turn (2 phút).** *"Describe a decision you made that had a big effect on your life. You should say: what the decision was, when and why you made it, what happened as a result, and whether you would make the same decision again."*

**F3 — Discussion (4–5 phút).** Người hỗ trợ dùng câu hỏi trong đáp án. Nếu tự học, trả lời 3 câu hỏi:
1. *Why do some people find it difficult to make important decisions?*
2. *Should young people follow their parents' advice when choosing a career?*
3. *Do you think people today have more choices than previous generations? Is that always a good thing?*
