# Vocabulary Assessment — Đánh giá vốn từ vựng A1–B2

Bài đánh giá **60 câu** chia 4 phần, giúp bạn biết: (1) vốn từ theo **chủ đề** ở cấp nào, (2) mức độ dùng **collocation, cụm động từ, họ từ**, và (3) những **từ dễ nhầm** cần ôn. [Đáp án và bản đồ ôn tập](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/EXAMS/VOCABULARY-ASSESSMENT-ANSWERS.md) nằm riêng.

> Bài tự biên soạn để định hướng ôn tập, **không** đo chính xác số lượng từ bạn biết và không quy đổi ra CEFR.

## Cách làm

- Thời gian: 35 phút. Không dùng từ điển, AI.
- Nếu không biết, để trống (đừng đoán) — để kết quả phản ánh đúng.
- Sau khi chấm, ghi các từ sai vào [Vocabulary Tracker](../13-LEARNING-TRACKER/VOCABULARY-TRACKER.md).

## Part 1 — Topic vocabulary (Q1–Q20)

Chọn từ phù hợp nhất.

**Q1.** My mother's sister is my ___.  A. niece  B. uncle  C. aunt  D. cousin

**Q2.** I usually have ___ at about 7 a.m. before work.  A. lunch  B. breakfast  C. dinner  D. supper

**Q3.** Could I have the ___, please? I'd like to pay.  A. price  B. receipt  C. bill  D. menu

**Q4.** The train was ___, so we waited two hours.  A. missed  B. booked  C. delayed  D. cancelled

**Q5.** She works as a ___ — she looks after patients in hospital.  A. nurse  B. surgeon  C. receptionist  D. pharmacist

**Q6.** I'd like to ___ a room for two nights.  A. reserve  B. remain  C. return  D. remove

**Q7.** He has a bad ___; he can't stop coughing.  A. rash  B. fever  C. headache  D. cough

**Q8.** Students who get excellent results may receive a ___ to study abroad.  A. pension  B. scholarship  C. loan  D. salary

**Q9.** Many people use public ___ to get to work.  A. transport  B. trip  C. traffic  D. travel

**Q10.** The company made a large ___ last year — it earned much more than it spent.  A. loss  B. budget  C. profit  D. debt

**Q11.** Recycling helps to reduce ___ in our cities.  A. wage  B. weather  C. waste  D. wealth

**Q12.** Please ___ your seatbelt.  A. tie  B. fasten  C. close  D. lock

**Q13.** The government plans to cut ___ on fuel to reduce prices.  A. fees  B. fares  C. fines  D. taxes

**Q14.** I need to ___ some money from the ATM.  A. deposit  B. lend  C. withdraw  D. borrow

**Q15.** The museum has a new ___ of ancient pottery.  A. performance  B. audience  C. exhibition  D. ceremony

**Q16.** The ___ in this region is hot and humid all year.  A. climate  B. weather  C. season  D. temperature

**Q17.** She was ___ for the job because she had the right experience.  A. hired  B. retired  C. resigned  D. fired

**Q18.** The new law will come into ___ next year.  A. strength  B. force  C. energy  D. power

**Q19.** Scientists are carrying out ___ into new treatments for cancer.  A. a research  B. researches  C. research  D. researching

**Q20.** Social media can spread false ___ very quickly.  A. informations  B. informative  C. information  D. inform

## Part 2 — Collocations and phrasal verbs (Q21–Q35)

Chọn từ phù hợp nhất.

**Q21.** Don't worry if you ___ a mistake.  A. do  B. take  C. make  D. have

**Q22.** Can you ___ me a favour?  A. take  B. make  C. do  D. give

**Q23.** We had to ___ the meeting because the manager was ill.  A. put out  B. put on  C. put off  D. put up

**Q24.** She ___ her grandmother — they have the same eyes.  A. looks after  B. takes off  C. looks up  D. takes after

**Q25.** We've ___ milk. Can you buy some?  A. come across  B. run into  C. got over  D. run out of

**Q26.** There was ___ traffic on the highway this morning.  A. high  B. heavy  C. big  D. strong

**Q27.** It is ___ likely that the price will rise.  A. deeply  B. highly  C. heavily  D. strongly

**Q28.** Please ___ attention to the safety instructions.  A. make  B. take  C. pay  D. give

**Q29.** He ___ the job offer because the salary was too low.  A. turned down  B. turned on  C. turned up  D. turned off

**Q30.** I can't ___ with this noise any longer.  A. get on  B. catch up  C. keep up  D. put up

**Q31.** The meeting will ___ place on Monday.  A. have  B. do  C. make  D. take

**Q32.** I'm trying to ___ down on sugar.  A. break  B. slow  C. cut  D. turn

**Q33.** It took him months to ___ over the flu.  A. take  B. get  C. come  D. go

**Q34.** We need to ___ action immediately.  A. take  B. do  C. make  D. give

**Q35.** She ___ up with a brilliant idea.  A. came  B. went  C. got  D. made

## Part 3 — Word families (Q36–Q45)

Viết dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc.

**Q36.** The ___ of the new airport will take three years. (CONSTRUCT)

**Q37.** He was very ___ when he lost the match. (DISAPPOINT)

**Q38.** It's ___ to drive in heavy rain. (DANGER)

**Q39.** Thank you for your ___. (HELP — danh từ, thường dùng *helpfulness* hoặc dạng gốc)

**Q40.** There has been a big ___ in the number of tourists. (GROW)

**Q41.** She answered all the questions ___. (CORRECT)

**Q42.** The film was so ___ that we left early. (BORE)

**Q43.** Good ___ skills are important in any job. (COMMUNICATE)

**Q44.** The results were ___ — nobody expected them. (EXPECT → phủ định)

**Q45.** We need to find a ___ to this problem. (SOLVE)

## Part 4 — Commonly confused words (Q46–Q60)

Chọn từ đúng.

**Q46.** I didn't ___ my homework yesterday.  A. do  B. make

**Q47.** Can you ___ me how to get to the station?  A. tell  B. say

**Q48.** I ___ my umbrella at home.  A. forgot  B. left

**Q49.** Could you ___ me your pen?  A. lend  B. borrow

**Q50.** The ___ of the new law is still unclear.  A. affect  B. effect

**Q51.** He's very ___ — he always gets upset easily.  A. sensitive  B. sensible

**Q52.** We need to ___ prices to cover the costs.  A. rise  B. raise

**Q53.** I'm looking forward to ___ you.  A. see  B. seeing

**Q54.** She ___ in Hanoi for ten years. (vẫn đang sống)  A. lived  B. has lived

**Q55.** The ___ of the story is "never give up".  A. moral  B. morale

**Q56.** I've ___ been to Japan. Have you?  A. never  B. ever

**Q57.** There are ___ people here than yesterday.  A. fewer  B. less

**Q58.** Please be ___ — the baby is sleeping.  A. quite  B. quiet

**Q59.** ___ going to the party tonight?  A. Your  B. You're

**Q60.** I bought a new ___ for the kitchen.  A. cooker  B. cook
