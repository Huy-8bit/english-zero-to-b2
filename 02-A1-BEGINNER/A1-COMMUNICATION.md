## 1. Meeting people — Làm quen

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *be*, present simple, question words.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Chào | *Hi! / Hello. / Good morning.* |
| Giới thiệu | *I'm Lan. / My name's Lan. / This is my friend, Tom.* |
| Đáp lại | *Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.* |
| Hỏi quê | *Where are you from? — I'm from Hue.* |
| Hỏi nghề | *What do you do? — I'm a nurse. / I work in a bank.* |
| Hỏi nơi sống | *Where do you live? — In District 7.* |
| Chào tạm biệt | *See you later! / Have a nice day! / It was nice talking to you.* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Anna:** Hi! I'm Anna. Are you new here?
> **Bao:** Yes, I am. My name's Bao. Nice to meet you.
> **Anna:** Nice to meet you, too. Where are you from, Bao?
> **Bao:** I'm from Quang Ngai, but I live in Da Nang now. And you?
> **Anna:** I'm from Australia. What do you do?
> **Bao:** I'm a software engineer. What about you?
> **Anna:** I teach English at a language centre.
> **Bao:** Oh, great! Maybe you can help me with my English!
> **Anna:** Ha ha, sure! It was nice talking to you.

**Nhiệm vụ 1:** Viết và đọc to đoạn hội thoại 8 dòng bạn làm quen với một đồng nghiệp mới người nước ngoài. Hỏi ít nhất 4 câu hỏi khác nhau.

## 2. At a café or restaurant — Ở quán ăn

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *would like*, *can*, *some/any*, *How much*.

| Chức năng | Khách hàng | Nhân viên |
|---|---|---|
| Bắt đầu | *A table for two, please.* | *Are you ready to order?* |
| Gọi món | *I'd like... / Can I have..., please?* | *What would you like?* |
| Hỏi thông tin | *What's this? / Is it spicy? / Do you have any vegetarian dishes?* | *Would you like anything to drink?* |
| Yêu cầu thêm | *Can I have some more water, please?* | *Anything else?* |
| Thanh toán | *Can I have the bill, please? / Can I pay by card?* | *Here you are. / That's 250,000 dong.* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Waiter:** Good evening. A table for how many?
> **Customer:** For two, please.
> **Waiter:** This way, please... Are you ready to order?
> **Customer:** Yes. Do you have any vegetarian dishes?
> **Waiter:** Yes, we have tofu with tomato sauce and fried vegetables.
> **Customer:** Great. I'd like the tofu, please. Is it spicy?
> **Waiter:** No, it isn't. Would you like anything to drink?
> **Customer:** A lemon juice, please. Not too sweet.
> *(later)*
> **Customer:** Excuse me, can I have the bill, please?
> **Waiter:** Sure. That's 180,000 dong.
> **Customer:** Can I pay by card?
> **Waiter:** Of course.

**Nhiệm vụ 2:** Đóng vai khách hàng có một yêu cầu đặc biệt (dị ứng đậu phộng, không ăn cay, muốn đổi món). Viết hội thoại 10–12 dòng.

## 3. Shopping — Mua sắm

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *How much*, *this/that/these/those*, comparatives, *Can I...?*

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Nhân viên mở lời | *Can I help you? — I'm just looking, thanks.* |
| Tìm đồ | *I'm looking for a jacket. / Do you have this in blue / in a bigger size?* |
| Thử | *Can I try it on? — Sure, the fitting room is over there.* |
| Nhận xét | *It's too small / too big. / It's perfect.* |
| Hỏi giá | *How much is this? / How much are these?* |
| Mặc cả (chợ) | *That's a bit expensive. Can you give me a discount?* |
| Quyết định | *I'll take it. / I'll think about it, thanks.* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Assistant:** Hi, can I help you?
> **Customer:** Yes, I'm looking for a white shirt for work.
> **Assistant:** What size are you?
> **Customer:** Medium, I think.
> **Assistant:** How about this one?
> **Customer:** It's nice. Can I try it on?
> *(later)*
> **Customer:** It's a bit too tight. Do you have a bigger size?
> **Assistant:** Yes, here's a large.
> **Customer:** That's better. How much is it?
> **Assistant:** It's 450,000 dong.
> **Customer:** OK, I'll take it.

**Nhiệm vụ 3:** Viết hội thoại mua giày ở chợ: hỏi màu khác, hỏi cỡ, mặc cả, quyết định mua.

## 4. Asking for and giving directions — Hỏi và chỉ đường

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** imperatives, prepositions of place, *there is/are*.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Hỏi đường | *Excuse me, where is the post office? / How do I get to the station? / Is there a pharmacy near here?* |
| Chỉ hướng | *Go straight on. / Turn left/right (at the traffic lights). / Take the second street on the left.* |
| Vị trí | *It's on your left/right. / It's next to / opposite / between... / It's on the corner.* |
| Khoảng cách | *It's about five minutes on foot. / It's not far. / It's quite far — take a taxi.* |
| Xác nhận | *Sorry, can you repeat that? / So, I turn left at the bank?* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Tourist:** Excuse me, is there a pharmacy near here?
> **Local:** Yes, there's one on Nguyen Hue Street.
> **Tourist:** How do I get there?
> **Local:** Go straight on to the traffic lights. Turn right. Walk for about two minutes. The pharmacy is on your left, next to a bakery.
> **Tourist:** Sorry, can you repeat that, please?
> **Local:** Sure. Straight on, right at the traffic lights, and it's on the left, next to the bakery.
> **Tourist:** Thank you so much!
> **Local:** You're welcome.

**Nhiệm vụ 4:** Vẽ một sơ đồ đơn giản khu phố nhà bạn. Viết chỉ dẫn từ nhà bạn đến 3 địa điểm (chợ, trường học, quán cà phê).

## 5. At a hotel — Ở khách sạn

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *I'd like*, *have got*, *Can I/you...?*, prepositions of time.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Đặt phòng | *I'd like to book a double room for two nights, from the 5th to the 7th.* |
| Nhận phòng | *I have a reservation. My name's... / What time is check-out?* |
| Hỏi tiện nghi | *Is breakfast included? / What's the Wi-Fi password? / Is there a gym?* |
| Yêu cầu | *Could you call a taxi for me? / Can I have an extra towel, please?* |
| Phàn nàn nhẹ | *The air conditioner isn't working. / The room is very noisy.* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Receptionist:** Good afternoon. How can I help you?
> **Guest:** Hi. I have a reservation. My name's Nguyen Van An.
> **Receptionist:** Let me check... Yes, a single room for three nights. Can I see your passport, please?
> **Guest:** Here you are. Is breakfast included?
> **Receptionist:** Yes, it is. Breakfast is from 6:30 to 9:30 on the first floor.
> **Guest:** Great. And what time is check-out?
> **Receptionist:** At 12 noon. Here's your key card. Your room is 405, on the fourth floor.
> **Guest:** Thank you. Oh, what's the Wi-Fi password?
> **Receptionist:** It's on the back of your key card.

**Nhiệm vụ 5:** Bạn gọi xuống lễ tân vì phòng có vấn đề (không có nước nóng, điều hòa hỏng). Viết hội thoại 8 dòng, lịch sự.

## 6. On the phone — Gọi điện thoại

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** present continuous, *will*, *can*.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Mở đầu | *Hello, this is Lan. / Hi, it's Lan here. / Can I speak to Mr Long, please?* |
| Người nhận | *Speaking. (Tôi đây.) / Just a moment, please. / Who's calling, please?* |
| Không có mặt | *I'm sorry, he's not here at the moment. Can I take a message?* |
| Để lại lời nhắn | *Can you tell him to call me back? / My number is...* |
| Sự cố | *Sorry, I can't hear you very well. / Can you speak more slowly?* |
| Kết thúc | *Thanks for calling. / Talk to you soon. / Bye!* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Receptionist:** Good morning, ABC Company.
> **Caller:** Hello, this is Mai from XYZ Travel. Can I speak to Mr Long, please?
> **Receptionist:** I'm sorry, he's in a meeting at the moment. Can I take a message?
> **Caller:** Yes, please. Can you tell him to call me back this afternoon?
> **Receptionist:** Of course. What's your number?
> **Caller:** It's 0912 345 678.
> **Receptionist:** 0912 345 678. OK, I'll give him the message.
> **Caller:** Thank you very much. Bye.

**Nhiệm vụ 6:** Gọi điện cho một phòng khám để hỏi giờ mở cửa và đặt lịch hẹn. Viết hội thoại 8–10 dòng.

## 7. Making plans and invitations — Lên kế hoạch, mời

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *be going to*, *would like*, *Let's / Shall we*, prepositions of time.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Hỏi kế hoạch | *What are you doing this weekend? / Are you free on Saturday?* |
| Mời | *Would you like to come to my party? / Do you want to see a film?* |
| Gợi ý | *Let's go to the beach. / Shall we meet at 7? / How about Sunday?* |
| Nhận lời | *I'd love to! / That sounds great! / Good idea!* |
| Từ chối | *I'd love to, but I'm busy. / Sorry, I can't. I'm going to visit my parents.* |
| Chốt lịch | *See you on Saturday at 7, then! / Where shall we meet?* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Ha:** Are you free this Saturday?
> **Tom:** I'm going to play football in the morning, but I'm free in the afternoon. Why?
> **Ha:** Would you like to go to the new art exhibition?
> **Tom:** I'd love to! What time?
> **Ha:** How about 3 o'clock?
> **Tom:** Perfect. Shall we meet at the café opposite the museum?
> **Ha:** Good idea. See you there at 3, then!

**Nhiệm vụ 7:** Viết 3 tin nhắn: (a) mời một người bạn đi ăn; (b) từ chối lời mời đi karaoke một cách lịch sự; (c) gợi ý đổi giờ hẹn.

## 8. At the doctor's / pharmacy — Ở phòng khám, nhà thuốc

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** *have/have got*, imperatives, *How often*, past simple.

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Bác sĩ hỏi | *What's the matter? / What's wrong? / How long have you had it?* (*have had* — sẽ học ở A2; hiểu nghĩa: "bị bao lâu rồi") |
| Mô tả triệu chứng | *I have a headache / a sore throat / a fever / a cough / a stomach ache.* |
| | *I feel sick / dizzy / tired. / My back hurts.* |
| Thời gian | *Since yesterday. / For three days.* |
| Lời khuyên | *Take this medicine three times a day. / Drink lots of water. / Don't go to work tomorrow.* |
| Ở nhà thuốc | *Do you have anything for a cold? / How often do I take it? — Twice a day, after meals.* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Pharmacist:** Hello. How can I help you?
> **Customer:** I have a sore throat and a bad cough. Do you have anything for that?
> **Pharmacist:** How long have you had it?
> **Customer:** For two days.
> **Pharmacist:** Do you have a fever?
> **Customer:** No, I don't.
> **Pharmacist:** OK. Take these tablets twice a day, after meals. And drink a lot of warm water.
> **Customer:** Thank you. How much are they?
> **Pharmacist:** 65,000 dong.

**Nhiệm vụ 8:** Bạn bị đau đầu và sốt. Viết hội thoại 8 dòng với bác sĩ: mô tả triệu chứng, trả lời câu hỏi, nhận lời khuyên.

## 9. Small talk — Trò chuyện xã giao

**Ngữ pháp liên quan:** past simple, *How was...?*, present continuous.

| Chủ đề | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Thời tiết | *Lovely day, isn't it? / It's so hot today! / I think it's going to rain.* |
| Cuối tuần | *How was your weekend? — It was great, thanks. I went to... / Did you do anything special?* |
| Công việc | *How's work? — Busy, as usual!* |
| Đồ ăn | *Have you tried the new café?* (hiểu nghĩa: "Bạn đã thử... chưa?") |
| Kéo dài cuộc trò chuyện | *Really? / That sounds fun! / What about you? / Oh, nice!* |
| Kết thúc | *Anyway, I need to go. See you later!* |

**Hội thoại mẫu:**

> **Kate:** Morning, Huy! How was your weekend?
> **Huy:** Pretty good, thanks. I went to Vung Tau with my family.
> **Kate:** Oh, nice! How was the weather?
> **Huy:** Sunny and windy. Perfect for the beach. Did you do anything special?
> **Kate:** Not really. I stayed at home and finished a really good book.
> **Huy:** That sounds relaxing. What's it about?
> **Kate:** It's a mystery story. I can lend it to you if you like.
> **Huy:** Thanks! Anyway, I have a meeting at 9. See you at lunch!

**Nhiệm vụ 9:** Chuẩn bị 5 câu hỏi small talk và 5 câu phản hồi (*Really? That sounds...*). Thực hành nói 2 phút với một người bạn hoặc tự ghi âm cả hai vai.

## 🎯 Thử thách tổng hợp A1

Chọn **một**, ghi âm 2–3 phút:

1. **A day in Hanoi:** Bạn là hướng dẫn viên: chào khách, giới thiệu bản thân, chỉ đường đến Hồ Gươm, gợi ý quán ăn, giúp khách gọi món.
2. **Weekend planning:** Gọi điện mời một người bạn đi chơi cuối tuần; người đó bận ngày thứ Bảy; hai người thống nhất ngày khác, giờ, địa điểm.
3. **Hotel problem:** Nhận phòng khách sạn, phát hiện điều hòa hỏng, gọi lễ tân, yêu cầu đổi phòng, hỏi giờ ăn sáng.
