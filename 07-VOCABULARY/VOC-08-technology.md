## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *I **open** the computer.* | *I **turn on / switch on** the computer.* | Dịch "mở máy" |
| *I **close** the light.* | *I **turn off** the light.* | |
| *I play **internet**.* | *I **use the internet** / **go online**.* | |
| *Technology **is developing** our life.* | *Technology **is changing / improving** our lives.* | Collocation |
| ***Informations** online are not reliable.* | ***Information** online **is** not always reliable.* | Không đếm được |
| *I'm addicted to **play** games.* | *I'm addicted to **playing** games.* | *to* là giới từ |

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** My phone has run out of ___. Can I borrow your charger?  A. storage  B. battery  C. connection
**2.** Please ___ the computer before you leave.  A. close  B. turn off  C. open
**3.** The video ___ viral and got millions of views.  A. went  B. got  C. became
**4.** His email account was ___, and someone sent spam to all his contacts.  A. hacked  B. installed  C. updated
**5.** Floppy disks are now ___.  A. cutting-edge  B. obsolete  C. addictive

### B. Điền từ: *algorithm, privacy, digital divide, back up, screen time*

**6.** Always ___ your important documents.
**7.** The ___ means some people have no access to the internet.
**8.** Parents should limit young children's ___.
**9.** Many users are worried about their online ___.
**10.** The ___ shows you videos similar to ones you've watched.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** Please open the light.
