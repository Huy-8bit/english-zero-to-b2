## 2. Collocations

| Collocation | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **start / raise** a family | lập gia đình, có con | *They want to **start a family** soon.* |
| **get** married / divorced / engaged | kết hôn / ly hôn / đính hôn | *They **got married** in 2020.* |
| **be married to** sb | kết hôn với ai | *She **is married to** a doctor.* (không ~~with~~) |
| **close / distant** relative | họ hàng gần / xa | *He's a **distant relative**.* |
| **have a lot in common** | có nhiều điểm chung | *We **have a lot in common**.* |
| **keep in touch** (with) | giữ liên lạc | *I **keep in touch with** my cousins online.* |
| **lose touch** (with) | mất liên lạc | *We **lost touch** years ago.* |
| **family gathering / reunion** | buổi họp mặt gia đình | *We have a big **family reunion** every Tet.* |
| **look after / take care of** | chăm sóc | *She **looks after** her elderly parents.* |
| **set a good example** | làm gương tốt | *Parents should **set a good example**.* |
| **make up** (with sb) | làm lành | *They argued but **made up** the next day.* |
| **spend quality time** | dành thời gian chất lượng | *I try to **spend quality time** with my kids.* |
| **strict / lenient** parents | bố mẹ nghiêm khắc / dễ dãi | *My parents were quite **strict**.* |
| **a sense of belonging** | cảm giác thuộc về | *Family gives me **a sense of belonging**.* |
| **break up** (with sb) | chia tay | *She **broke up with** her boyfriend.* |

## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *She married **with** him.* | *She married him. / She **is married to** him.* | Không *with* |
| *My **cousin brother**.* | *My **cousin**.* | *cousin* đã đủ, không phân biệt giới |
| *I have two **brother and sister**.* | *I have **a brother and a sister** / two **siblings**.* | Số nhiều, mạo từ |
| *My **grandmother side** of the family* | *My **mother's side** of the family* | Cụm cố định |
| *He looks like **with** his father.* | *He looks like his father.* | Không *with* |
| *We are **close family**.* | *We are **a close family** / **close-knit**.* | Mạo từ |

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** My sister's son is my ___.  A. niece  B. nephew  C. cousin
**2.** She ___ her father — they have the same smile.  A. takes after  B. looks after  C. brings up
**3.** He's married ___ a teacher.  A. with  B. to  C. for
**4.** We ___ touch after I moved abroad.  A. lost  B. missed  C. broke
**5.** Many young couples now live in a ___ family rather than with grandparents.  A. extended  B. nuclear  C. close

### B. Điền từ (dạng đúng): *upbringing, supportive, drift apart, generation gap, bring up, fall out*

**6.** My parents were very ___ when I decided to change careers.
**7.** It's hard for single parents to ___ children on their own.
**8.** There's a ___ between teenagers and their grandparents when it comes to technology.
**9.** Old friends sometimes ___ when they live in different cities.
**10.** She had a happy ___ in a small village.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** She married with a Japanese man.
**12.** He is my cousin brother.
**13.** I get on well to my in-laws.
