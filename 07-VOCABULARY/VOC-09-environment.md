## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *We must protect **environment**.* | *We must protect **the environment**.* | Cần *the* |
| *There are many **rubbishes**.* | *There is a lot of **rubbish**.* | Không đếm được |
| *The weather in Vietnam is tropical.* (nói về lâu dài) | *The **climate** in Vietnam is tropical.* | *climate* = khí hậu |
| *Pollution **makes** many diseases.* | *Pollution **causes** many diseases.* | Collocation |
| *Tigers are **extinct** soon.* | *Tigers **could become extinct**.* | |
| *Save **the** energy.* (nói chung) | *Save energy.* | Ø cho nói chung |
