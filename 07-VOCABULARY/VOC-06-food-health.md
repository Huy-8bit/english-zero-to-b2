## 2. Collocations

| Collocation | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **a balanced / healthy** diet | chế độ ăn cân bằng | |
| **go on** a diet | ăn kiêng | *I'm **going on a diet**.* |
| **put on / lose** weight | tăng / giảm cân | *He's **lost** a lot of **weight**.* |
| **take** medicine | uống thuốc | (không ~~drink medicine~~) |
| **catch** a cold / the flu | bị cảm | *I **caught a cold**.* |
| **have** a temperature / a fever | bị sốt | |
| **see** a doctor | đi khám | *You should **see a doctor**.* |
| **keep / stay** fit | giữ dáng | |
| **do** exercise | tập thể dục | *I **do exercise** three times a week.* |
| **junk / fast** food | đồ ăn vặt / ăn nhanh | |
| **a sweet tooth** | người hảo ngọt | *I have **a sweet tooth**.* |
| **cut down on** sugar | cắt giảm đường | |
| **make an appointment** | đặt lịch hẹn | *I **made an appointment** with the dentist.* |
| **reduce the risk of** | giảm nguy cơ | *Exercise **reduces the risk of** heart disease.* |

## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *I **drink** medicine.* | *I **take** medicine.* | Collocation |
| *I eat **many** fruits and vegetables.* | *I eat **a lot of** fruit and vegetables.* | *fruit* thường không đếm được khi nói chung |
| *This food is very **delicious**.* (nhấn mạnh) | *This food is **absolutely delicious** / **really delicious**.* | *delicious* là tính từ mạnh; *very* ít tự nhiên |
| *I'm **allergic with** shrimp.* | *I'm **allergic to** shrimp.* | Giới từ |
| *I **have sick**.* | *I **am sick / ill**.* | *sick* là tính từ |
| *I **do** a diet.* | *I'm **on** a diet.* | |

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** You should ___ this medicine after meals.  A. drink  B. take  C. eat
**2.** I think I've ___ a cold.  A. caught  B. taken  C. got on
**3.** She's ___ to peanuts.  A. allergic  B. allergy  C. sick
**4.** He doesn't eat meat — he's a ___.  A. vegetable  B. vegetarian  C. diet
**5.** This drug may cause ___ such as headaches.  A. symptoms  B. side effects  C. prescriptions

### B. Điền từ: *recipe, obesity, recover, nutrition, prevention*

**6.** It took me two weeks to ___ from the flu.
**7.** Could you give me the ___ for this cake?
**8.** Childhood ___ is linked to junk food and lack of exercise.
**9.** ___ is better than cure.
**10.** Good ___ is essential for children's development.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** I drink medicine three times a day.
**12.** She is allergic with seafood.
**13.** I have sick today.
