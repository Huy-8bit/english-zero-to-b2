| **Ngữ pháp** | [B2-G026 — Common Collocations](../05-B2-UPPER-INTERMEDIATE/grammar/B2-G026-collocations.md) (giải thích khái niệm, lỗi dịch thẳng) |

> Collocation là các từ **thường đi cùng nhau** theo thói quen của người bản ngữ. Không có quy tắc để suy ra — hãy **học theo cụm** và dùng trong câu của chính bạn. Phát âm: tra từng từ trên từ điển; trong cụm, trọng âm thường rơi vào từ mang nghĩa chính (danh từ/tính từ).

## 1. Động từ + danh từ

### MAKE (tạo ra, quyết định)

*make a decision · make a mistake · make progress · make an effort · make a choice · make a complaint · make a suggestion · make money · make friends · make a difference · make an excuse · make a phone call · make a promise · make sense · make an appointment · make a reservation · make the bed*

### DO (thực hiện công việc, nhiệm vụ)

*do homework · do research · do business · do someone a favour · do your best · do damage · do harm · do exercise · do the shopping · do the washing-up · do a course · do the laundry*

### TAKE

*take a risk · take a break · take a photo · take notes · take action · take responsibility · take part (in) · take advantage (of) · take place · take care (of) · take an exam · take medicine · take time*

### HAVE

*have a rest · have a look · have a chat · have a good time · have an argument · have a problem · have an effect (on) · have an impact (on) · have difficulty (in) · have a go · have a day off*

### PAY / GIVE / KEEP / CATCH / BREAK / RAISE

| Động từ | Collocation |
|---|---|
| pay | *pay attention (to) · pay a visit · pay a compliment · pay the price · pay tribute (to)* |
| give | *give advice · give a speech · give a presentation · give permission · give someone a hand · give an example · give priority (to)* |
| keep | *keep a promise · keep a secret · keep in touch · keep calm · keep an eye on · keep fit* |
| catch | *catch a cold · catch a bus · catch fire · catch someone's attention* |
| break | *break a promise · break the law · break a record · break the news · break the ice* |
| raise | *raise awareness · raise money · raise a question · raise prices · raise a family* |
| reach | *reach an agreement · reach a decision · reach a conclusion · reach a target* |
| meet | *meet a deadline · meet a need · meet requirements · meet expectations* |

## 3. Trạng từ + tính từ / động từ

| Trạng từ | Đi với |
|---|---|
| **highly** | likely, unlikely, recommended, successful, qualified, competitive, effective |
| **deeply** | concerned, moved, disappointed, rooted, regret |
| **bitterly** | disappointed, cold, opposed |
| **fully** | aware, booked, committed, understand, recover |
| **completely / utterly / absolutely** | exhausted, ridiculous, impossible, delicious, amazing |
| **widely** | known, used, accepted, available, believed |
| **strongly** | recommend, believe, disagree, oppose |
| **seriously** | injured, ill, consider |
| **badly** | damaged, hurt, need |
| **firmly / sincerely** | believe / apologise |

> Tính từ cực độ (*exhausted, delicious, amazing, furious*) đi với *absolutely/completely*, không với *very*.

## 4. Danh từ + danh từ / of

## 5. Cụm cố định với giới từ

*on purpose · by accident · in advance · in charge of · out of order · on time / in time · at risk · under pressure · in public · on average · by chance · at first sight · for a change · in the long run*

## 6. Lỗi "dịch thẳng" từ tiếng Việt

| Tiếng Việt | ❌ | ✅ |

| mưa to | big rain | heavy rain |

| học kiến thức | learn knowledge | gain / acquire knowledge |
| nói dối | say a lie | tell a lie |
| uống thuốc | drink medicine | take medicine |

| chụp ảnh | catch a photo | take a photo |

**8.** I was ___ disappointed with the result.  A. bitterly  B. widely  C. fully
**9.** There was ___ traffic on the way home.  A. strong  B. big  C. heavy
**10.** The hotel was fully ___.  A. booked  B. aware  C. committed
