## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *Let's discuss **about** it.* | *Let's discuss it.* | Không *about* |
| *He **said** me the truth.* | *He **told** me the truth.* | *tell* + người |
| *Can you **explain me** this?* | *Can you **explain this to me**?* | *explain sth to sb* |
| *I **speak** with him on the phone.* (đã xong) | *I **spoke to/with** him on the phone.* | Thì |
| *He **talked** a joke.* | *He **told** a joke.* | |
| *I gave him **a feedback**.* | *I gave him **some feedback**.* | Không đếm được |

## 4. Bài tập

> ✍️ Đáp án: [VOC-11-ANSWERS](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/VOCABULARY/VOC-11-ANSWERS.md)

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** Could you ___ this rule to me again?  A. explain  B. say  C. tell
**2.** Let's ___ the problem at the meeting.  A. discuss about  B. discuss  C. talk
**3.** He's never ___ a lie in his life.  A. said  B. told  C. spoken
**4.** Please don't ___ me while I'm speaking.  A. interrupt  B. persuade  C. negotiate
**5.** She tried to be ___ when telling him his work wasn't good enough.  A. blunt  B. tactful  C. fluent

### B. Điền từ: *clarify, feedback, break the ice, convey, misunderstanding*

**6.** The teacher played a game to ___ on the first day.
**7.** Sorry, could you ___ what you mean by "soon"?
**8.** Emojis help people ___ emotions in messages.
**9.** I got some useful ___ on my essay.
**10.** Their argument was caused by a simple ___.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** We discussed about the plan for an hour.
**12.** Can you explain me how this works?
**13.** She said me a secret.

**15.** Nói 1 phút về một lần bạn gặp hiểu lầm trong giao tiếp.
