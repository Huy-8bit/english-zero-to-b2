## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *Can you **borrow** me some money?* | *Can you **lend** me some money?* | *lend* = cho mượn |
| *The price is very **expensive**.* | *The price is very **high**. / It's very expensive.* | *price* + high/low |
| *I spent 2 million **for** this phone.* | *I spent 2 million **on** this phone.* | *spend on* |
| *How much **money** does it cost?* (dư) | *How much does it cost?* | |
| *I can't afford **buying** it.* | *I can't **afford to buy** it. / I can't afford it.* | *afford + to V* |
| *Can I have the **bill**?* (khi mua quần áo) | *Can I have the **receipt**?* | *bill* trước khi trả (nhà hàng); *receipt* sau khi trả |
