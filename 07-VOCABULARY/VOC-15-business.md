## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *The company **got** a profit.* | *The company **made** a profit.* | |
| *We **made** a meeting.* | *We **had / held** a meeting.* | |
| *He **opened** a business.* | *He **set up / started** a business.* | (*open a shop* thì được) |
| *The revenue **were** high.* | *Revenue **was** high.* | Không đếm được |
| *We discussed **about** the contract.* | *We discussed the contract.* | |
| *She works **at** the marketing.* | *She works **in** marketing.* | *in* + lĩnh vực |

## 4. Bài tập

> ✍️ Đáp án: [VOC-15-ANSWERS](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/VOCABULARY/VOC-15-ANSWERS.md)

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** The company ___ a profit of $2 million last year.  A. got  B. made  C. did
**2.** Two banks announced a ___ to form one larger bank.  A. merger  B. supplier  C. launch
**3.** We ___ a meeting every Monday morning.  A. make  B. hold  C. do
**4.** Sales were so bad that the company went ___.  A. bankrupt  B. revenue  C. target
**5.** She ___ her own online business at 25.  A. set up  B. opened up  C. took up

### B. Điền từ: *strategy, launch, competitors, contract, e-commerce*

**6.** The company will ___ its new phone in March.
**7.** ___ has grown rapidly in Vietnam.
**8.** We need a clear marketing ___.
**9.** Read the ___ carefully before signing it.
**10.** Our main ___ have lowered their prices.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** Our company got a big loss this year.
**12.** We made a meeting with the clients.
**13.** He works at the finance.
