## 2. Collocations

| Collocation | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **take / sit** an exam | thi | *I'm **taking** my exam next week.* |
| **pass / fail** an exam | đỗ / trượt | *He **failed** the driving test twice.* |
| **do / study for** a degree | học lấy bằng | *She's **doing a degree** in law.* |
| **get / receive** a scholarship | nhận học bổng | |
| **meet / miss** a deadline | kịp / lỡ hạn | *We must **meet the deadline**.* |
| **attend** classes / lectures | đi học / dự giảng | *Students must **attend** 80% of **classes**.* |
| **hand in / submit** an assignment | nộp bài | *Please **hand in** your essays.* |
| **make progress** | tiến bộ | *You've **made** great **progress**.* |
| **learn by heart** | học thuộc lòng | *We had to **learn** the poem **by heart**.* |
| **drop out** (of school) | bỏ học | *He **dropped out of** university.* |
| **higher education** | giáo dục bậc cao | *Should **higher education** be free?* |
| **gain / acquire** knowledge | tích lũy kiến thức | (không ~~learn knowledge~~) |
| **a steep learning curve** | quá trình học khó, nhanh | *The new job was **a steep learning curve**.* |
| **broaden** your horizons | mở rộng tầm nhìn | *Studying abroad **broadens your horizons**.* |

## 3. Lỗi thường gặp

| ❌ | ✅ | Giải thích |
|---|---|---|
| *I **learn** at Hanoi University.* | *I **study** at Hanoi University.* | *study at* + trường |
| *I have many **homeworks**.* | *I have a lot of **homework**.* | Không đếm được |
| *I **learned knowledge**.* | *I **gained knowledge**.* | Collocation |
| *She **graduated** Hanoi University.* | *She graduated **from** Hanoi University.* | Anh–Anh cần *from* |
| *I **made** an exam yesterday.* | *I **took** an exam yesterday.* | *take an exam* |
| *I'm **reviewing** for my exam.* (Anh–Anh) | *I'm **revising** for my exam.* | *review* phổ biến hơn trong Anh–Mỹ; cả hai được hiểu |

## 4. Bài tập

> ✍️ Đáp án: [VOC-03-ANSWERS](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/VOCABULARY/VOC-03-ANSWERS.md)

### A. Chọn từ đúng

**1.** She got a ___ to study in Australia, so she doesn't pay tuition fees.  A. degree  B. scholarship  C. qualification
**2.** You must ___ your assignment by Friday.  A. hand in  B. drop out  C. take part
**3.** I'm ___ for my final exams next week.  A. revising  B. enrolling  C. graduating
**4.** Learning without understanding is called ___ learning.  A. lifelong  B. rote  C. vocational
**5.** He ___ the exam because he didn't study.  A. failed  B. lost  C. missed

### B. Điền từ: *curriculum, extracurricular, deadline, plagiarism, literacy*

**6.** Copying someone's essay without credit is ___.
**7.** Many schools now include coding in the ___.
**8.** Joining the debate club is a great ___ activity.
**9.** I worked all night to meet the ___.
**10.** Digital ___ means knowing how to use technology safely.

### C. Sửa lỗi

**11.** I learn English at a language centre and I have many homeworks.
**12.** She graduated Da Nang University in 2021.
**13.** At university we learn a lot of knowledge.

### D. Viết / Nói

**14.** Viết đoạn 120 từ: *"Should university education be free?"* dùng ≥ 6 từ B1–B2.
**15.** Nói 1 phút về trường cấp 3 của bạn: môn học, giáo viên, áp lực học tập.
