## Phần A — Ngữ pháp (16 câu)

Chọn đáp án đúng.

**A1.** She ___ to work by bus every day.
A. go  B. going  C. is go  D. goes

**A2.** There aren't ___ eggs left in the fridge.
A. some  B. no  C. any  D. much

**A3.** I ___ a shower when the phone rang, so I couldn't answer it.
A. had  B. have had  C. was having  D. am having

**A4.** This is ___ film I've ever seen.
A. the most boring  B. more boring  C. the boringest  D. most boring

**A5.** We ___ in this house since 2015.
A. live  B. lived  C. are living  D. have lived

**A6.** If it ___ tomorrow, we'll cancel the picnic.
A. rains  B. will rain  C. rained  D. would rain

**A7.** You ___ smoke here. It's a hospital.
A. don't have to  B. mustn't  C. needn't  D. didn't have to

**A8.** By the time we arrived, the film ___.
A. already started  B. has already started  C. had already started  D. was already starting

**A9.** If I ___ you, I'd apologise to her.
A. were  B. am  C. would be  D. have been

**A10.** The bridge ___ in 1990.
A. built  B. is built  C. has built  D. was built

**A11.** She asked me where ___.
A. did I live  B. I lived  C. do I live  D. I do live

**A12.** I'm really looking forward to ___ you.
A. see  B. seen  C. be seeing  D. seeing

**A13.** He ___ have taken the keys — he wasn't even here yesterday.
A. can't  B. mustn't  C. shouldn't  D. needn't

**A14.** ___ the heavy rain, the match went ahead as planned.
A. Although  B. Despite  C. Because of  D. However

**A15.** Not only ___ late, but he also forgot the tickets.
A. he arrived  B. arrived he  C. did he arrive  D. he did arrive

**A16.** I'd rather you ___ in the car. It makes me feel sick.
A. don't smoke  B. didn't smoke  C. won't smoke  D. not smoke

## Phần B — Từ vựng (10 câu)

Điền từ thích hợp từ khung (mỗi từ dùng một lần; có 2 từ thừa).

| afford | commute | advice | deadline | reliable | pollution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **cope** | **apply** | **impact** | **recommend** | **reduce** | **crowded** |

**B1.** I can't ___ a new car at the moment; it's too expensive.
**B2.** My daily ___ takes about an hour by bus.
**B3.** Could you give me some ___ about which course to take?
**B4.** We need to finish the report before Friday's ___.
**B5.** The buses here aren't very ___; they're often late.
**B6.** Air ___ in big cities is a serious health problem.
**B7.** Many people find it hard to ___ with stress at work.
**B8.** I'm going to ___ for a job at the new hotel.
**B9.** Social media has a huge ___ on how young people communicate.
**B10.** Can you ___ a good restaurant near here?

## Phần C — Cấu tạo từ (5 câu)

Dùng dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc.

**C1.** The ___ of the new bridge took three years. (CONSTRUCT)
**C2.** She spoke very ___ about her mistakes. (HONEST)
**C3.** It's ___ to drive without a seatbelt. (SAFE)
**C4.** His ___ to help surprised everyone. (WILLING)
**C5.** We need to find a ___ to this problem. (SOLVE)

## Phần D — Viết lại câu (5 câu)

Viết lại câu sao cho nghĩa không đổi, bắt đầu bằng từ cho sẵn.

**D1.** "I'm tired," she said. → She said that ___.
**D2.** People say that he is very rich. → He is said ___.
**D3.** I didn't study, so I failed the exam. → If I ___.
**D4.** It's a pity I can't speak French. → I wish ___.
**D5.** They will finish the work tomorrow. → The work ___.

## Phần E — Sản xuất ngôn ngữ (25 phút)

**E1 — Viết (15 phút).** Chọn một đề theo cấp độ:

| Cấp | Đề |
|---|---|
| A2 | Write 80–100 words about what you did last month and one plan for next month. |
| B1 | Write an email (120–150 words) to a friend about a problem you had this month and how you solved it. |
| B2 | Write 200–250 words: *Is it better to learn English with a teacher or by yourself?* |

**E2 — Nói (10 phút).** Ghi âm, không đọc giấy:

1. (1 phút) *Describe the most useful thing you learned this month.*
2. (2 phút) *What is the hardest part of learning English for you, and what are you doing about it?*

Chấm E1 bằng [W13](../09-FOUR-SKILLS/Writing/W13-writing-evaluation-rubrics.md), E2 bằng [S08](../09-FOUR-SKILLS/Speaking/S08-speaking-evaluation-rubrics.md).
