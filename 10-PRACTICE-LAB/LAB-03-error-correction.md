# LAB-03 · Error Correction — Tìm và sửa lỗi sai

| Mục | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| **Cấp độ** | A2 → B2 |
| **Cách dùng** | Mỗi câu có **một lỗi** (trừ khi ghi khác). Gạch chân lỗi, viết bản sửa và ghi **tên chủ điểm** của lỗi. |
| **Đáp án** | [LAB-03-ANSWERS](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/PRACTICE-LAB/LAB-03-ANSWERS.md) |

> Kỹ năng tự sửa lỗi là chìa khóa để viết tốt. Các lỗi dưới đây dựa trên [60 lỗi phổ biến của người Việt](../06-GRAMMAR-REFERENCE/COMMON-GRAMMAR-MISTAKES.md).

## Part A — A2 (1–15)

1. My father work in a bank.
2. I am agree with you.
3. She is teacher at a primary school.
4. I have seen him yesterday.
5. There are many informations on this website.
6. He don't like spicy food.
7. I live in Hanoi since 2019.
8. Can you tell me where is the post office?
9. I'm very boring because there's nothing to do.
10. She speaks very well English.
11. We have three luggages.
12. I'm interested to learn Japanese.
13. My mother cooks very good.
14. How many money do you have?
15. I usually go to school by my bike.

## Part B — B1 (16–30)

16. Although it was raining, but we went out.
17. If I would have more time, I would learn the guitar.
18. She said me that she was tired.
19. I'm looking forward to see you.
20. He suggested me to take a break.
21. The film was so interesting that I watched it twice times.
22. Despite it was expensive, we bought it.
23. I wish I can speak French fluently.
24. My car is repairing at the moment.
25. The man which lives next door is a doctor.
26. I don't know where does she live.
27. She made me to wait for an hour.
28. Neither of my brothers don't like football.
29. It was such beautiful day that we went to the beach.
30. I'll call you when I will arrive.

## Part C — B2 (31–40)

31. If I had studied harder, I would have had a better job now.
32. Hadn't I known, I would have helped.
33. The temple is believed to build in the 11th century.

35. It's time we go home.

37. Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful.

40. She denied to take the money.

## Sau khi làm

Đếm lỗi bạn **không phát hiện được** theo nhóm (thì, mạo từ, giới từ, mẫu động từ, câu điều kiện...). Nhóm nào nhiều nhất → ôn bài tương ứng và ghi vào [Error Log](../13-LEARNING-TRACKER/ERROR-LOG.md).
