## Part A — B1 (1–15)

**1.** I started learning English five years ago. **FOR**
I ______ five years.

**2.** "I'm tired," she said. **THAT**
She said ______ tired.

**3.** Someone stole my bike last night. **WAS**
My bike ______ last night.

**4.** It's a pity I can't drive. **WISH**
I ______ drive.

**5.** Although it was cold, we went swimming. **DESPITE**
We went swimming ______ cold.

**6.** I didn't study, so I failed. **HAD**
If I ______, I wouldn't have failed.

**7.** The film was so boring that I fell asleep. **SUCH**
It was ______ that I fell asleep.

**8.** A mechanic repaired my car. **HAD**
I ______ by a mechanic.

**9.** I'm sure she is at home. **MUST**
She ______ at home.

**10.** He's too young to vote. **OLD**
He isn't ______ vote.

**11.** "Don't touch the wire," he told us. **NOT**
He told ______ the wire.

**12.** It's possible that they missed the bus. **MIGHT**
They ______ the bus.

**13.** This is the first time I've eaten durian. **NEVER**
I ______ durian before.

**14.** She suggested going to the cinema. **WE**
She suggested that ______ to the cinema.

**15.** I prefer tea to coffee. **RATHER**
I ______ than coffee.

## Part B — B2 (16–30)

**16.** I didn't know her number, so I didn't call her. **KNOWN**
Had ______, I would have called her.

**17.** People believe the thief escaped through the window. **BELIEVED**
The thief ______ through the window.

**18.** I regret not studying harder at school. **WISH**
I ______ harder at school.

**19.** "I'm sorry I'm late," said Tom. **APOLOGISED**
Tom ______ late.

**20.** As soon as I arrived, the meeting started. **SOONER**
No ______ than the meeting started.

**21.** Please don't use your phone in class. **RATHER**
I'd ______ your phone in class.

**22.** It wasn't necessary to buy tickets, but we did. **NEEDN'T**
We ______ tickets.

**23.** Although he is rich, he lives simply. **AS**
Rich ______, he lives simply.

**24.** My grandmother taught me to cook. **WHO**
It ______ me to cook.

**25.** I've never seen such a beautiful sunset. **NEVER**
______ such a beautiful sunset.

**26.** If you don't hurry, you'll miss the train. **UNLESS**
You'll miss the train ______.

**27.** We should leave now; it's late. **TIME**
It's ______ left.

**28.** "You stole my wallet!" she said to him. **ACCUSED**
She ______ her wallet.

**29.** I didn't realise my mistake until later. **ONLY**
______ realise my mistake.

**30.** It's possible that she didn't receive my email. **HAVE**
She might ______ my email.
