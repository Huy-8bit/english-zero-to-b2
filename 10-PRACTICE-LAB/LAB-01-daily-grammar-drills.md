# LAB-01 · Daily Grammar Drills — Luyện ngữ pháp mỗi ngày

| Mục | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| **Cấp độ** | A1 → B2 |
| **Cách dùng** | Mỗi ngày làm **một bộ 8 câu** (5–7 phút) của cấp bạn đang học. Chấm ngay, ghi lỗi vào [Error Log](../13-LEARNING-TRACKER/ERROR-LOG.md). |
| **Đáp án** | [LAB-01-ANSWERS](../12-ANSWERS-AND-EXPLANATIONS/PRACTICE-LAB/LAB-01-ANSWERS.md) |

> Bài tập ngắn, lặp lại hằng ngày giúp kiến thức chuyển từ "biết" sang "dùng tự động". Mỗi bộ trộn nhiều chủ điểm đã học — nếu sai, mở lại bài gốc ghi trong đáp án.

## Cấp A1

### Bộ A1-1

1. She ___ (live) in Da Nang.
2. ___ you like coffee? — Yes, I do.
3. They ___ (not / watch) TV every evening.
4. Look! The children ___ (play) in the garden.
5. This is ___ book. (của tôi)
6. There ___ two cats under the table.
7. I ___ (go) to the beach last weekend.
8. My sister is ___ than me. (tall)

### Bộ A1-2

1. He ___ (have) a big family.
2. What time ___ the shop open?
3. I can ___ (swim) very well.
4. We ___ (be) at home yesterday evening.
5. How ___ apples do you want?
6. ___ you visit your grandparents last month?
7. I'm ___ (meet) my friend tomorrow. Chọn: *going to meet / go to meet*
8. This is the ___ (good) restaurant in town.

## Cấp A2

### Bộ A2-1

1. I ___ (cook) dinner when the phone rang.
2. Have you ever ___ (be) to Japan?
3. She has worked here ___ 2020.
4. I ___ (play) football a lot when I was a child, but now I don't. (*used to*)
5. You ___ wear a seatbelt — it's the law.
6. If it ___ (rain) tomorrow, we'll stay home.
7. The book ___ (write) in 1990. (bị động)
8. Can you tell me where ___? (the bank is / is the bank)

### Bộ A2-2

1. I've ___ finished my homework. (đã xong rồi)
2. We ___ (visit) Hue last year.
3. She ___ (not / finish) the report yet.
4. I'm not used to ___ (get) up so early.
5. You ___ to bring food — lunch is provided. (không cần)
6. The man ___ lives next door is a doctor.
7. The film was really ___ (bore), so I fell asleep.
8. She's not old ___ to drive.

## Cấp B1

### Bộ B1-1

1. I'm exhausted. I ___ (work) all day.

4. If she had studied, she ___ (pass) the exam.
5. I wish I ___ (can) speak French.

7. My car ___ (repair) at the moment.

### Bộ B1-2

## Cấp B2

### Bộ B2-1

1. If I had taken that job, I ___ (live) in Singapore now.
2. ___ I known, I would have helped. (đảo ngữ)
3. The castle is said ___ (build) in the 15th century.
4. Never ___ (I / see) such a beautiful view.
5. It's high time we ___ (leave).
6. The doctor recommended that he ___ (rest).
7. ___ (finish) the report, she went home. (hoàn tất trước)
8. You ___ have bought milk — we had plenty. (đã mua, không cần)

### Bộ B2-2

1. By 2030, I ___ (work) here for ten years.
2. He denied ___ (steal) the money.
3. The number of tourists ___ (increase) sharply this year.
4. What I need ___ a long holiday.
5. Not only ___ (she / speak) French, but she also speaks Spanish.
6. The man ___ (stand) at the door is my uncle. (rút gọn)
7. I'd rather you ___ (not / smoke) here.
8. Viết lại theo cấu trúc *Adj + as + S + V*: *Although he was tired, he kept working.* → ___
