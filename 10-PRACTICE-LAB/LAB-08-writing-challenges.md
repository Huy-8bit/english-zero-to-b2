## Nhóm A — Câu và đoạn ngắn (A2, 10–15 phút, 60–80 từ)

| # | Đề |
|---|---|
| A1 | Write about your best friend: appearance, personality, and why you like them. |
| A2 | Describe your weekend routine. Use at least three adverbs of frequency. |
| A3 | Write about a place you visited last year. What did you do there? |
| A4 | Write a message to a friend inviting them to your birthday party. Include time, place and what to bring. |
| A5 | Compare your hometown with a big city. Use at least three comparative adjectives. |

## Nhóm B — Email và đoạn văn (B1, 20 phút, 120–150 từ)

| # | Đề |
|---|---|
| B1 | You stayed at a hotel and left your phone in the room. Write an email to the hotel asking for help. |
| B2 | Your English-speaking friend is coming to visit your city for two days. Write an email suggesting what to do. |
| B3 | Write a paragraph: *What are the benefits of learning a second language?* Include a topic sentence, two reasons and an example. |
| B4 | Describe a time when something went wrong on a trip. Use past simple, past continuous and past perfect. |
| B5 | Write an email to your manager asking for a day off next week. Give a reason and suggest how your work will be covered. |
| B6 | Write a short review of a restaurant, café or film. Include what was good, what was disappointing, and whether you recommend it. |

## Nhóm C — Bài luận (B2, 40 phút, 250+ từ)

| # | Đề | Dạng |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | *Some people think students should wear uniforms at school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?* | Opinion |
| C2 | *Some people prefer to work for a large company, while others prefer a small one. Discuss both views and give your opinion.* | Discussion |
| C3 | *More and more people are working from home. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?* | Adv/Disadv |
| C4 | *Traffic congestion is a serious problem in many cities. What are the causes, and what solutions can you suggest?* | Problem–solution |
| C5 | *Young people today spend too much time on their phones. Do you agree?* | Opinion |
| C6 | *Should governments spend more money on public transport than on building new roads?* | Opinion / Discussion |
| C7 | *Tourism brings both benefits and problems to local communities. Discuss.* | Discussion |

## Nhóm D — Viết lại và nâng cấp (B1–B2, 15 phút)

| # | Nhiệm vụ |
|---|---|
| D1 | Viết lại đoạn sau cho mạch lạc hơn, thêm từ nối và gộp câu: *I like my job. My job is interesting. My boss is nice. The salary is low. I want to find a new job. I am not sure.* |
| D2 | Nâng cấp từ vựng (thay ít nhất 5 từ/cụm đơn giản): *Pollution is a very big problem. It makes people sick. The government should do more things to stop it. People should also use cars less.* |

## Checklist tự sửa (trước khi xem gợi ý)

| Kiểm tra | ✓ |
|---|---|
| Trả lời **đúng và đủ** yêu cầu đề | |
| Mỗi đoạn có một ý chính rõ ràng | |
| Có từ nối đa dạng (không lặp *and, but, so*) | |
| Thì động từ nhất quán | |
| Chủ ngữ – động từ hòa hợp (*he goes, people are*) | |
| Mạo từ *a / an / the* | |
| Không có câu chạy dài nối bằng dấu phẩy | |
| Đủ số từ yêu cầu | |
