## Part A — A2 level (A1–A15): Chia động từ trong ngoặc

> Last summer, my family and I **(A1)** ___ (go) to Phu Quoc for a week. It **(A2)** ___ (be) my first time there. While we **(A3)** ___ (swim) on the first day, it suddenly started to rain. My little brother **(A4)** ___ (be) a bit scared because the thunder was so loud.
>
> I **(A5)** ___ (live) in Hanoi since 2018, and I **(A6)** ___ (visit) many places in Vietnam, but Phu Quoc is my favourite. Next year, we **(A7)** ___ (go) back — my parents have already booked the hotel.
>
> When I was a child, I **(A8)** ___ (use to / be) afraid of the sea, but now I love it. If the weather **(A9)** ___ (be) good next summer, I **(A10)** ___ (try) scuba diving.
>
> The island **(A11)** ___ (visit) by thousands of tourists every year. Most of them **(A12)** ___ (come) for the beaches. The hotel **(A13)** ___ we stayed was very comfortable. The staff were friendly and **(A14)** ___ (help). I'd recommend it to anyone who enjoys **(A15)** ___ (relax) by the sea.

## Part B — B1 level (B1–B15): Chọn đáp án đúng

**B1.** By the time we got to the station, the train ___. A. left B. had left C. has left
**B2.** If I ___ you, I'd apologise. A. would be B. am C. were
**B3.** She told me she ___ the film the week before. A. has seen B. had seen C. saw
**B4.** The new hospital ___ next year. A. is building B. will be built C. will build
**B5.** My brother, ___ lives in Canada, is a chef. A. that B. who C. which
**B6.** He ___ be tired — he's been working for 12 hours. A. mustn't B. can't C. must
**B7.** I regret ___ him the truth. A. to tell B. tell C. telling
**B8.** ___ it was raining, we played football. A. However B. Despite C. Although
**B9.** You should ___ your eyes tested. A. have B. let C. make
**B10.** The more you read, ___ your vocabulary becomes. A. the more rich B. the richer C. richer
**B11.** I wish I ___ to the party last night. A. went B. would go C. had gone
**B12.** She asked me ___. A. where I lived B. where did I live C. where do I live
**B13.** Both my parents ___ teachers. A. are B. is C. was
**B14.** He's been learning English ___ three years. A. since B. during C. for
**B15.** It was ___ a cold day that we stayed inside. A. so B. too C. such

## Part C — B2 level (C1–C15): Hoàn thành câu (1–4 từ)

**C1.** If I had saved more money, I ___ (afford) a car now.
**C2.** ___ (should) you need help, please call me.
**C3.** The bridge is believed ___ (build) by the French.
**C4.** Not until I got home ___ (realise) I'd lost my phone.
**C5.** I'd rather you ___ (not / tell) anyone.
**C6.** The manager insisted that every report ___ (submit) by Friday.
**C7.** ___ (finish) her work, she went for a walk.
**C8.** You ___ (need / bring) food — there was plenty. (bạn đã mang)
**C9.** By next month, I ___ (live) here for five years.
**C10.** He apologised ___ (be) late.
**C11.** It was my sister ___ found the keys.
**C12.** The number of students ___ (rise) every year.
**C13.** ___ hard he tried, he couldn't open the door.
**C14.** The people ___ (invite) to the party were all old friends.
**C15.** Hardly ___ (we / arrive) when it started to snow.
