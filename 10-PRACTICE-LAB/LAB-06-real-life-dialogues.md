## Dialogue 1 — At a pharmacy (A2)

> **Pharmacist:** Hello, how can I help?
> **Customer:** Hi. I've had a sore throat **(1)** ___ two days. Do you have anything for it?
> **Pharmacist:** Do you have a fever as **(2)** ___?
> **Customer:** No, just a sore throat and a bit of a cough.
> **Pharmacist:** These lozenges should help. Take one every three hours. If you don't feel better in three days, you **(3)** ___ see a doctor.
> **Customer:** OK. How **(4)** ___ are they?
> **Pharmacist:** 45,000 dong.
> **Customer:** Can I pay **(5)** ___ card?
> **Pharmacist:** Of course.

**Thẻ đóng vai:** Bạn bị đau đầu và chóng mặt từ sáng; hỏi dược sĩ thuốc và lời khuyên.

## Dialogue 2 — Checking into a hotel (A2–B1)

> **Receptionist:** Good evening. Welcome to the Riverside Hotel.
> **Guest:** Hi, I **(6)** ___ a reservation under the name Nguyen.
> **Receptionist:** Let me check... Yes, a double room for three nights. Could I see your passport, please?
> **Guest:** Here you are. What time is breakfast **(7)** ___?
> **Receptionist:** From 6:30 to 10 in the restaurant on the ground floor.
> **Guest:** And is there Wi-Fi in the room?
> **Receptionist:** Yes, it's free. The password is on your key card. Is there anything **(8)** ___ I can help you with?
> **Guest:** Actually, could you recommend a good place **(9)** ___ dinner nearby?
> **Receptionist:** Certainly. There's an excellent seafood restaurant just five minutes' **(10)** ___ from here.

**Thẻ đóng vai:** Phòng của bạn có điều hòa không hoạt động; gọi lễ tân phàn nàn và đề nghị đổi phòng.

## Dialogue 3 — A job interview (B1)

> **Interviewer:** So, tell me a little about yourself.
> **Candidate:** Well, I graduated **(11)** ___ the University of Economics two years ago, and since then I **(12)** ___ (work) as a marketing assistant.
> **Interviewer:** Why are you interested **(13)** ___ this position?
> **Candidate:** I'm looking for a role with more responsibility, and I really admire your company's social media campaigns.
> **Interviewer:** What would you say is your greatest strength?
> **Candidate:** I'm very organised. **(14)** ___ example, in my current job I manage the content calendar for five clients.
> **Interviewer:** And your weakness?
> **Candidate:** I sometimes find it hard to say no when people ask for help, but I'm learning to prioritise.
> **Interviewer:** Do you have any questions for us?
> **Candidate:** Yes, could you tell me what a typical day **(15)** ___ like in this role?

**Thẻ đóng vai:** Bạn phỏng vấn vị trí hướng dẫn viên du lịch; chuẩn bị trả lời 3 câu: điểm mạnh, kinh nghiệm, lý do ứng tuyển.

## Dialogue 4 — Making a complaint (B1–B2)

> **Customer:** Hello, I'm calling about an order I placed last week. I'm afraid there's a problem.
> **Agent:** I'm sorry to hear that. Could you give me your order number?
> **Customer:** It's 58219. I ordered a blue jacket, but I **(16)** ___ (send) a green one.
> **Agent:** I do apologise. Would you like us to exchange it or give you a refund?
> **Customer:** I'd **(17)** ___ an exchange, please. But I need it by Friday — I'm going on holiday.
> **Agent:** I understand. We'll send the correct jacket by express delivery, **(18)** ___ you should receive it by Thursday.
> **Customer:** That's great. And what should I do with the green one?
> **Agent:** Just give it to the courier **(19)** ___ he delivers the new one. There's no charge.
> **Customer:** Thank you. I appreciate your help.
> **Agent:** You're welcome. Sorry again for the **(20)** ___.

**Thẻ đóng vai:** Bạn đặt bàn ở nhà hàng cho 8 người nhưng khi đến nơi nhà hàng nói không có tên bạn; giải quyết lịch sự nhưng kiên quyết.

## Dialogue 5 — Discussing plans with a colleague (B2)

> **Linh:** Have you had a chance to look at the proposal?
> **Tom:** I have. **(21)** ___ speaking, I think it's strong, but the budget seems a bit optimistic.
> **Linh:** That's a fair point. What would you suggest?
> **Tom:** Well, if we **(22)** ___ (reduce) the number of events, we could save quite a lot.
> **Linh:** I see what you mean, **(23)** ___ I'm worried that fewer events would mean less publicity.
> **Tom:** True. **(24)** ___ about combining two of the smaller events into one bigger one?
> **Linh:** That could work. Let's run it **(25)** ___ the manager tomorrow.

**Thẻ đóng vai:** Thảo luận với đồng nghiệp về việc chọn địa điểm cho buổi team-building (biển hay núi); đồng ý một phần, phản đối lịch sự, đi đến quyết định.
